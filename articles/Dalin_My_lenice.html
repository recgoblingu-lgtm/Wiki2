Background

Dalin Myślenice is a Polish sports club based in Myślenice with association football and wrestling sections. The football team currently competes in the IV liga Lesser Poland , the fifth tier of Polish league system. The men's senior football team played for many years in the third national tier . The volleyball sections split to firm a separate legal entity, but retained the original name. The women's volleyball team used to play in the top national division after gaining promotion by winning the second division in 2004.
