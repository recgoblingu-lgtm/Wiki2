USS Reid

USS Reid is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Four ships of the United States Navy have been named USS Reid, after Sailing Master Samuel Chester Reid.The first USS Reid (DD-21) was a destroyer in service from 1909 to 1919. The second USS Reid (DD-292) was a destroyer in service from 1919 to 1930. The third USS Reid (DD-369) was a destroyer launched in 1936 and sunk by kamikazes in the Philippines in 1944. The fourth USS Reid (FFG-30) was a guided-missile frigate commissioned 1983 and transferred to Turkey in 1999.
