Background

Luis Vigoreaux Lorenzana Jr. (born July 1, 1951) is a Puerto Rican actor and producer. He is the son of Luis Vigoreaux , a media personality and Rosaura Lorenzana Vilches. He is the brother of Roberto Vigoreaux , former senator, television producer, actor, singer, marketing executive, telecommunications technician and entrepreneur. At the time of his murder, Vigoreaux Sr. had been working with his father on a new TV show named A Millón , for WAPA-TV . Aspiring to become a producer, Vigoreaux was to assist his father with production for the show; however, with his father's death, he became the show's main producer. Under Vigoreaux, A Millón , became the #1 game show for several years, hosted by Hector Marcano , Sonia Noemí , and Rafael Jose . In 1984, Vigoreaux began an entertainment career by co-hosting a daily game show with his brother Roberto called Parejo, Doble y Triple ( Square, Double and Triple ), at WAPA-TV. Vigoreaux's appearance on this show dramatically increased his popularity within Puerto Rico. Vigoreaux went on to host other shows such as Sabado en Grande ( Big Saturday , also with Roberto), El Show del Mediodía ( The Midday Show ) and De Magazin , one of several successful media partnerships with talent agent and former beauty queen Desiree Lowry . Early years

In June 1952 he was baptized Catholic at Iglesia Santa Teresita de Jesús, his godparents being José Ramón Vigoreaux and Gelly de Vigoreaux. He was one of three siblings from this matrimony of whom the youngest, Felipe, died at the age of nine. Due to his parentage, Vigoreaux received media attention from his early years, with his first grade graduation being the subject of a newspaper column. While in school, he participated in his first theatrical plays. In his youth, he participated in various society events at San Juan. In 1967, Vigoreaux participated in the coronation of the queen of New York's Puerto Rican Day Parade, Raquel Solano of Santurce, whom he accompanied. In 1969, Vigoreaux worked as a referee for the Voleyball Superior Femenino. In 1970, Vigoreaux was part of a Banco de Ponce team that won the Baloncesto Interbanco. He returned to the team the following season, which it once again advanced to the finals. In March 1973, Vigoreaux became the owner of the Banco de Ponce's basketball team in the Torneo Interbancario. He returned to the league in the same role the following year. Vigoreaux also served as part of the directors of the Banco de Ponce's annual golf tournament. In September 1978, Vigoreaux became the account executive of the Young & Rubicam public relations firm. Vigoreaux brothers act

In August 1983, Vigoreaux worked with WBMJ in Buenos Días Puerto Rico and Plaza Tours in tourism events. In November 1983, he took over as presenter in the tenth edition of the Festival de la Canción y la Voz de Puerto Rico , a role that his father had performed for years. He also replaced his father in the morning radio show Buenos Días Puerto Rico along Luis Francisco Ojeda and Elsa Fernández Miralles. The arrest of Echevarría in September 1984 caused widespread shock throughout the Puerto Rican artistic community, a sentiment shared by the Vigoreaux children. During the preliminary hearing against Echevarría and the other accused, the prosecution tried to include him as a last-minute witness. In February 1985, the Vigoreaux brothers debuted the evening game show Parejo, doble o triple at WAPA-TV . Producer Elín Ortiz expected the brothers to refresh the programming of the channel, presenting the show in a press conference at the Condado Holiday Inn. The duo, which were described as "the most innovative" aspect of this overhaul, also appeared in other programming such as Charytín . In May 1985, Vigoreaux served as the presenter of Son Borincano , an event dedicated to Puerto Rican music and featuring Chucho Avellanet and Tony Croatto among others. Role as a media producer

In August 1986, Vigoreaux was removed from the cast of Sábado en Grande . The reason later stated by his brother was that his personality was causing a backstage clash. The brothers returned to host the 1986 MDA tele-marathon. Vigoreaux was also contracted to host a series of events for the Puerto Rico Tourism Company. In October 1986, Vigoreaux debuted the morning cooking comedy show Entrando por la Cocina in WAPA-TV, with Silverio Pérez as writer. The following month, he served as guest cook at the Festival del Vino . The brothers reunited in Silverio Pérez's Peña en Down Beat . In January 1987, WAPA-TV cancelled Parejo, doble y triple . Later that month, Vigoreaux held a reunion with the General Manager of WLUZ-TV , George Rivera, about the possibility of joining that station. The brothers created Hermandad Luis Vigoreaux, which worked with the municipality of Ceiba to build a monument for their father. In February 1987, Vigoreaux was selected the Rey Momo of the Carnaval Juan Ponce de León 1987. In March 1987, Vigoreaux was the master of ceremonies of a Los Chicles concert. The following month, he was part of Bicicletada Familiar con Pacheco , a bicycling road race sponsored by WAPA-TV and the municipality of San Juan. He also joined a basketball celebrities team.
