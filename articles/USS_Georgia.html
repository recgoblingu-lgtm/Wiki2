USS Georgia

USS Georgia is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Two ships of the United States Navy have been named USS Georgia in honor of the fourth state.USS Georgia (BB-15), a Virginia-class battleship, provided training and convoy escort services during World War I, and became a transport to bring troops home after the war ended USS Georgia (SSGN-729), the fourth Ohio-class submarine, no longer serves as a ballistic missile submarine (SSBN), but has been converted into a guided missile submarine (SSGN).
