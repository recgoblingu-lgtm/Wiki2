Background

North Madison is a census-designated place (CDP) comprising the unincorporated communities of Redbird and Madison-on-the-Lake in Lake County , Ohio , United States. The population was 8,188 at the 2020 census . The area uses a mailing address of "Madison", with the ZIP Code 44057.

Geography

The North Madison CDP is located in northeastern Lake County along the shore of Lake Erie in the northern part of Madison Township . The area known as Redbird is in the western part of the CDP, and Madison-on-the-Lake is in the eastern part. The CDP is located 1 mile (1.6 km) north of the unincorporated community of North Madison and U.S. Route 20 . It is 4 miles (6 km) north of the village of Madison , 7 miles (11 km) northwest of Geneva , and 9 miles (14 km) northeast of Perry . According to the United States Census Bureau , the CDP has a total area of 3.9 square miles (10.1 km), all of it land.
