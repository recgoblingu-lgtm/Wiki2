The Zod Engine

The Zod Engine is an encyclopedia entry in the Game engines collection.

Z is a real-time strategy video game by The Bitmap Brothers. It was initially published for MS-DOS in 1996 by Renegade Software, then ported to Mac, PlayStation, and Saturn. In Z, two armies of robots battle to conquer different planets.
