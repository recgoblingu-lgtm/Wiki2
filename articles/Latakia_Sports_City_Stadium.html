Latakia Sports City Stadium

Latakia Sports City Stadium is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Latakia Sports City Stadium is a multi-purpose stadium in Latakia, Syria. It is mostly used for football matches. With a capacity of 45,000 spectators, the stadium is the second largest sports venue in Syria. It was opened in 1987 as a part of the Latakia Sports City and became the main venue of the 10th Mediterranean Games in Latakia the same year.

Background

Latakia Sports City Stadium ( Arabic : ملعب المدينة الرياضية باللاذقية ) is a multi-purpose stadium in Latakia , Syria . It is mostly used for football matches. With a capacity of 45,000 spectators, the stadium is the second largest sports venue in Syria. It was opened in 1987 as a part of the Latakia Sports City and became the main venue of the 10th Mediterranean Games in Latakia the same year.
