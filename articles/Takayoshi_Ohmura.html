Background

Takayoshi Ohmura ( Japanese : 大村孝佳 , Hepburn : Ōmura Takayoshi ; born 26 December 1983) is a Japanese musician and songwriter. In 2004, he released the album Nowhere to Go , which included contributions from Richie Kotzen , Mark Boals , and Doogie White . In 2005, Ohmura formed the band Cross Hard and issued the album Eclipse from East . In 2006, along with Cross Hard's bassist, Kaoru, he formed Gloria , releasing the albums MOTIF (2008) and Acrobatic Road (2009). Between 2015 and 2016, he was a member of Uroboros .
