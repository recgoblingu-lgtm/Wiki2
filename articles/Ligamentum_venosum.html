Background

The ligamentum venosum , also known as Arantius' ligament , is the fibrous remnant of the ductus venosus of the fetal circulation . Usually, it is attached to the left branch of the portal vein within the porta hepatis . It may be continuous with the round ligament of liver . It is invested by the peritoneal folds of the lesser omentum within a fissure on the visceral/posterior surface of the liver between the caudate and main parts of the left lobe. It is grouped with the liver in Terminologia Anatomica .
