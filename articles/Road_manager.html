Road manager

Road manager is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

In the music industry, a road manager is a person who works with small to mid-size tours. Job responsibilities include :advancing show dates making travel and hotel arrangements hiring backline techs or recommending techs to be hired coordinating artist media obligations ensuring artist rider requirements are met collecting payments due to the artist at showtime making vendor payments handling personnel issues distributing per diem

Background

In the music industry , a road manager is a person who works with small to mid-size tours (in terms of personnel involved, based on the size of the production).
