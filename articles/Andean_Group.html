Background

Andean Group ( Spanish : Grupo Andino ) is a trade organization in Lima, Peru . In 1969, Bolivia , Chile , Colombia , Ecuador , and Peru established the group by the Treaty of Cartagena . In 1973, Venezuela joined. Chile quit in 1976, as did Peru in 1992. The group created a free trade area called the Andean Pact in 1992.
