Background

Brienno ( Comasco : Brienn [briˈɛn] ) is a comune (municipality) in the Province of Como in the Italian region of Lombardy , located about 50 kilometres (31 mi) north of Milan and about 10 kilometres (6 mi) northeast of Como . Brienno borders the following municipalities: Argegno , Carate Urio , Laglio , Nesso , and Schignano .
