Background

Naowarat Yuktanan ( Thai : เนาวรัตน์ ยุกตะนันท์ ), b. February 7, 1958 (age 64) in Bangkok, Thailand. She is a Thai film actress . She was very popular in the late 1970s and early 1980s. She has a bachelor's degree and a master of public administration from Bangkok-Dhonburi University . She also won a Golden Doll Award in 1976 at the age of 18 for her performance in her first film, Phaendin Khong Raw (lit. Our Land ). To date, she has acted in more than 300 films. As of January 2011 she has been in production for the historical film Phan Tai Norasingh . Yuktanan attended the 52nd Academy Awards in 1980.
