Brandis (surname)

Brandis (surname) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Brandis is a German language surname. Notable people with the surname include:Christian August Brandis (1790–1867), German philologist and historian of philosophy Dietrich Brandis (1824–1907), German forester considered the father of tropical forestry George Brandis, Australian politician Jock Brandis, Canadian author, film technician, and inventor.
