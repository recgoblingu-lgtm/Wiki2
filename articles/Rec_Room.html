Rec Room

Rec Room is a social multiplayer platform where users can create, share, and play games in user-generated rooms across VR, PC, console, and mobile devices.

It is widely known for its strong community-driven ecosystem and in-game creation tools that allow players to build interactive experiences without traditional programming.

Background

Rec Room was developed by Rec Room Inc. and originally launched as a VR social experience before expanding into a full cross-platform game.

Gameplay

Players explore thousands of community-made rooms, ranging from casual hangouts to competitive multiplayer games and roleplay environments.

Games include shooters, puzzles, horror maps, and social hubs built entirely by the community.

Creation System

The Maker Pen tool allows players to build environments, objects, and game logic inside Rec Room.

Advanced creators use circuits and logic systems to build fully interactive multiplayer games.

Social Features

Rec Room includes voice chat, parties, clubs, and public rooms where players can meet and interact socially.

It emphasizes community interaction and collaboration over competitive gameplay.

Popular Activities

  • Paintball
  • Dodgeball
  • Quest adventures
  • Horror maps
  • Roleplay rooms

Economy

Rec Room uses a token-based economy allowing players to purchase cosmetics and support creators.

Creators can monetize their rooms and earn rewards based on engagement and sales.

Community

The Rec Room community includes millions of players worldwide, ranging from casual users to advanced builders.

User-generated content is the core of the platform’s ecosystem, with thousands of new rooms published regularly.
