8 May 1945 Stadium

8 May 1945 Stadium is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

The 8 May 1945 Stadium is a multi-use stadium in Sétif, Algeria. It is currently used mostly for football matches and is the home ground of ES Sétif. The stadium has a capacity of 25,000 spectators.

Background

The 8 May 1945 Stadium ( Arabic : ملعب 8 ماي 1945 , French : Stade du 8 Mai 1945 ) is a multi-use stadium in Sétif , Algeria. It is currently used mostly for football matches and is the home ground of ES Sétif . The stadium has a capacity of 25,000 spectators.
