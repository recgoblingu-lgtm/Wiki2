Olorus

Olorus is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Olorus was the name of a king of Thrace. His daughter Hegesipyle married the Athenian statesman and general Miltiades, who defeated the Persians at the Battle of Marathon in 490 BC.

Background

Olorus ( Greek : Ὄλορος ) was the name of a king of Thrace . His daughter Hegesipyle married the Athenian statesman and general Miltiades , who defeated the Persians at the Battle of Marathon in 490 BC.
