Vitali–Hahn–Saks theorem

Vitali–Hahn–Saks theorem is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

In mathematics, the Vitali–Hahn–Saks theorem, introduced by Vitali, Hahn, and Saks, proves that a pointwise convergent sequence of finite measures is uniformly absolutely continuous with respect to any common dominating finite measure, and its limit is also a finite measure. This holds in the more general setting of finite signed measures or complex measures.

Background
