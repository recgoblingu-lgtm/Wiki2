September 11 (Eastern Orthodox liturgics)

September 11 (Eastern Orthodox liturgics) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

September 11 in Eastern Orthodox liturgical calendars is a feast day and a day of commemoration of saints and martyrs. Although some Orthodox churches use the Revised Julian Calendar and others use the traditional Julian calendar, the fixed commemorations for their respective September 11 are broadly the same, no matter which calendar is used.

Background

September 11 in Eastern Orthodox liturgical calendars is a feast day and a day of commemoration of saints and martyrs. Although some Orthodox churches use the Revised Julian Calendar and others use the traditional Julian calendar , the fixed commemorations for their respective September 11 are broadly the same, no matter which calendar is used.
