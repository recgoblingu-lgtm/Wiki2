Background

Milo May "Max" Marshall (September 18, 1913 – September 16, 1993) was an American professional baseball outfielder . He played three seasons in Major League Baseball , primarily as a right fielder , for the Cincinnati Reds from 1942 to 1944. He was a native of Randolph, Iowa . Marshall is one of many ballplayers who only appeared in the major leagues during World War II . He was a regular for the Reds during most of his 2½ years with the team. In 1942, he finished in the league's top ten for sacrifice hits , and in 1943 did the same for triples and stolen bases . Career totals include 329 games played , 311 hits , 15 home runs , 105 RBI , 140 runs , a .245 batting average , and a slugging percentage of .339. He was an average defensive outfielder for his era, and in his 317 appearances had a fielding percentage of .975. One defensive highlight was 12 assists in 1943. Marshall died in Salem, Oregon , at the age of 79.
