History

During the reign of the Roman Empire in the first century BCE, the province was the location of the Roman fortress of Durostorum , and a major river port on the Danube river. In the 3rd century, it was subject of multiple attacks by the Goths . The Slavs invaded and captured the region in the 6th century. During the late Middle Ages , it was part of the First and Second Bulgarian Empires , until it fell to the Ottomans as a result of the Bulgarian–Ottoman wars of the late 14th century. Tt was later involved in the conflicts between the Ottoman and Russian Empires . As a part of Southern Dobruja , the region was included in the Principality of Bulgaria , following the liberation of the country in 1878. It was occupied by Romania during the Second Balkan War in 1913 and remained in Romania until 1940, when it was returned to Bulgaria.

Geography

Silistra is one of the 28 provinces of Bulgaria. It was formerly known as the Silistra okrug . It shares borders with the provinces of Dobrich , Shumen , Razgard , and Rousse , and an international border with Romania. The province is situated on the eastern end of the Danube river plains. The river is used for navigation, and irrigation purposes. It has a continental climate with hot dry summers and cool winters. The Srebarna Lake in the province is a national reserve and a UNESCO World Heritage Site .

Sub-divisions

The province is divided into seven municipalities– Alfatar , Dulovo , Glavinitsa , Kaynardzha Municipality , Silistra , Sitovo , and Tutrakan . The largest is the municipality of Silistra .
