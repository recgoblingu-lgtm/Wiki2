USS Ability

USS Ability is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Three ships of the United States Navy have borne the name Ability.USS Ability (PYc-28), was a yacht used for anti-submarine warfare during World War II. USS Ability (MSO-519), was the lead ship of the Ability-class minesweepers. USS Ability (AFDL-7), was a small auxiliary floating dry dock.
