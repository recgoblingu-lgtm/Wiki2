USS Parker

USS Parker is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Two ships in the United States Navy have been named USS Parker for Foxhall A. Parker, Jr.The first USS Parker (DD-48) was an Aylwin-class destroyer, commissioned in 1913, served in World War I and was decommissioned in 1922. The second USS Parker (DD-604) was a Benson-class destroyer, commissioned in 1942, served in World War II and was decommissioned in 1947.
