Background

The Nišava District ( Serbian : Нишавски округ , romanized : Nišavski okrug , pronounced [ nîʃaʋskiː ôkruːɡ ] ) is one of administrative districts of Serbia . It lies in the southeastern parts of Serbia. According to the 2022 census, it has a population of 343,950 inhabitants. The administrative center of the district is the city of Niš . After South Bačka District , it is the second largest District in Serbia.

Cities and municipalities

The Nišava District encompasses one city and eight municipalities: The City of Niš is divided into municipalities of: Medijana , Niška Banja , Palilula , Pantelej , and Crveni Krst .
