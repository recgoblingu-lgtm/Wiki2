Steneotarsonemus pallidus

Steneotarsonemus pallidus is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

The cyclamen mite is a tiny mite often found as a pest on African violets and cyclamen plants. It is invisible to the naked eye, measuring only 0.02 cm (0.01 inch) at maturity. It requires a warm, humid environment, and is therefore problematic primarily in greenhouses.

Background

The cyclamen mite ( Phytonemus pallidus ) is a tiny mite often found as a pest on African violets and cyclamen plants. It is invisible to the naked eye, measuring only 0.02 cm (0.01 inch) at maturity. It requires a warm, humid environment, and is therefore problematic primarily in greenhouses .
