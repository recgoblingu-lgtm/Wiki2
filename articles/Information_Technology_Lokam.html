Background

Information Technology Lokam (or IT Lokam ) is a monthly computer magazine in Malayalam . It was started in 2001 by Infofriend Publications , in Kozhikode . The magazine is the first venture of the company. In September 2005, IT Lokam inaugurated a CD edition, like its competitor, Info Kairali . The IT Lokam CD mainly contains free software programs and games , useful articles, demo and shareware programs, wallpapers , drivers , skins , etc.
