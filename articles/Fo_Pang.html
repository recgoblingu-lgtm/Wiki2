Background

Fo Pang ( Chinese : 火棚 ) is an area located in a valley of Kowloon , Hong Kong . It is at the northeast of Danger Flag Hill and south of Ho Man Tin proper. It is about present-day Wylie Road and east of Wah Yan College, Kowloon .

History

At the time of the 1911 census, the population of Fo Pang was 180. The number of males was 126. Before the construction of the college, the No. 1 Kowloon Cemetery for Europeans was located in the area and closed on 15 December 1933.
