Background

Inner Mongolia Medical University ( Chinese : 内蒙古医科大学 ) is a medical university in Inner Mongolia , China under the authority of the Autonomous Region government. It is located in Hohhot , the capital city of Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region. It was established in 1956. It was renamed from Inner Mongolia Medical College in 2012. The main campus is downtown about 400 m west of the central square on the north side. The new campus was opened in 2006: 35 km west of downtown and 8 km west of JinChuan. Teachers, except foreign teachers, take the free university bus from downtown to the new campus. It is a 45-minute ride.
