Background

DreadOut 2 is a 2020 Survival horror game developed by Digital Happiness . It is a sequel to DreadOut (2014). The story follows Linda who lost their one of the students and teacher as well as traps in another world. following the success, of the first game Rachmad Imron and Vadi Vanadi announces the spin-off of the game in September 2018 and released for Windows in February 2020 followed by ports to various video game consoles , PS4 , PS5 , Xbox One , Xbox Series X/S , and Nintendo Switch .

Gameplay

DreadOut 2 is a third-person horror adventure that draws inspiration from Indonesian folklore and urban legend. Play as Linda Meillinda, a high school student with supernatural power – the ability to sense and see ghosts. With the aid of her trusty smartphone, Linda must hunt the nightmarish spirits and dark forces that threaten not only her hometown, but all of mankind. This spine-chilling sequel expands on the cult hit original with all-new melee combat and a greater emphasis on exploration, making DreadOut 2 another compelling and terrifying addition to the horror genre.

Development and release

Producer, Dito Suwardita announces the next sequel at BEKRAF Outlook Conference, Djakarta Theater, Jakarta in October 2018. He stated: "We haven’t gone too far yet; we are still in the vertical slice phase, working toward a demo for publishing". DreadOut 2 was released on Windows on 20 February 2020, via Steam . Digital Happiness announces the release of DreadOut 2 for Xbox One , Xbox Series X/S , PS4 , PS5 it was released in July 2022. In January 2024, it was announced that the game was release for Nintendo Switch .
