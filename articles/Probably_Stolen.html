Background

Probably Stolen , subtitled Cyberpunk Shopkeeper Sim , is a 2026 simulation video game developed by Questing Goose Studio and published by tinyBuild . It was released for Windows on October 28, 2026. Set on a dystopian space station, the game follows a shopkeeper who buys and sells goods while managing the demands of customers and rival factions. Its activities include inspecting merchandise, crafting, and scavenging.

Gameplay

Probably Stolen is a single-player shopkeeping simulation. Players choose a starting specialization, which determines their initial supplies and resources. Options include an apprentice, a hydroponic farmer, and a scenario in which the player begins without goods or money. The player purchases stock from traders, sells goods to customers, and organizes merchandise within a limited inventory. Goods range from food and household supplies to weapons and contraband. The business must generate enough income to meet recurring rent payments, with the eventual objective of purchasing the shop. Players can appraise merchandise and examine items for signs of counterfeiting. Outside the shop, they can scavenge for materials and trade with other businesses. Transactions can affect the player's relationships with factions. For example, players may report wanted customers to security or choose to conceal their presence. These decisions can alter the shopkeeper's reputation and their subsequent interactions with other characters. Faction relationships can bring benefits, such as discounts and donations, or disadvantages, including security checks and fewer customers.

Setting

The game takes place in the Lower Levels of a space station divided by wealth and corruption. The shopkeeper conducts business amid competing political and criminal interests. The setting also features people affected by rogue artificial-intelligence augmentation, whom the player must identify among the shop's visitors.

Release

tinyBuild and Questing Goose Studio announced Probably Stolen on August 3, 2026, alongside the availability of a PC playtest. On September 14, they announced an October 28 release date; a demo was available at the time. During the October Steam Next Fest, PC Gamer reported that the demo had reached 34,230 concurrent players. The full game was released on October 28.
