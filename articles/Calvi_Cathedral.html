Calvi Cathedral

Calvi Cathedral is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Calvi Cathedral is a former Roman Catholic church located in Calvi on the island of Corsica, France. It was the episcopal seat of the Bishop of Sagona, which in 1801 was succeeded by the Bishopric of Ajaccio. The cathedral is a national monument.

Background

Calvi Cathedral ( Pro-cathédrale Saint-Jean-Baptiste de Calvi ) is a former Roman Catholic church located in Calvi on the island of Corsica , France . It was the episcopal seat of the Bishop of Sagona, which in 1801 was succeeded by the Bishopric of Ajaccio . The cathedral is a national monument.
