Đông Hải district

Đông Hải district is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Đông Hải was a rural district (huyện) of Bạc Liêu province in the Mekong Delta region of Vietnam. As of 2003 the district had a population of 130,557. The district covered an area of 550 km2. The district capital lies at Gành Hào.

Background

Đông Hải was a rural district ( huyện ) of Bạc Liêu province (Now Cà Mau province ) in the Mekong Delta region of Vietnam. As of 2003 the district had a population of 130,557. The district covered an area of 550 km. The district capital lies at Gành Hào .
