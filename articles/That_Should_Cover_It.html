Background

That Should Cover It! is a covers album by the Tewkesbury ska punk band Spunge . It also contains three re-recorded songs from earlier in the band's career and two completely new songs. It was released on February 23, 2004, on the band's own label Dent'All Records . The CD-ROM also contains the music video for the song "Centerfold" (made with the help of Goblin Pictures ). The video is made up shots of the band playing live, interspersed with shots of the band doing random things elsewhere.
