Background

Nicarchus or Nicarch ( Greek : Nίκαρχoς ) was one of the generals of the Seleucid king Antiochus III the Great (223–187 BC). He served in Coele-Syria in the war between Antiochus and Ptolemy Philopator (221–203 BC). Together with Theodotus he superintended the siege of Rabbatamana , and with the same general headed the phalanx at the battle of Raphia in 217 BC.
