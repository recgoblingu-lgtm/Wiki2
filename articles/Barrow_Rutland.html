Background

Barrow is a village and civil parish in the county of Rutland in the East Midlands of England. It is located about five miles (8 km) north-east of Oakham . At the 2011 census the population remained less than 100 and is included in the civil parish of Cottesmore . The village's name means 'Burial-mound/hill', probably signifying to the one on the crest of the hill near The Green.
