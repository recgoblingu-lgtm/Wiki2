Background

After Taxes is the second solo studio album by American rapper Sheek Louch . It was released on November 8, 2005, through Ruff Ryders / D-Block / Koch Records . Production was handled by Supa Mario, Vinny Idol, Red Spyda , Mr. Devine, Rockwilder , Buckwild , DJ Cocoa Chanelle , DJ Twinz, Havoc and The Alchemist . It features guest appearances from Jadakiss , Styles P , Beanie Sigel , Carl Thomas , Fabolous , Ghostface Killah , J-Hood and T.I. The album peaked at number 23 on the Billboard 200 , number 3 on the Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums , number 2 on the Top Rap Albums and topped the Independent Albums charts in the United States. Its single "One Name" made it to No. 71 on the Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs and No. 70 on the R&B/Hip-Hop Airplay .
