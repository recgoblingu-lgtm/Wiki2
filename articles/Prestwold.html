Background

Prestwold is a hamlet and civil parish in the Charnwood district of Leicestershire , England. The parish has a population of around 60. The population at the 2011 census remained less than 100 and is included in the civil parish of Hoton . Nearby places are Hoton, just to the north, and Burton on the Wolds , to the south-east. The parish includes Prestwold Hall and parish church St Andrew's . Prestwold has historical connection with the Packe family for generations after family founder Sir Christopher Packe .
