Background

Rafał Dębski (born 1969) is a Polish writer of fantasy, historic, sensational and criminal novels. He works as a psychologist. Since June 2009 he has been editor in chief of the Science Fiction, Fantasy and Horror magazine. He debuted in Nowa Fantastyka in its May 1998 issue with his tale "Siódmy liść". In 2005 his first novel Łzy Nemezis was published. In the scope of fantasy literature he won the " Nautilus " award in 2007 for his novel Czarny Pergamin and in 2008 for Gwiazdozbiór kata . He published a series of criminal novels, in which the main character is police officer Michał Wroński. He also wrote a novel which takes place during the Russo-Chechen conflict.
