Background

Monk's Walk School is a secondary school with academy status located on the outskirts of Welwyn Garden City , Hertfordshire , England . It opened in 1964 and became an Academy in 2012. The school's motto is: "Excellence for All". The school's annual student intake is approximately 220 eleven- and twelve-year-olds of both sexes, with 1,324 students throughout the school. It also has approximately 185 teachers and staff.
