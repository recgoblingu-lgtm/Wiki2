Undulating number

Undulating number is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

In mathematics, an undulating number is a number that has the digit form ABABAB... when in the base 10 number system. It is sometimes restricted to non-trivial undulating numbers, which are required to have at least three digits and A ≠ B.
