Background

Caringbah railway station is a suburban railway station located on the Cronulla line , serving the Sydney suburb of Caringbah . It is served by Sydney Trains T4 Eastern Suburbs & Illawarra Line services.

History

Caringbah station opened on 16 December 1939 when the Cronulla line opened from Sutherland to Cronulla . Caringbah was originally one of two stations on the single track, the other being Gymea . On 15 July 1985, the line from Gymea to Caringbah was duplicated with a new track laid north of the existing single line. In July 2001, an upgrade to the station was complete. In April 2010, the rest of the line was duplicated.
