Background

Peace, Death! is a 2017 pixel art simulation game , developed and published by the Russian studio AZAMATIKA. The player controls Reaper, a novice worker of Death , who must examine the attributes of deceased characters and send them to their correct afterlife. The game is available on Windows , Linux , Android , iOS and Nintendo Switch .

Gameplay

The gameplay consists of selecting who goes to Hell , Heaven or Purgatory , as accurately as possible and within a time limit. In order to do it properly, the player must examine each individual and see what criteria they meet. Initially it is as simple as sending demons down to Hell on the left elevator and humans up to Heaven on the right elevator. However, new rules are added every day and the process of checking them gets increasingly more complex. The player can be asked, for example, to verify if someone is carrying a weapon or if their body is stained with blood, with both cases meaning the character is meant to be sent to Hell. Some crucial elements might be hidden with objects such as hats or glasses, which must be removed in order to make a proper judgement. Each day has a set number of people to verify, after which the player receives rewards depending on their performance. If enough mistakes are made, a day can be failed midway. After a certain point, Purgatory is added as a third destination for those who cannot go to either Heaven or Hell . The criteria include, for example, letting go of a weapon (which would otherwise result in Hell) or renouncing holy text (which would otherwise lead to Heaven) when the player left-clicks the corresponding character. Plot

The protagonist, an unemployed Grim Reaper (also known simply as Reaper), is relaxing on their couch when their stomach growls. There is no food inside the fridge, and the character does not have enough money to order food either. They decide, thus, to apply for a job at Apocalypse Corporation, working primarily for Death . Reaper can also work for other members of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse , who often make requests. Their workplace is described as "beyond space and time". After successfully completing seven weeks of a trial period, Reaper gets paid and is hired for a full-time job.

Development and release

The head of the Russian studio AZAMATIKA, Azamat Baizulaev (also known as Azamat Zage), revealed his original plan was to make a sequel to his Western game Gun Done , with the same hero, but it did not work out. At the same time, he came across an online comment sarcastically asking his next game to not be called Peace, Death! (a Russian swear pun). The developer, instead, was motivated by the comment and began working on the title. The title, born as a pun, virtually defined the early stages of development. Azamat expressed the desire to build something both Russian speakers and English speakers could enjoy. According to him, the central idea of the game remained mostly the same over time. He highlighted, however, how Reaper would originally give orders with a lever instead of his hands, which became visually problematic when Purgatory was added. In general terms, developing the idea of character attributes for the game took two months to be finished, the same amount of time spent writing the character lines. The Hell element was originally inspired by traditional Christianity , due to its commonness. However, the final product ended up much more varied, including the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse . Azamat denied any intentions to offend people with the title, claiming to have deliberately avoided certain topics. He also contradicted the idea of a deeper meaning for the game
