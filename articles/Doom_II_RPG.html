Background

Doom II RPG is a 2009 role-playing video game developed and published by id Software . It is the sequel to Doom RPG . It was released for Java ME on November 23, 2009, and for iOS on February 8, 2010. It uses the Wolfenstein RPG engine and was developed by id partners Fountainhead Entertainment via the merger id Mobile . The iOS version can be played on modern hardware (PC and Android) with touchHLE emulator.
