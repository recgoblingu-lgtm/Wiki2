Background

Lynchburg is an Unincorporated community in southeastern Laclede County , Missouri , United States. It is located at the intersection of Missouri routes 32 and 95 , approximately 30 miles southeast of Lebanon . A post office called Lynchburg was established in 1897, and remained in operation until 1996. The community has the name of David and R. D. Lynch, early settlers.
