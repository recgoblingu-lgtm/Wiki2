Background

Mark Clay Brandenburg (born July 14, 1970) is an American former middle-relief pitcher in Major League Baseball who played from 1995 through 1997 for the Texas Rangers (1995–96) and Boston Red Sox (1996–97). Brandenburg batted and threw right-handed. Listed at 6' 0", 180 lb., he was selected by the Texas Rangers in the 1992 draft out of Texas Tech . In a three-season career, Brandenburg posted a 5–8 record with a 4.49 earned run average , 121 strikeouts , 56 walks , 144 ⅓ innings , and no saves , in 97 games pitched . In July 2008, Brandenburg was inducted into the Texas Tech Athletics Hall of Fame. His daughter, Ryan Kate, appeared on Season 3 of MasterChef Junior and Season 15 of MasterChef .
