Background

Ian Slater is a former professional rugby league footballer who played in the 1980s. He played at club level for Huddersfield , Featherstone Rovers , and Bradford Northern , as a stand-off .

Club career

Slater was signed by Huddersfield from Leeds in September 1979 for a club record fee of £10,000. Slater made his début for Featherstone Rovers on Friday 9 March 1984.

Genealogical information

Ian Slater is the grandson of the rugby league footballer who played in the 1930s, 1940s and 1950s; Frank Hemingway .
