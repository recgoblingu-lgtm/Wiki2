Background

Zomergem ( Dutch pronunciation: [ˈzoːmərɣɛm] ) is a town and former municipality located in the Flanders and in the province of East Flanders , in Belgium . The municipality comprises the towns of Oostwinkel , Ronsele and Zomergem proper. On 1 January 2018, Zomergem had a total population of 8,466. The total area is 38.78 km. Effective 1 January 2019, Waarschoot , Lovendegem and Zomergem were merged into the new municipality of Lievegem .
