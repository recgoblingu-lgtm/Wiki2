Background

Songs of Glimmerwick is an open-world simulation video game developed and published by Eastshade Studios, known by 2019 adventure game Eastshade . It was released on Steam on September 30, 2026. It was largely compared to the world of Harry Potter , with the same theme of a "magic school".

Gameplay

The player takes control of a student in a magic academia, living through four seasons of everyday school life. Players cast magic by using they keyboard to create music notes, forming a unique melody that is transformed into a spell, which can help with tasks like fishing or gardening. By exploring the world and completing tasks, players will gain experience, which will allow unlocking special skills, useful in everyday routine. Players can also brew potions from the ingredients grown by them in gardens.
