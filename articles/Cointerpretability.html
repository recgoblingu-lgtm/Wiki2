Background

In mathematical logic , cointerpretability is a binary relation on formal theories : a formal theory T is cointerpretable in another such theory S when the language of S can be translated into the language of T in such a way that S proves every formula whose translation is a theorem of T . The "translation" here is required to preserve the logical structure of formulas. This concept, in a sense dual to interpretability , was introduced by Japaridze (1993) , who also proved that, for theories of Peano arithmetic and any stronger theories with computable axiomatizations , cointerpretability is equivalent to -conservativity.
