Background

Miner Willy is the protagonist in a series of platform games for the ZX Spectrum , MSX , Amstrad CPC and the Commodore 64 home computers . The first two games - Manic Miner and Jet Set Willy were written by Matthew Smith during the early 1980s. The Willy saga was to be a trilogy and a third game in the series was planned, Miner Willy Meets The Taxman . The series started in 1983 with the release of Manic Miner , and was followed up a year later with Jet Set Willy and Jet Set Willy II . Another game in the series, The Perils of Willy , was released solely for the VIC-20 . Andre's Night Off was published as a type-in listing in the June 1984 issue of Computer & Video Games . In addition, quite a few unofficial sequels, remakes , homages and updates have been released.
