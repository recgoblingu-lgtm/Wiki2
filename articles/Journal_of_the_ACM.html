Background

The Journal of the ACM ( JACM ) is a peer-reviewed scientific journal covering computer science in general, especially theoretical aspects. It is an official journal of the Association for Computing Machinery . Its current editor-in-chief is Venkatesan Guruswami . The journal was established in 1954 and "computer scientists universally hold the Journal of the ACM in high esteem".
