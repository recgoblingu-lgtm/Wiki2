Rhode Island Route 81

Rhode Island Route 81 is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Route 81 is a numbered state highway running 8.0 miles (12.9 km) in Rhode Island. Route 81's southern terminus is at Rhode Island Route 179 in Little Compton and the northern terminus is a continuation as Massachusetts Route 81 near Fall River, Massachusetts.
