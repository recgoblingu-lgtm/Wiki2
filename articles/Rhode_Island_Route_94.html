Rhode Island Route 94

Rhode Island Route 94 is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Route 94 is a numbered state highway running 12.7 miles (20.4 km) in Rhode Island. Route 94's southern terminus is at Route 14 and Route 102 in Foster and the northern terminus is at U.S. Route 44 in Chepachet.
