Background

The Truce of Ulm ( German : Waffenstillstand von Ulm ) (also known as the Treaty of Ulm ) was signed in Ulm on 14 March 1647 between France , Sweden , and Bavaria . This truce was developed after France and Sweden invaded Bavaria during the Thirty Years' War . Both invading nations forced Maximilian I, Elector of Bavaria , to conclude the truce and renounce his alliance with Emperor Ferdinand III . However, Maximilian broke the truce on 14 September and returned to his alliance with Ferdinand.
