Background

Middleburg is an unincorporated community in northern Zane Township , Logan County , Ohio , United States. It has a post office with the ZIP code 43336. It is located at the intersection of State Route 287 with County Road 153, near the headwaters of the Big Darby Creek and a short distance southwest of U.S. Route 33 . Middleburg was platted in 1832. This unincorporated community is located in Ohio .
