Background

Samogitia National Park or Žemaitija National Park is a national park in Lithuania . It is situated on the Samogitian Upland , 45 km from the Baltic Sea . Lakes cover more than 7% of its territory. Lake Plateliai (12.05 km, 47 m depth) is the largest lake in the park. The town of Plateliai , situated on the shore of the lake, is the seat of the park's administration and a popular tourist destination. The Park has Category II in the classification of the IUCN.
