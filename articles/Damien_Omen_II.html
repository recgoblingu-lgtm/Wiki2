Background

Damien – Omen II is a 1978 supernatural horror film directed by Don Taylor . It is the sequel to The Omen , and the second installment of The Omen series . It stars William Holden and Lee Grant , with Jonathan Scott-Taylor , Robert Foxworth , Lew Ayres , Sylvia Sidney , Lance Henriksen , Ian Hendry , and Leo McKern . Set seven years after the first film, it follows a now-pubescent Damien Thorn (Scott-Taylor) as he begins to realize his destiny as the Antichrist . Franchise producer Harvey Bernhard wrote the screen story, with a screenplay by Stanley Mann and Mike Hodges . Hodges was the original director, but was replaced by Don Taylor early in production. Unlike the first film, which was shot in England and Italy, filming of the sequel took place primarily in the United States, in locations around Chicago, Illinois and Wisconsin . The opening scene was shot in Israel . It was released by 20th Century Fox on June 9, 1978, and received mixed reviews from critics. A sequel, The Final Conflict , was released in 1981.

Plot

One week after the events of the first film , archaeologist Carl Bugenhagen learns that Damien Thorn is still alive. In Acre , Israel , Bugenhagen asks his friend Michael Morgan to deliver a box to Damien's new guardian, explaining that Damien is the Antichrist and that the box contains the Seven Daggers of Megiddo needed to kill him. When Morgan expresses skepticism, Bugenhagen takes him to a local ruin to see the mural of Yigael's Wall, which was said to have been drawn by a monk who had visions of the Antichrist as he would appear from birth to adulthood. Morgan believes him upon seeing Damien's face painted on the mural, but the ruins abruptly collapse and both he and Bugenhagen are buried alive. Seven years later, the 12-year-old Damien is living in Chicago with his uncle and step-aunt, Richard and Ann Thorn. Damien gets along well with his cousin Mark, with whom Damien is enrolled in a military academy, but is despised by Richard's aunt Marion, who sees him as a bad influence on Mark and threatens to cut Richard out of her will if he does not separate the two boys. Late one night, Marion suffers a fatal heart attack after being scared by a raven that appears in her room. Mark and Damien leave for the academy, where their commanding officer, Sergeant Neff, takes Damien under his wing, indirectly revealing that he is a Satanist sent to watch over him. Development and direction

Following the commercial success of The Omen (1976), 20th Century Fox began developing a series of sequels. David Seltzer , who wrote the original film, declined to return as screenwriter. In November 1976, Al Ramrus and John Herman Shaner were hired to write the sequel, then known by the working title The Inheritance , but neither remained with the production. The finished film credited Harvey Bernhard with the story and Stanley Mann and Mike Hodges with the screenplay. Richard Donner , who directed the original film, was unavailable for the sequel because of his commitments to Superman (1978). Hodges was consequently hired as director, but departed in November 1977 after approximately four weeks of filming, reportedly because of artistic differences. Don Taylor replaced him, and the change required eight days of reshoots. Hodges retained his screenplay credit on the completed film. In a 2003 interview with The Guardian , Hodges recalled that during an argument about the production design budget, Bernhard removed a loaded handgun from his bag and placed it on a table. Hodges described the incident as frightening. In a 2022 interview with MUBI , Hodges maintained that he had left the production voluntarily and clarified that Bernhard had not directly threatened him with the weapon.

Casting and animal effects

Leo McKern reprised his role as Carl Bugenhagen from The Omen (1976), in which his appearance had been uncredited. Animal trainer Ray Berwick was responsible for training the ravens used in the film. Berwick had previously trained birds for Alfred Hitchcock 's The Birds (1963), for which he worked with approximately 300 birds.
