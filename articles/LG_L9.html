LG L9

LG L9 is an encyclopedia entry in the Streaming services collection.

The LG L9 is a SoC for Smart TV devices made by LG Corp. Specifically, it will be paired with the Google TV platform. It includes a dual-core ARM architecture CPU and a quad-core GPU.

Background

The LG L9 is a SoC for Smart TV devices made by LG Corp . Specifically, it will be paired with the Google TV platform. It includes a dual-core ARM architecture CPU and a quad-core GPU. LG also produces a phone called the LG Optimus L9 , this is not related to this SoC.
