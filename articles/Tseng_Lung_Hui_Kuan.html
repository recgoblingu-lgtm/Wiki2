Tseng Lung Hui Kuan is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

The Tseng Lung Hui-Kuan were Malaysian associations for people from Zengcheng and Longmen counties of Guangzhou prefecture in South China.Tseng Lung Hui Kuan, Penang, founded in 1886 by Capitan China Chung Keng Quee Tseng Lung Hui Kuan, Market Road, Taiping, Perak, founded in 1885 by Capitan China Chung Keng Quee Tseng Lung Hui Kuan, Gopeng, Perak, founded in 1885 by Capitan China Chung Keng Quee

Background

The Tseng Lung ( Zeng Long ) Hui-Kuan were Malaysian associations for people from Zengcheng ( Chinese : 增城 ) and Longmen ( Chinese : 龙门 ) counties of Guangzhou ( Chinese : 广州 ) prefecture in South China .
