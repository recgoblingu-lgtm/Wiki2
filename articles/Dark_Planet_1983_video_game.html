Background

Dark Planet is a 1983 multidirectional shooter video game developed and published by Stern Electronics for arcades . It was released in North America in January 1983. It is notable for its use of an active shutter 3D system similar to Sega 's SubRoc-3D , which was released around the same time. In 2023, Atari SA acquired the rights to twelve Stern Electronics video games including Dark Planet .

Gameplay

The player controls a spaceship which defeats enemies on the surface of a distant planet. There is no background as gameplay is superimposed on a painting of the planet's surface. Enemies can be destroyed with the player's ammunition or limited amount of lasers, giving players varying amounts of points on defeat.
