Background

Walking Concert is an indie rock band from New York City , featuring Walter Schreifels on vocals and guitar , Jeffery E. Johnson on guitar, Ryan Stratton on bass guitar , and Drew Thomas on drums . The band takes its name from the line "Boy, you're gonna be a Walking Concert!", spoken by a music store clerk to Ralph Macchio 's character in the 1986 film, Crossroads .
