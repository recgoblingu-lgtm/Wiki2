This Is Football

This Is Football is an encyclopedia entry in the Game franchises collection.

This Is Football is an association football video game series originally developed by Team Soho and published by Sony Computer Entertainment. Later instalments were developed by London Studio after Team Soho's merge into the studio. Sports Director Limited took over developing the latest game.

Background

This Is Football (known in North America as World Tour Soccer ) is an association football video game series originally developed by Team Soho and published by Sony Computer Entertainment . Later instalments were developed by London Studio after Team Soho's merge into the studio. Sports Director Limited took over developing the latest game.
