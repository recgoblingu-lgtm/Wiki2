Background

Eiwaz or Eihaz is the reconstructed Proto-Germanic name of the rune ᛇ , coming from a word for " yew ". Two variants of the word are reconstructed for Proto-Germanic, * īhaz ( * ē²haz , from Proto-Indo-European * eikos ), continued in Old English as ēoh (also īh ), and * īwaz ( * ē²waz , from Proto-Indo-European * eiwos ), continued in Old English as īw (whence English yew ). The latter is possibly an early loan from the Celtic, compare Gaulish ivos , Breton ivin , Welsh ywen , Old Irish eó . The common spelling of the rune's name, " Eihwaz ", combines the two variants; strictly based on the Old English evidence, a spelling "Eihaz" would be more proper.
