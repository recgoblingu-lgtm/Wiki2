Tom Stafford (astronomer)

Tom Stafford (astronomer) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Tom Stafford is an American astronomer who has discovered a number of asteroids since 1997, including 12061 Alena, 12533 Edmond, 13436 Enid, 13688 Oklahoma and 15904 Halstead at Zeno Observatory in Edmond, Oklahoma. Background

Tom Stafford is an American astronomer who has discovered a number of asteroids since 1997, including 12061 Alena , 12533 Edmond , 13436 Enid , 13688 Oklahoma and 15904 Halstead at Zeno Observatory (observatory code 727) in Edmond, Oklahoma .
