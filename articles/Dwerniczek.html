Background

Dwerniczek [dvɛrˈɲit͡ʂɛk] is a village in the administrative district of Gmina Lutowiska , within Bieszczady County , Subcarpathian Voivodeship , in south-eastern Poland , close to the border with Ukraine . The San river crosses the area.
