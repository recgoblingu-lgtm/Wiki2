Background

Eastwick station is a SEPTA Regional Rail station in Philadelphia . It serves the Airport Line to Philadelphia International Airport . Eastwick has two low-level side platforms with a connecting walkway across the tracks. Located below 84th Street and situated between Mario Lanza Boulevard and Bartram Avenue ( PA 291 ), it is the sole stop between central Philadelphia and the Philadelphia International Airport Terminals. In 2013, this station saw 367 boardings and 421 alightings on an average weekday. The station is accessed from Interstate 95 northbound via exit 10 and southbound via exit 12B. Access to the station is granted via a cul-de-sac off of Bartram Avenue and also from Mario Lanza Boulevard. Pedestrians and drivers coming from 84th Street must use either Crane Street to Lanza Boulevard or 84th to south on Bartram. While the station has no official SEPTA parking lot, a number of parking spaces along Mario Lanza Boulevard and Crane Street are used by commuters patronizing the stop. Eastwick station, located along the original Philadelphia, Wilmington and Baltimore Railroad main line, was conceived as 84th Street , under which name (as "proposed") it appeared on 1984 SEPTA informational maps.
