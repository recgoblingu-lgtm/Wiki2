Background

Westlicher Saalkreis ("Western Saale district") was a Verwaltungsgemeinschaft ("collective municipality") in the Saalekreis district, in Saxony-Anhalt , Germany . It was situated west of Halle (Saale) . The seat of the Verwaltungsgemeinschaft was in Salzmünde. It was disbanded on 1 January 2010.
