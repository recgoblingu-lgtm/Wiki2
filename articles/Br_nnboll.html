Brännboll

Brännboll is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Brännboll, also known as rundboll, and in parts of Finland, fyramål, is a bat-and-ball game similar to longball, played at amateur level throughout Sweden, as well in Finland, Norway, Denmark and Germany.

Background

Brännboll ( Swedish pronunciation: [ˈbrɛ̂nːbɔl] , Norwegian : brentball , German : Brennball , lit. ' burn-ball ' ), also known as rundboll ( Danish : rundbold , lit. ' round-ball ' ), and in parts of Finland , fyramål ( Finland Swedish , lit. ' four-goal ' ), is a bat-and-ball game similar to longball , played at amateur level throughout Sweden , as well in Finland , Norway , Denmark and Germany . The game is mostly played on fields, sports grounds, and in public parks, but it is also part of the PE curriculum in some areas. The world championship, called Brännbollscupen , is an annual event in the Swedish city of Umeå .

Name

The name brännboll ( lit. ' burn-ball ' ) is derived from the act of catching a player between two bases at the end of a batting round, referred to as "burning" them ( bränna ), roughly equivalent to being run out in cricket or out in baseball. The 'burn-ball' is also the German name for the game, Brennball , and in Norway , the name 'burn-ball' ( Norwegian : brentball ) is shared along slåball ( lit. ' hit-ball ' ) with Danish longball . Another name is rundboll , which is also the Danish name for the game ( Danish : rundbold ), which refers to the act of rounding the field, i.e. a home run ( frivarv/helrunda , lit. ' free lap/whole-round ' ).

History

The game has been known since the 18th century but has only recently reached the same popularity as the closely related ball game long ball .

Rules

The rules of brännboll differ between different areas and there is no governing body. Nevertheless, this section outlines some rules and traditions which are commonly upheld. In contrast to baseball and cricket, there is no dedicated pitcher or bowler : but the batters themselves throw or bounce the ball (usually a tennis ball ) before hitting it with their bat. A selection of bats is sometimes available, with a regular wooden or metallic baseball bat usually present, and a paddle-like bat (known as klappträ , washing paddle , tjejträ "girl bat" or kärringracket , "girl racket") resembling a cricket bat available for less experienced batters. On some occasions, there is also a stiff racket. The valid area for a successful batting is usually delimited by natural features such as trees, or simply an agreed upon imaginary border, this rule only restricts how widely the batter can hit, but not how far.
