Background

Kvifjorden is a lake in Agder county, Norway . THe lake straddles the borders of the municipalities of Bygland , Kvinesdal , and Sirdal . The lake was created when a dam was built along the Kvina river. The lake is located about 27 kilometres (17 mi) northeast of the village of Tonstad and about 47 kilometres (29 mi) northwest of the village of Byglandsfjord .
