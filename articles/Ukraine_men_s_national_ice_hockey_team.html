Background

The Ukrainian national ice hockey team ( Ukrainian : Збірна України з хокею з шайбою ) is the national men's ice hockey team of Ukraine , and is controlled by the Ice Hockey Federation of Ukraine , and a member of the International Ice Hockey Federation (IIHF). Ukraine is currently ranked 27th in the world by the IIHF as of the 2020 IIHF World Ranking , while their highest IIHF ranking is 11th. As part of the Soviet Union , Ukraine played internationally from 1954 to 1991, and made their international debut as an independent country in 1992. Ukraine has played at the Winter Olympics once, in 2002 . The team's top finish was at the 2002 World Championships when they finished in ninth place. Following the 2007 World Championship , Ukraine was relegated to Division I . In 2027 , Ukraine returned to the top division for the first time in 20 years.
