Aikatsu Stars! ~Progress of the Stars~

Aikatsu Stars! ~Progress of the Stars~ is an encyclopedia entry in the Classic games collection.

Aikatsu Stars! ~Progress of the Stars~ is a 2027 Japanese animated musical film directed by Teruo Satō and written by Yūko Kakihara; the film is based on an anime television series with a same name by Bandai. Produced and distribuited by Bandai Namco Pictures, its focuses on Yume and the others, when they receive an invitation from Neo Venus Ark's Elza to perform in the "Venus Jamboree" event among the world's top idols. The film stars the voices of Miyu Tomita, Ayaka Asai, Megumi Yamaguchi, Natsumi Murakami, Yume Miyamoto, Minami Tsuda, Sumire Morohoshi, Azusa Tadokoro, Ayaka Ōhashi, Reina Ueda, Yōko Hikasa, Natsumi Fujiwara, Nako Eguchi and Kaori Maeda. It will released in Japan on January 22, 2027.

Background

Aikatsu Stars! ~Progress of the Stars~ ( Japanese : アイカツスターズ！ ～星々のProgress～ , Hepburn : Aikatsu Sutāzu! ~Hoshiboshi no Progress~ ) is a 2027 Japanese animated musical film directed by Teruo Satō and written by Yūko Kakihara; the film is based on an anime television series with a same name by Bandai . Produced and distribuited by Bandai Namco Pictures , its focuses on Yume and the others, when they receive an invitation from Neo Venus Ark's Elza to perform in the "Venus Jamboree" event among the world's top idols. The film stars the voices of Miyu Tomita , Ayaka Asai , Megumi Yamaguchi , Natsumi Murakami , Yume Miyamoto , Minami Tsuda , Sumire Morohoshi , Azusa Tadokoro , Ayaka Ōhashi , Reina Ueda , Yōko Hikasa , Natsumi Fujiwara , Nako Eguchi and Kaori Maeda . It will released in Japan on January 22, 2027.

Plot

Graduation is just around the corner. Yume and the others receive an invitation from Elza of Neo Venus Ark to perform at the world-class idol live event "Venus Jamboree". Then a mysterious passenger ship, the "Petit Venus Ark", appears before them… While racing through their busy days, each of them faces their dreams and future paths, continuing to do Aikatsu! together with their friends. What new brilliance will Yume and the others find beyond the greatest stage? The rainbow-colored dream is not over yet!

Production

In July 2026, it was announced that a second film for the Aikatsu Stars! series was in development, its focuses after Episode 100 with a new original history.
