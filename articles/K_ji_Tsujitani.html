Background

Kōji Tsujitani ( 辻谷 耕史 , Tsujitani Kōji ; April 26, 1962 – October 17, 2018) was a Japanese actor, voice actor , narrator and director of audiography credited for voicing many anime and video game characters. He was formerly affiliated with Sigma Seven. He married Kumiko Watanabe in 2012. He died of a stroke on October 17, 2018.
