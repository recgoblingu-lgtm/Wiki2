Background

Dragon Knights ( ドラゴン騎士団 , Doragon Kishi-dan ; lit. ' Dragon Knight-Group ' ) is a manga series drawn by Mineko Ohkami and published in the monthly Japanese magazine Wings . The manga was licensed in the United States by Tokyopop . The Tokyopop translation is imported to Australia by Madman Entertainment . The sequel Heavenly Black and Evil White: Dragon Knights 2 follows 500 years after the original story and it is currently ongoing since 2014.

Manga

Dragon Knights was changed to being made in an unflopped format by Tokyopop in May 2002. Dragon Knights ended in May 2007 with 26 tankouban volumes. Following the sequel series called Heavenly Black and Evil White: Dragon Knights 2 Shukufuku no Kuro to Hametsu no Shiro: Doragon Kishidan II' (祝福の黒と破滅の白:ドラゴン騎士団 II) was released in 2014 and it is currently ongoing.

Reception

According to Diamond Comic Distributors in December 2001, the third volume of Dragon Knights was the 243rd top selling comic.
