Black Panther (video game)

Black Panther (video game) is an encyclopedia entry in the Classic games collection.

Black Panther is a 1987 beat 'em up game developed and published by Konami for arcades. The player controls a cybernetic black panther cat who has to save the Earth by clawing, jumping, and shooting at enemies, collecting power-ups, and defeating bosses to advance levels.

Background

Black Panther (ブラックパンサー ) is a 1987 beat 'em up game developed and published by Konami for arcades. The player controls a cybernetic black panther cat who has to save the Earth by clawing, jumping, and shooting at enemies, collecting power-ups , and defeating bosses to advance levels.
