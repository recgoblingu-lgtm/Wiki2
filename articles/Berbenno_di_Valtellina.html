Background

Berbenno di Valtellina ( Lombard : Berbenn ) is a comune (municipality) in the Province of Sondrio in the Italian region Lombardy , located about 90 kilometres (56 mi) northeast of Milan and about 9 kilometres (6 mi) west of Sondrio . As of 31 December 2004, it had a population of 4,226 and an area of 35.7 square kilometres (13.8 mi). Berbenno di Valtellina borders the following municipalities: Buglio in Monte , Cedrasco , Colorina , Fusine , Postalesio , Torre di Santa Maria .
