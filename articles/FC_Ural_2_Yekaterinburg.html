Background

FC Ural-2 Yekaterinburg ( Russian : ФК «Урал-2» (Екатеринбург) ) is a Russian football team based in Yekaterinburg . It is the farm club for FC Ural Yekaterinburg . For 2017–18 season, it received the license for the third-tier Russian Professional Football League . Ural's second team previously played on the professional level as FC Uralmash-d Yekaterinburg in the Russian Third League from 1994 to 1996.
