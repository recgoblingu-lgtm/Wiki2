Background

Dédalo was the name of two ships of the Spanish Navy:Spanish seaplane carrier Dédalo was a seaplane and balloon carrier converted from German cargo ship Neuenfels in 1922 Spanish aircraft carrier Dédalo was an aircraft carrier, initially commissioned as USS Cabot in 1943, acquired and renamed by the Spanish Navy in 1967, decommissioned in 1989
