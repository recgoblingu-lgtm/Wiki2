Background

The Peugeot 4007 is a compact crossover SUV produced by Mitsubishi Motors for the French automobile marque Peugeot , between July 2007 and April 2012. The equivalent Citroën badge-engineered version was the C-Crosser . Both were produced in Mitsubishi's Nagoya Plant in Okazaki , Japan , based on the second-generation Outlander . It was shown at the Geneva Motor Show in March 2007. Together, the 4007 and C-Crosser were the first Japan-produced cars sold under any French brand. They had their sales target of 30,000 units per year. It was officially launched on 12 July 2007. The standard 4007 comes with a Holland & Holland 4007 show car. Both vehicles had been planned to be assembled in the Nedcar plant in Born , Netherlands for the European market, however, the plan was postponed indefinitely as sales of the two models fell below the target of 30,000 units.
