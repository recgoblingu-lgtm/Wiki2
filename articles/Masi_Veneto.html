Background

Masi is a comune (municipality) in the Province of Padua in the Italian region Veneto , located about 70 kilometres (43 mi) southwest of Venice and about 45 kilometres (28 mi) southwest of Padua . Masi borders the following municipalities: Badia Polesine , Castelbaldo , Merlara , Piacenza d'Adige .
