Gamma Bros

Gamma Bros is an encyclopedia entry in the Indie games collection.

Gamma Bros is a 2006 Flash shoot 'em up game developed and published by American game indie studio PixelJAM Games, released as a free online title. It was nominated for "Best Web Browser Game" at the 2007 Independent Games Festival. It was the studio's first game after the mini-game Ratmaze. The game was also released as a smartphone game in 2016.

Background

Gamma Bros is a 2006 Flash shoot 'em up game developed and published by American game indie studio PixelJAM Games , released as a free online title. It was nominated for "Best Web Browser Game" at the 2007 Independent Games Festival . It was the studio's first game after the mini-game Ratmaze . The game was also released as a smartphone game in 2016. In the game, the player takes control of brothers Buzz and Zap Gamma who are on their way home from a galactic commute and must survive hordes of oncoming enemies.
