Background

Lucas County is a county located in the U.S. state of Iowa . As of the 2020 census , the population was 8,634. The county seat is Chariton . The county was formed in 1846 and was named for Robert Lucas , a Governor of the Territory.

Geography

According to the United States Census Bureau , the county has a total area of 434 square miles (1,120 km), of which 431 square miles (1,120 km) is land and 3.8 square miles (9.8 km) (0.9%) is water.

2020 census

As of the 2020 census , the county had a population of 8,634 and a population density of 20.0/sq mi (7.7/km). The census counted 96.75% of residents as belonging to a single race—92.76% were non-Hispanic White, 0.27% were Black, 0.08% were Native American, 0.34% were Asian, 0.00% were Native Hawaiian or Pacific Islander, and 4.26% identified with some other race or more than one race.
