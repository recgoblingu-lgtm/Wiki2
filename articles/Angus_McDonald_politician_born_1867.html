Background

Angus McDonald (July 18, 1867 – September 25, 1926) was a Canadian politician. He represented the riding of Timiskaming in the House of Commons of Canada from 1920 to 1925 after winning a by-election in 1920 and retaining his seat in the 1921 general election . He was an independent MP , with no party affiliation. Macdonald was a carpenter by trade and held pro- One Big Union sentiments. The area had deep radical labour roots, stemming back to strong IWW and OBU labour activism in 1907 . McDonald's riding was abolished prior to the 1925 election (to become the ridings of Timiskaming North and Timiskaming South), and he did not run in 1925 or any time later.
