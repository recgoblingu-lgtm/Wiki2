July 12 (Eastern Orthodox liturgics)

July 12 (Eastern Orthodox liturgics) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

July 12 in Eastern Orthodox liturgical calendars is a feast day and a day of commemoration of saints and martyrs. Although some Orthodox churches use the Revised Julian Calendar and others use the traditional Julian calendar, the fixed commemorations for their respective July 12 are broadly the same, no matter which calendar is used.

Background

July 12 in Eastern Orthodox liturgical calendars is a feast day and a day of commemoration of saints and martyrs. Although some Orthodox churches use the Revised Julian Calendar and others use the traditional Julian calendar , the fixed commemorations for their respective July 12 are broadly the same, no matter which calendar is used.
