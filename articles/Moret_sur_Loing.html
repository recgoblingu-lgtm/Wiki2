Background

French pronunciation: [mɔʁɛ syʁ lwɛ̃] , literally Moret on Loing ) is a former commune in the Seine-et-Marne department located at the Île-de-France region in north-central France. It is situated along the confluence of the rivers Loing and Seine . Moret–Veneux-les-Sablons station has rail connections to Montargis, Melun, Montereau-Fault-Yonne, Laroche-Migennes and Paris. On 1 January 2015, Moret-sur-Loing and Écuelles merged, becoming the commune of Orvanne , which in turn merged into the new commune of Moret-Loing-et-Orvanne on 1 January 2016. The town was a source of inspiration for Monet , Renoir , and Sisley .
