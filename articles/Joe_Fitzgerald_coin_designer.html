Background

Joe Fitzgerald (born in Washington, D.C. ) is an American artist and graphic designer. He is one of two designers of the 2005 United States nickel . His design, President Jefferson with Handwritten Liberty , appears on the obverse of that coin. Fitzgerald is a painter , who has had numerous one-man-shows. His work has also been exhibited in the U.S. State Department's Art in Embassies Program. He was one of twenty-four artists selected to create new designs under the United States Mint 's Artistic Infusion Program . Fitzgerald retired in 2005 as the chief of graphics at the National Library of Medicine in Bethesda, Maryland . While with the Library, he led or participated artistically in numerous projects and exhibitions over the years, such as the Turning the Pages series of rare-book animations and the Visible Human Project . He is also known as a public speaker. His wife, Jean Fitzgerald, has retired from her position as an archivist with the Smithsonian Institution . He is listed in Who's Who and Who's Who in American Art .
