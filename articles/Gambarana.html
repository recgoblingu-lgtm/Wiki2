Background

Gambarana is a comune (municipality) in the Province of Pavia in the Italian region Lombardy , located about 60 km (37 mi) southwest of Milan and about 35 km (22 mi) southwest of Pavia . As of 31 December 2021, it had a population of 199 and an area of 12.0 km (4.6 sq mi). Gambarana borders the following municipalities: Bassignana , Frascarolo , Isola Sant'Antonio , Mede , Pieve del Cairo , Suardi .
