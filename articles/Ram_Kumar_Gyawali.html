Ram Kumar Gyawali

Ram Kumar Gyawali is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Ram Kumar Gyawali is a Nepalese politician, belonging to the CPN. He was elected MP in 1994, but lost his seat in 1999. He is the deputy in charge of Sudurpashchim Province of CPN(Unified Socialist).

Background
