Hong Kong Guide

Hong Kong Guide is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Hong Kong Guide is a Hong Kong atlas published by the Survey and Mapping Office (SMO), Lands Department of Hong Kong Government. From 2005, Hong Kong Guide 2005 includes photomaps in parallel to traditional maps.

Background

Hong Kong Guide ( Chinese : 香港街 ) is a Hong Kong atlas published by the Survey and Mapping Office (SMO), Lands Department of Hong Kong Government . From 2005, Hong Kong Guide 2005 includes photomaps in parallel to traditional maps .
