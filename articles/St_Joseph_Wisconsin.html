Background

St. Joseph is a town in St. Croix County, Wisconsin , United States. The population was 4,178 at the 2020 census . The unincorporated communities of Burkhardt , and Houlton are located in the town.

Geography

According to the United States Census Bureau , the town has a total area of 32.0 square miles (82.88 km), of which 29.7 square miles (76.92 km) is land.

2010 census

As of the census of 2010 , there were 3,842 people, 1,388 households, and 1,136 families living in the town. The population density was 129.4 inhabitants per square mile (50.0/km). There were 1,511 housing units at an average density of 50.9 per square mile (19.7/km). The racial makeup of the town was 96.1% White , 2.6% from other races , and 1.3% from two or more races. Hispanic or Latino of any race were 2.4% of the population. The average household size was 2.74 and the average family size was 3.00. The gender makeup of the town was 51.6% male and 48.4% female. The median household income in the town was $84,769; the per capita income was $40,466.
