Background

Henry Sarwer-Foner is a Canadian television director. He has won numerous Canadian Screen Awards , Gemini , DGC and Canadian Comedy Awards as the director of This Hour Has 22 Minutes , Made in Canada and Corner Gas . In 2007 Henry Sarwer-Foner directed the CBC TV special Bob & Doug McKenzie's Two-Four Anniversary . The show featured notable guest appearances by Geddy Lee and others, with hosting by Canadian former prime minister Paul Martin .
