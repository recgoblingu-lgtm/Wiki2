Background

Cartan is a small lunar impact crater near the eastern edge of the Moon . It lies just to the west of the larger Apollonius . This crater originated from Crisium basin impact ejecta, and thus dates to the Nectarian period on the lunar geologic timescale . The rim is circular with a tiny crater along the eastern side. The interior floor is about half the diameter of the crater. A smaller crater attached to the southern rim is also attached to the north rim of Apollonius H, forming a short crater chain. Previously identified as satellite feature Apollonius D, this crater is named after the French mathematician Élie Cartan (1869-1951). Its designation was formally adopted by the International Astronomical Union in 1976.
