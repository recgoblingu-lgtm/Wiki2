Background

The 10th Parliament of Upper Canada was opened 8 January 1829. Elections in Upper Canada had been held in July 1828. All sessions were held at York, Upper Canada . This parliament was dissolved 8 September 1830 on the announcement of the death of King George IV . The House of Assembly of the 10th Parliament of Upper Canada had two sessions 8 January 1829 to 6 March 1830: Both the House and Parliament sat at the old York Court House on King Street.
