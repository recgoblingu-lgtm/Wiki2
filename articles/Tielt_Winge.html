Background

Tielt-Winge ( Dutch pronunciation: [ˌtiltˈʋɪŋə] ) is a municipality located in the Belgian province of Flemish Brabant . The municipality comprises the towns of Houwaart, Meensel-Kiezegem, Sint-Joris-Winge and Tielt. On 1 January 2006, Tielt-Winge had a total population of 11,604. The total area is 44.16 km² which gives a population density of 227 inhabitants per square kilometre.

Sint-Joris-Winge

Sint-Joris-Winge is a village in Tielt-Winge, which as of 2003, had 2,870 inhabitants. It is one source of the last name Winge , which is a habitational name derived from the village's name.
