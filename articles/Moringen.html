Background

Moringen ( German pronunciation: [ˈmoːʁɪŋən] ) is a town in the district Northeim , in the southern part of Lower Saxony , Germany . The town consists of the center Town and eight surrounding villages, Fredelsloh being one of them.

History

The town and its villages were founded over a thousand years ago. It is the seat of a psychiatric hospital, founded in late 19th century. During World War II the hospital was emptied and used by the Nazis as the Moringen concentration camp . Today it is again a psychiatric hospital.

Economy

Among the larger firms operating in the community is Piller, a maker of industrial fans. The Northeim district's major waste landfill is in Moringen.
