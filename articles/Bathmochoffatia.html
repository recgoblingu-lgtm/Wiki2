Background

Bathmochoffatia is an extinct mammal of the Upper Jurassic . It was a relatively early member of the also extinct order Multituberculata . It lived in Portugal at about the same time as the far more famous dinosaur, Allosaurus . It is in the suborder " Plagiaulacida ", family Paulchoffatiidae . The genus Bathmochoffatia (basal choffatia) was named by Hahn G. and Hahn R. in 1998. The primary species, Bathmochoffatia hapax , was also named by Hahn and Hahn. Fossil remains were found in strata dating to the Kimmeridgian (Upper Jurassic ) of Guimarota, Portugal. Remains consist of a solitary molar and one cheek tooth.
