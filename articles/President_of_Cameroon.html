Background

The President of Cameroon ( French : Président de la République du Cameroun ) is the executive head of state and de facto head of government of Cameroon and is the commander in chief of the Cameroon Armed Forces . The authority of the state is exercised both by the president and by the Parliament .

History

The office of president of Cameroon was established in 1960, following the country's independence from France . Only two people have held the office of president— Ahmadou Ahidjo , who served from 1960 to 1982, and Paul Biya , who has served since Ahidjo's resignation in 1982.
