Rodulf (missionary bishop)

Rodulf (missionary bishop) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Rodulf was a Roman Catholic bishop and Norman kinsman of Edward the Confessor. After working as a missionary for Olaf II of Norway in Norway and maybe Iceland, he was appointed by Edward as an Abbot of Abingdon in 1051 but died in 1052.

Background

Rodulf was a Roman Catholic bishop and Norman kinsman of Edward the Confessor . After working as a missionary for Olaf II of Norway in Norway and maybe Iceland, he was appointed by Edward as an Abbot of Abingdon in 1051 but died in 1052 (Kelly 2000).
