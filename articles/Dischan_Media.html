Dischan Media

Dischan Media is an encyclopedia entry in the Indie games collection.

Juniper's Knot is a kinetic visual novel developed by Canadian studio Dischan Media. Created in under a month, Juniper's Knot revolves around a lost boy and an imprisoned demoness, as they help overcome each other's obstacles through wit and memory, respectively.
