Background

Ardauli ( Sardinian : Ardaùle ) is a village and comune (municipality) in the Province of Oristano in the autonomous island region of Sardinia in Italy , located about 100 kilometres (62 mi) north of Cagliari and about 35 kilometres (22 mi) northeast of Oristano . It has 738 inhabitants. Ardauli borders the municipalities of Ghilarza , Neoneli , Nughedu Santa Vittoria , Sorradile , Tadasuni , and Ula Tirso .

Demographics

As of 2026, the population is 738, of which 47.4% are male, and 52.6% are female. Minors make up 8.5% of the population, and seniors make up 41.9%.

Immigration

As of 2025, of the known countries of birth of 738 residents, the most numerous are: Italy (674 – 91.3%), Romania (14 – 1.9%), France (13 – 1.8%), Belgium (12 – 1.6%), Morocco (6 – 0.8%), Moldova (5 – 0.7%).
