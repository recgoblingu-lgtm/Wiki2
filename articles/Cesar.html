Background

Cesarò is a comune (municipality) in the Metropolitan City of Messina in the Italian region Sicily , located about 120 kilometres (75 mi) east of Palermo and about 80 kilometres (50 mi) southwest of Messina . It is included in the Parco dei Nebrodi .
