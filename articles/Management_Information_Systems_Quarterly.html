Background

Management Information Systems Quarterly , referred to as MIS Quarterly , is an online-only quarterly peer-reviewed academic journal that covers research in management information systems and information technology . It was established in 1977 and is considered a major periodical in the information systems industry. An official journal of the Association for Information Systems , it is published by the Management Information Systems Research Center at the University of Minnesota . The current editor-in-chief is Susan Brown, University of Arizona. The journal had the highest impact factor (4.978) of all peer-reviewed academic journals in the field of business from 1992 to 2005. According to the Journal Citation Reports , the journal has a 2015 impact factor of 5.384.
