King Zhuang of Zhou

Background

King Zhuang of Zhou (died 682 BC) ( Chinese : 周莊王 ; pinyin : Zhōu Zhuāng Wáng ), personal name Ji Tuo , was a king of China's Zhou dynasty . He succeeded his father, King Huan , and was in turn succeeded by his son, King Xi . His younger son was Prince Tui.
