Background

Jennifer Ward (born December 4, 1963) is an American children's picture book author. Many of her books focus on science, nature and the Sonoran Desert . Ward has a B.A. in education from the University of Arizona . Her books have been translated into many languages and have received the International Reading Association's / CBC Children's Choice Award, the American Booksellers Association Book Sense Summer Pick, a Learning Magazine Teachers' Choice Award for Best Children's Picture Book, a Parents' Choice Award , a Governor's Selection for the states of New Jersey and Arizona , the Grand Canyon Reader Award, among other honors. Her book, There Was a Coyote Who Swallowed a Flea has been integrated into the architecture at the Buckeye Public Library in Buckeye, Arizona .
