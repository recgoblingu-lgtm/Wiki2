Background

Montefiore dell'Aso is a comune (municipality) in the Province of Ascoli Piceno in the Italian region Marche , located about 70 kilometres (43 mi) southeast of Ancona and about 25 kilometres (16 mi) northeast of Ascoli Piceno . One of several Hilltowns in Central Italy , Montefiore dell'Aso borders the following municipalities: Campofilone , Carassai , Lapedona , Massignano , Monterubbiano , Moresco , Petritoli , Ripatransone . It is one of I Borghi più belli d'Italia ("The most beautiful villages of Italy"). Historical sights include the Romanesque-Gothic church of St. Francis, housing the sepulchres of Cardinal Gentile Partino (1310) and painter Adolfo de Carolis , while the apses has frescoes by the Master of Offida .
