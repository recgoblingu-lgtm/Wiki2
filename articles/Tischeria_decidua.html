Background

Tischeria decidua is a moth of the family Tischeriidae . It is found in Central and Southern Europe , but has recently expanded its range and has been spotted in the Netherlands and Poland . The larvae feed on Castanea sativa and Quercus species, including Quercus cerris , Quercus faginea , Quercus macrolepis , Quercus pedunculiflora , Quercus petraea , Quercus pubescens and Quercus robur x turneri . They mine the leaves of their host plant.
