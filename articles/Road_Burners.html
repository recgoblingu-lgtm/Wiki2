Background

Road Burners is a 1999 motorcycle racing arcade game developed and released by Atari Games . It features a tilting motorcycle and the ability to be hooked to other like machines for up to 8 simultaneous players.

Gameplay

The player's score is based upon the distance accumulated in the time limit. Players must pass checkpoints to earn additional time. Tracks in the game include Washington, D.C. , Paris , London , Las Vegas , and the Isle of Man .
