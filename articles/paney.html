Épaney

Épaney is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Épaney is a commune in the Calvados department in the Normandy region in northwestern France. Jehan Le Saulx d'Espanney, the 16th-century French playwright, was born in the village.

Background

Épaney ( French pronunciation: [epanɛ] ) is a commune in the Calvados department in the Normandy region in northwestern France . Jehan Le Saulx d'Espanney , the 16th-century French playwright , was born in the village.

Geography

The commune is made up of the following collection of villages and hamlets, Fontaine , La Queue de Renard and Épaney . The river Perrières flows through the commune.

Notable people

Jehan Le Saulx d'Espanney (Born 16th century -died 17th century) a French playwright was born here.
