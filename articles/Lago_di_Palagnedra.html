Background

Lago di Palagnedra is a manmade lake at Palagnedra , Ticino , Switzerland . The reservoir has a capacity of 4,260,000 m (3,450 acre⋅ft) and a surface area of 25 ha (62 acres). The dam on the Melezza river was completed in 1952, height 72 m (236 ft). The lake lies between Pizzo Ruscada and the Gridone . The north side of the lake is overlooked by the Domodossola-Locarno railway .
