Background

Taeko Kawata ( 川田 妙子 , Kawata Taeko ; born March 20, 1965) is a Japanese voice actress best known for voicing Amy Rose from the Sonic the Hedgehog franchise . Her former stage name is Taeko Yamada ( 山田 妙子 , Yamada Taeko ) . After graduating from Jissen Commercial High School, she worked at 81 Produce until 2010. On October 1, 2019, she announced the formation of a private firm, T-River LLC, and the opening of a private school at the same time.
