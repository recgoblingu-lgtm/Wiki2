Background

Zimatlán de Alvarez is a town and municipality in Oaxaca in south-western Mexico . The municipality covers an area of 255.16 km. It is part of the Zimatlán District in the west of the Valles Centrales Region , and consists of four separate exclaves. As of 2005, the municipality had a total population of 18,370.
