June 7 (Eastern Orthodox liturgics)

June 7 (Eastern Orthodox liturgics) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

June 7 in Eastern Orthodox liturgical calendars is a feast day and a day of commemoration of saints and martyrs. Although some Orthodox churches use the Revised Julian Calendar and others use the traditional Julian calendar, the fixed commemorations for their respective June 7 are broadly the same, no matter which calendar is used.

Background

June 7 in Eastern Orthodox liturgical calendars is a feast day and a day of commemoration of saints and martyrs. Although some Orthodox churches use the Revised Julian Calendar and others use the traditional Julian calendar , the fixed commemorations for their respective June 7 are broadly the same, no matter which calendar is used.
