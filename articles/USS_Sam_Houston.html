USS Sam Houston

USS Sam Houston is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Two ships of the United States Navy have been named USS Sam Houston.The first USS Sam Houston (1861) was a schooner serving during the American Civil War. The second USS Sam Houston (SSBN-609) was an Ethan Allen-class submarine serving during the Cold War, named for the President of the Republic of Texas.
