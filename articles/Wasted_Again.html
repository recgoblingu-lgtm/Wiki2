Background

Wasted…Again is an album released by American hardcore punk band Black Flag in 1987 on SST Records . It is a "best-of" compilation released after Black Flag's breakup in 1986. It features various songs about drinking and beer from their discography. In 2005, Spin named it one of "The Ten Greatest Compilations of the Spin Era".
