Background

Hellblade is an action-adventure video game franchise created and developed by Ninja Theory . The series follows the journey of Senua , a Pict warrior who experiences psychosis , as she confronts personal trauma, grief, and mythological forces inspired by Norse and Celtic mythology . The franchise is noted for its depiction of mental illness, cinematic presentation, and use of binaural audio. The series began with Hellblade: Senua's Sacrifice in 2017 and was followed by Senua's Saga: Hellblade II in 2024 and Senua in 2027.

Hellblade: Senua's Sacrifice (2017)

Senua's Sacrifice was released on 8 August 2017 for Windows and PlayStation 4 . The game follows Senua as she travels through a vision of Helheim in an attempt to rescue the soul of her deceased lover Dillion. The title received critical acclaim for its narrative, visual presentation, audio design, and portrayal of psychosis .

Senua's Saga: Hellblade II (2024)

Released in May 2024 for Windows and Xbox Series X/S , Hellblade II continues Senua's story as she journeys through 10th-century Iceland . The game expanded upon the cinematic presentation of its predecessor and received praise for its graphics, sound design, and performances.

Senua (2027)

Senua is scheduled for release in 2027 for PlayStation 5 , Windows, and Xbox Series X/S. Set after the events of Hellblade II , the game follows Senua as she becomes trapped in a vision of purgatory based on her childhood homeland. Unlike previous entries, the title was designed as a larger-scale action-adventure game featuring expanded exploration, puzzle-solving, and combat systems.
