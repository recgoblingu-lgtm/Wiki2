Background

Ballinakil ( Irish : Baile na Coille ) is a small, sparsely populated townland , located in County Wicklow , Ireland . It is situated off the Glenealy - Rathdrum road, close to Deputy's Pass . The nearest town is Rathdrum , 3 km to the south-west.
