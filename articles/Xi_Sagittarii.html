Background

The Bayer designation Xi Sagittarii ( ξ Sagittarii ) is shared by two stars , ξ Sagittarii and ξ² Sagittarii , in the constellation Sagittarius , separated by 0.46° in the sky. Because they are close to the ecliptic , they can be occulted by the Moon and, very rarely, by planets . The last occultation of ξ² Sagittarii by a planet took place on 22 December 1810, when it was occulted by Venus .
