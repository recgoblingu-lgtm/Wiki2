Background

Hung Shing Temples or Tai Wong Temples are temples dedicated to Hung Shing Tai Wong ( Chinese : 洪聖大王 ). Hung Shing temples have been widely built in southern China, especially Guangdong province and in Hong Kong .

Existing temples

There are several Hung Shing Temples in Hong Kong, including 6 on Lantau Island and one only in urban Kowloon . The table provides a partial list of these temples. Hung Shing Festivals ( 洪聖誕 ) are celebrated on the 13th day of the 2nd month in Chinese calendar at the Hung Shing Temples in Ap Lei Chau , Tai Kok Tsui , Cheung Chau , Sha Lo Wan and Kau Sai Chau .
