Background

Hitchita is a town in McIntosh County , Oklahoma , United States. The Encyclopedia of Oklahoma History and Culture says that the town was named for a band of Muskhogean Indians that had been absorbed into the Creek tribe. The population of Hitchita was 60 at the 2020 census , down from 88 in 2010 .

Geography

Hitchita is located in northwestern McIntosh County, in the vicinity of the area known as the High Spring Mountains. It is less than a mile north of U.S. Route 266 , which leads east-southeast 14 miles (23 km) to Checotah and west 6 miles (10 km) to Grayson . According to the U.S. Census Bureau , the town of Hitchita has a total area of 0.1 square miles (0.26 km), all land. It is 1.5 miles (2.4 km) north of Oklahoma’s largest lake, Lake Eufaula , a reservoir on the Canadian River .
