Background

The Diocese of Jeonju ( Latin : Dioecesis Ieoniuensis ) is a Latin Church ecclesiastical territory or diocese of the Catholic Church in South Korea . It is in the ecclesiastical province of the metropolitan Archdiocese of Gwangju , yet depends on the missionary Dicastery for Evangelization . Its cathedra is in the Joongang Cathedral of the Sacred Heart of Jesus, in the episcopal see of Jeonju 전주시, Jeollabuk-do 전라북도. It also has a former cathedral , now Jeondong Church of the Sacred Heart of Jesus, in Jeonju. Both are named for the Sacred Heart of Jesus .

Statistics

As of 2014, it pastorally served 190,465 Catholics (10.2% of 1,872,965 total) on 8,066 km in 93 parishes and 5 missions with 213 priests (209 diocesan, 4 religious), 341 lay religious (7 brothers, 334 sisters) and 42 seminarians.
