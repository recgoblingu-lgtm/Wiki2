Torino 2006 (video game)

Torino 2006 (video game) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Torino 2006 is the official video game of the XX Olympic Winter Games, hosted by Turin, Italy in 2006. Developed by German studio 49Games and published by 2K, it was released for Microsoft Windows, PlayStation 2, and Xbox. It is the first licensed Olympic video game to be released on a Microsoft home console, since a planned Xbox version of the prior installment, Salt Lake 2002, was cancelled.

Background

Torino 2006 is the official video game of the XX Olympic Winter Games , hosted by Turin , Italy in 2006. Developed by German studio 49Games and published by 2K (and I-play), it was released for Microsoft Windows , PlayStation 2 , and Xbox . It is the first licensed Olympic video game to be released on a Microsoft home console, since a planned Xbox version of the prior installment, Salt Lake 2002 , was cancelled.
