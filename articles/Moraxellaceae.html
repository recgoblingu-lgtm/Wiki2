Background

The Moraxellaceae are a family of Gammaproteobacteria , including a few pathogenic species. Others are harmless commensals of mammals and humans or occur in water or soil. The species are mesophilic or psychrotrophic ( Psychrobacter ). Moraxella catarrhalis and Acinetobacter baumannii are human pathogens, and Moraxella bovis is the cause of "pinkeye" of cattle ( infectious bovine keratoconjunctivitis ).
