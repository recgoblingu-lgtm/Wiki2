Background

Sweeny is a city in Brazoria County, Texas , United States, the westernmost incorporated town in the county. The population was 3,626 as of 2020 . The city's motto is "A City with Pride". The city was once known as Adamston.

Geography and transport

Sweeny is 30 feet (9.1 m) above sea level and is 20 miles (32 km) from the Gulf of Mexico . The San Bernard River flows 1-mile (1.6 km) east of city limits. The town is in a dense forest on coastal plains. Sweeny is at the intersections of Texas Farm to Market Roads 1459 and 524, along the Missouri Pacific Railroad, twenty miles southwest of Angleton , in west central Brazoria County. The Union Pacific Railroad cuts a path through a small piece of the south side of Sweeny, with two grade crossings and a railyard. Train speeds through here usually range from 35 to 60 miles per hour (56 to 97 km/h) .
