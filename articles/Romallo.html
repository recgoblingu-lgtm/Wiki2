Background

Romallo ( Nones : Romàl ) is a comune (municipality) in Trentino in the northern Italian region Trentino-Alto Adige/Südtirol , located about 35 kilometres (22 mi) north of Trento . As of 31 December 2004, it had a population of 576 and an area of 2.4 square kilometres (0.93 mi). Romallo's parish church is the Chiesa di San Vitale [ it ] . Romallo borders the municipalities of Revò , Cloz , Dambel and Sanzeno .
