The Sterling Chase

The Sterling Chase is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

The Sterling Chase, also known as Graduation Week, is a 1999 drama film written and directed by Tanya Fenmore and starring Nicholle Tom, Jack Noseworthy, Devon Odessa, Sean Patrick Thomas, Alanna Ubach and John Livingston. The independent film was screened at the Mill Valley Film Festival on September 17, 1999. Screen story was written by Jeremy Dauber, currently an assistant professor at Columbia University. Filming took place on the campuses of Bryn Mawr College and Rosemont College, namely in the Main Building, or Joseph Sinnott Mansion.
