Background

Pease is a lunar impact crater that lies on the far side of the Moon , in the north-northwestern edge of the huge skirt of ejecta that surrounds the Mare Orientale impact basin. It lies just over one crater diameter to the east of the smaller crater Butlerov . To the east-northeast of Pease is the somewhat larger Nobel . It is a roughly circular, bowl-shaped formation with an outer rim that is only moderately eroded. No significant craters lie across the rim edge or the interior, and there is a slight straightening of the western rim.
