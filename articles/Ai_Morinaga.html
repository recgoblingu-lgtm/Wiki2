Background

Ai Morinaga ( 森永あい , Morinaga Ai ) (28 April 1981 – 2 August 2019) was a Japanese manga artist from Okayama Prefecture . Among her best-known series are Your and My Secret and My Heavenly Hockey Club . Morinaga's last work was Kirara no Hoshi , which was published in Betsufure magazine from 2010 to 2015. Morinaga died on August 2, 2019.
