Downfall (2009 video game)

Downfall (2009 video game) is an encyclopedia entry in the Indie games collection.

Downfall is a 2009 indie point-and-click horror adventure game developed by Harvester Games for Windows. It follows Joe Davis, who is searching for his wife at the Quiet Haven Hotel, after her disappearance.

Background

Downfall is a 2009 indie point-and-click horror adventure game developed by Harvester Games for Windows . It follows Joe Davis, who is searching for his wife at the Quiet Haven Hotel after her disappearance. To progress, the player must explore rooms, solve puzzles and make choices. It was created by Remigiusz Michalski, with soundtrack contributions from his brother Michal Michalski. The original game was generally well received, with praise for its music and background art. However, criticism was directed at the glitches and portions of the writing. A remake was released in February 2016, featuring revised graphics, a new soundtrack, keyboard controls and voice acting. It is part of the Devil Came Through Here trilogy, alongside The Cat Lady and Lorelai .

Gameplay

It is a point-and-click game where the player controls the main character, Joe Davis. The gameplay loop involves exploring rooms, fetching objects and completing puzzles. There is an inventory where items can be kept and used in certain situations. At a certain point, a second character named Agnes can be controlled instead of Joe. The game also includes a boss fight with a life bar. Sometimes the player has to make choices by clicking on one of two panels. The game has multiple endings, depending on choices made along the journey.

Plot

Joe Davis and his wife, Ivy, are having trouble in their marriage. The couple decides to leave London for a break, but they are caught in a storm while Ivy has a panic attack. The two arrive at Quiet Haven Hotel, where Joe plans to spend the night. He tries to reason with Ivy, who is speaking incoherently. Suddenly, she disappears and the hotel becomes a "nightmare" version of itself. Joe has to search for and kill four manifestations of an entity in order to reach the other side of a mirror, where he hopes to find her.

Themes

Downfall explores child abuse and eating disorders, as well as loss of love. Remigiusz Michalski described the game as "a tale of a man blinded by love, desperately trying to save someone against their own will". He also characterized the horror setting as a metaphor for real-life situations, and the Quiet Haven Hotel as Joe's attempt at symbolic escapism from his problems.
