Background

Marosszék ( Hungarian pronunciation: [ˈmɒroʃseːk] ) was one of the seats in the historical Székely Land area. Historically it was part of the Kingdom of Hungary . After World War I it became part of Romania . It was named after the Maros , a river with the biggest discharge in the seat. The composer Zoltán Kodály wrote the Dances of Marosszék , a 1927 piece for piano which was later orchestrated, based on the folk music of the region.
