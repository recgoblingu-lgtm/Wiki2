Saskatchewan Highway 102

Saskatchewan Highway 102 is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Highway 102 is a provincial highway in Saskatchewan, Canada. It runs from Highway 2 at La Ronge and Lac La Ronge Provincial Park to Southend, at Reindeer Lake. The entire route is within the Northern Saskatchewan Administration District. The highway is about 221 kilometres (137 mi) long, the majority of which is gravel surface. The CanAm Highway comprises Saskatchewan Highways 35, 39, 6, 3, 2, and 102.
