Background

The Australian Science Fiction Media Awards (ASFMA Awards) were annual awards for achievement in non-literary media and media appreciation within Australian science fiction. They were awarded at the Australian National Science Fiction Convention from 1984 to 1997. The awards were formally dissolved and incorporated into the Ditmar Awards at the Business Meeting of the Australian National Science Fiction Convention (Spawncon II), held in Melbourne in 1999, with the formal merger of the Australian ‘Media NatCon’ and the NatCon.

1983: Conquest '83, 24–25 September 1983 Brisbane

It was decided to give ASFMA awards at the annual Media Natcon, starting in 1984.
