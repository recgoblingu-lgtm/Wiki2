USS Shawmut

USS Shawmut is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

USS Shawmut, named for the shawmut, is the name of more than one ship of the United States Navy:USS Shawmut (1863) was an American Civil War gunboat in use 1863–1877. Massachusetts was renamed Shawmut (CM-4) in 1917 and was renamed USS Oglala on 1 January 1928; the minelayer was decommissioned in 1946. USS Shawmut (CM-11) was the final name of USS Salem (CM-11), a minelayer and net-laying ship during World War II.
