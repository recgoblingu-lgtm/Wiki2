Background

Nugegoda ( Sinhala : නුගේගොඩ ) is a large, densely populated suburb of Colombo , Sri Lanka. It is located just south of Sri Jayawardenapura Kotte / Maharagama and north-east of Dehiwela Mt. Lavinia . It has a population of over 250,000 people.
