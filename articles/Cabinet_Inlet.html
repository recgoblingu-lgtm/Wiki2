Cabinet Inlet

Cabinet Inlet is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Cabinet Inlet is an icy inlet, 36 miles (58 km) long in a northwest–southeast direction, and some 27 miles (43 km) wide at its entrance between Cape Alexander and Cape Robinson, along the east coast of Graham Land, Antarctica. It was named and charted by the Falkland Islands Dependencies Survey (FIDS) and aerially photographed by the Ronne Antarctic Research Expedition in December 1947. It is named for the British War Cabinet which authorized the FIDS in 1943.

Background

Cabinet Inlet ( 66°35′S 63°10′W ﻿ / ﻿ 66.583°S 63.167°W ﻿ / -66.583; -63.167 ) is an icy inlet , 36 miles (58 km) long in a northwest–southeast direction, and some 27 miles (43 km) wide at its entrance between Cape Alexander and Cape Robinson , along the east coast of Graham Land , Antarctica . It was named and charted by the Falkland Islands Dependencies Survey (FIDS) and aerially photographed by the Ronne Antarctic Research Expedition in December 1947. It is named for the British War Cabinet which authorized the FIDS in 1943.
