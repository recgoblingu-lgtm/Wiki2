Background

Christ the Redeemer is a Russian icon discovered by accident in a dilapidated woodshed near Zvenigorod in 1919. With several other icons stored nearby, it was attributed as the work of Andrei Rublev , painted for one of Zvenigorod cathedrals in the 1420s. It is exhibited in the Tretyakov Gallery of Moscow . After the intense wear over the years, it went over a restoration in 1956, after the de-Stalinization of the Soviet Union .
