Background

In mathematics , a differentiable manifold of dimension n is called parallelizable if there exist smooth vector fields on the manifold, such that at every point of the tangent vectors provide a basis of the tangent space at . Equivalently, the tangent bundle is a trivial bundle , so that the associated principal bundle of linear frames has a global section on A particular choice of such a basis of vector fields on is called a parallelization (or an absolute parallelism ) of .
