Background

Toribash is a free-to-play turn-based third-person fighting game created by Singaporean developer Nabi Studios and published by Nicalis , which released on Microsoft Windows in 2006. A port for the Wii was released in July 2010, and for OS X and Linux in May 2014. A sequel, titled Toribash Next , was released in January 2024.

Gameplay

The game is mainly played by manipulating the various joints on the player's doll-like character over turn-based sequences to execute movements. These movements primarily include relaxing, holding, contracting and extending of the player model's neck , pecs , elbows , wrists , abdomen , glutes , hips , knees and ankles ; raising and lowering the shoulders , tilting the lumbar and chest left and right, and grabbing and un-grabbing of the hands . The game features singleplayer and multiplayer, where movements are executed in turn-frames of varying lengths within set match-frames. The game is heavily sand-box based.

Reception

Toribash has been reviewed by PC Gamer UK , PC Gamer and PC Format , receiving 87%, 68% and 90% respectively.
