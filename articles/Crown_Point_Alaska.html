Background

Crown Point is a census-designated place (CDP) in Kenai Peninsula Borough , Alaska , United States. As of the 2020 census , Crown Point had a population of 119. Land access to this area is by either the Alaska Railroad or the Seward Highway .

Geography

Crown Point is located in the north-central part of the Kenai Peninsula at 60°25′8″N 149°21′19″W ﻿ / ﻿ 60.41889°N 149.35528°W ﻿ / 60.41889; -149.35528 (60.418833, -149.355340), near the confluence of the Trail River and Kenai Lake . It is bordered to the north by Moose Pass and to the south by Primrose . Alaska Route 9, the Seward Highway, leads north from Crown Point 12 miles (19 km) to Alaska Route 1 at Tern Lake, and south 24 miles (39 km) to Seward . Portions of this CDP were once the settlement known as Lawing , which was centered around Alaska Nellie's Homestead . According to the United States Census Bureau , the Crown Point CDP has a total area of 3.6 square miles (9.3 km), all of it land.

Demographics
