Background

Aplin Islet is a small island north of Shelburne Bay in far north Queensland , Australia about 140 km north of Cape Grenville , Cape York Peninsula in the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Queensland , Australia . It is within the Shire of Cook . The island has an area of about 4 hectares. It rises to a height of 5.82 metres above sea level. It is about 30 km east of Orford Ness .

Ecology

Aplin Islet is a restricted access area to protect nesting seabirds. Crocodiles have been sighted on Aplin Islet.
