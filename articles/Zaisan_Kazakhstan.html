Background

Zaisan or Zaysan ( Kazakh : Зайсан , Zaisan ; Russian : Зайсан ), is a town in the East Kazakhstan Region of Kazakhstan , the administrative center of Zaisan District . It is situated near the southeastern corner of Lake Zaysan at an altitude of 660 meters (2170 ft) above sea level. Population: 14,389 (2009 census results); 16,021 (1999 census results). The town is located near the eastern terminus of European route E127 , which connects it with Omsk in Russia as well as the rest of the European route network.

Climate

Zaisan has a hot-summer humid continental climate ( Köppen climate classification Dfa ).
