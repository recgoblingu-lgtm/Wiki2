Background

Herndon is a city in Rawlins County , Kansas , United States. As of the 2020 census , the population of the city was 119. It is located approximately 12.5 miles (20.1 km) south of the Kansas- Nebraska state border.

History

Herndon was originally called Pesth (named after Pest, Hungary, which later merged to become Budapest ) and under the latter name was laid out in 1878. It was renamed Herndon in 1879, in honor of William H. Herndon , law partner of Abraham Lincoln . Herndon was incorporated as a city in 1906.

Geography

According to the United States Census Bureau , the city has a total area of 0.26 square miles (0.67 km), all land.
