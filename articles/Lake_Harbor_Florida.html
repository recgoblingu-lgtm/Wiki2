Background

Lake Harbor is a census-designated place (CDP) in Palm Beach County , Florida , United States. It is part of the Miami metropolitan area of South Florida despite being physically and culturally closer to the Florida Heartland . The population was 49 at the 2020 US census.

Geography

According to the United States Census Bureau , the CDP has a total area of 3.4 km (1.3 sq mi), all land. The community is centered at the intersection of Mutt Thomas Road and U.S. Route 27 (which runs concurrently with Florida State Road 80 ). It is located along the southern banks of Lake Okeechobee , at the beginning of the Miami Canal . John Stretch Park is also located alongside the north end of Lake Harbor and the Lake Okeechobee .

2020 census

As of the 2020 United States census , there were 49 people, 15 households, and 15 families residing in the CDP.
