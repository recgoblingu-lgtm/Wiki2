Background

The Dodge Demon was a roadster concept car made by DaimlerChrysler , designed to be slotted below the Viper for the Dodge brand. The name was previously used in the 1970s for the Dodge Dart Demon, a fastback model. The Demon was first shown at the 2007 Geneva Auto Show , and was considered for production. The materials, construction, and design of the Demon were such that production would be feasible. It was powered by a 2.4 2.4 L gasoline World Engine , producing 172 hp (128 kW) at 6000 rpm and 165 lb⋅ft (224 N⋅m) of torque at 4400 rpm. It was conceived to compete with cars like the Pontiac Solstice , Saturn Sky , and Mazda MX-5 .
