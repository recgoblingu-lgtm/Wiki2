Background

Yingtan ( simplified Chinese : 鹰潭 ; traditional Chinese : 鷹潭 ; pinyin : Yīngtán ; lit. 'Eagle Pond') is a prefecture-level city in the east of Jiangxi province, People's Republic of China, bordering Fujian to the southeast. Its location near the trisection of Jiangxi, Fujian, and Zhejiang has made it a strategically important city for centuries. Today, it continues to be a major rail transport hub. It is best known as the Capital of Copper , and located here is Jiangxi Copper and its the Guixi Smelter . Near the city of Yingtan is the resort area of Mount Longhu , which purports to be the birthplace of Taoism and hence, has great symbolic value to Taoists. The region has many interesting temples, cave complexes, mountains, and villages.
