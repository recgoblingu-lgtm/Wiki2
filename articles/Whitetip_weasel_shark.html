Background

The whitetip weasel shark ( Paragaleus leucolomatus ) is a weasel shark of the family Hemigaleidae . Only one specimen, in Kosi Bay , South Africa , has been caught. That specimen was 96 cm (38 in) long. In 2020, a fuzzy image believed to be this shark was obtained on the show Extinct or Alive . The reproduction of this shark is viviparous .
