Kindergarten (video game)

Kindergarten (video game) is an encyclopedia entry in the Indie games collection.

Kindergarten is an indie abstract puzzle adventure game developed by Con Man Games alongside Sean Young and published by SmashGames for Windows and macOS in June 2017. In July 2017, it was released for Linux.

Background

Kindergarten is an indie abstract puzzle adventure game developed by Con Man Games alongside Sean Young and published by SmashGames for Windows and macOS in June 2017. In July 2017, it was released for Linux. It follows an unnamed child who keeps repeating the same day for some unexplained reason, with the main goal of finding out what happened to a missing classmate. The game employs interconnected character quests across the repeated days and includes dark humor. According to the developers, it was inspired by painful childhood memories. The game received praise for its writing and comedy, while criticism was directed at the repetition of dialogue and sometimes of entire quests. A sequel, called Kindergarten 2 , was released in 2019. In 2025, Kindergarten 3 was launched. A fourth entry for the game series was teased as well, Kindergarten 4 .

Gameplay

The player takes control of a young child, who goes to a kindergarten. The main gameplay loop consists of playing the same day (Monday) again and again. By making different choices in each attempt, including who to interact with, the player can cause drastically different events to take place. When a day starts, the player can choose to withdraw money from a piggy bank in the starting hub. A day is divided into five sections: before school, class, lunch time, play time and "show and tell". The actions the protagonist can take within each period of time are limited, represented in-game by apples in the lower right corner of the screen. Once every apple is used, the game advances to the next section. Unused apples can be traded for money, which is useful for buying items. On each new attempt, the player can choose a classmate (or the teacher) and complete their quest. By performing certain actions over the course of the day, they ultimately receive a special item. These objects are added permanently to the protagonist's home and the player can choose to bring them into new loops, which sometimes is essential for certain quests. Money is also saved between attempts, allowing the player to accumulate it. Other inventory items are reset between days, and characters killed during an attempt come back in the next. Setting

The kindergarten is a brutal place where the students might be killed for various reasons. One of the children, Billy, has gone missing. The protagonist's goal is to find out what happened to him. In order to do that, he must help different characters in their own quests across repeated versions of Monday. By doing so, the main character is able to complete Lily's quest and uncover the truth.
