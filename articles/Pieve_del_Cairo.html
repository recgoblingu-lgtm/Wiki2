Background

Pieve del Cairo is a comune (municipality) in the Province of Pavia in the Italian region of Lombardy , located about 60 km (37 mi) southwest of Milan and about 30 km (19 mi) southwest of Pavia . As of 31 December 2004, it had a population of 2,179 and an area of 25.5 km (9.8 sq mi). Pieve del Cairo borders the municipalities of Ferrera Erbognone , Galliavola , Gambarana , Isola Sant'Antonio , Mede , Mezzana Bigli , and Villa Biscossi .
