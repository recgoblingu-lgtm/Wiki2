Background

Grinnell is a city in Gove County , Kansas , United States. As of the 2020 census , the population of the city was 260. It is located approximately 0.5 miles (0.80 km) north of the intersection of I-70 and Grove County Rd 30.

History

Grinnell was named for businessman Moses Hicks Grinnell. The first newspaper in Grinnell was the Golden Belt , in 1885.

2025 tornado

Before 7pm on May 18, 2025, an EF3 tornado struck the west side of Grinnell causing heavy damage to roughly 15 to 20 homes.

Geography

According to the United States Census Bureau , the city has a total area of 0.51 square miles (1.32 km), all land.
