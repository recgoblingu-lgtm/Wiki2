Background

Havok Vision Game Engine is a discontinued, cross-platform , 3D game engine originally authored by Trinigy and later acquired by Havok . It was first released in 2003, and saw its final release in 2014. As of its eighth version, available target platforms were Microsoft Windows ( DX9 , DX10 , DX11 ), Xbox 360 , PlayStation 3 , Nintendo Wii and Wii U , iOS , Android , Sony 's PlayStation Vita , and most major browsers ( IE6 and up, Firefox 2.0 and up, Google Chrome , Opera 9 and up). Trinigy and its Vision Engine were acquired by Havok in 2011. Vision Engine has since been discontinued with no support being provided.
