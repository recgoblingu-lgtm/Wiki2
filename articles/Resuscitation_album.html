Background

Resuscitation is a 2001 album released by American electronic music duo ADULT. Before this release, ADULT.'s output had consisted of various EPs and 12 inch singles released independently; this is actually a compilation album of many of their earlier singles. A bulk of the songs are presented on Resuscitation in remixed or re-recorded forms. ADULT.'s members, Adam Lee Miller (music) and Nicola Kuperus (vocals and music) distributed the album on their own label, Ersatz Audio . Kuperus, a visual artist, is also responsible for the album's cover art photography.
