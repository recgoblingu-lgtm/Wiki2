Background

The Christian Democratic Party was a political party in Namibia . It is known for its centrist and Christian democratic values. Led by Petrus "Piet" Matheus Junius , the Christian Democratic Union merged into CDP, strengthening its political presence in the country. It was part of the Democratic Turnhalle Alliance of Namibia (DTA), along with many other small/regional parties including the Democratic Turnhalle Party and the National Unity Democratic Organization (NUDO). The DTA was set up as a South African-supported opposition group to the pro-independence SWAPO movement/party. In 2017, the DTA changed its name to the Popular Democratic Movement (PDM). In October 1989, the party was a signatory to the report of the UN Security Council concerning the question of Namibia.
