Background

The Burrowa-Pine Mountain National Park is a national park in the Hume region of Victoria, Australia. The 18,400-hectare (45,000-acre) national park is situated approximately 315 kilometres (196 mi) northeast of Melbourne and 120 kilometres (75 mi) east of Wodonga . The Pine Mountain , one of the largest monoliths in Australia, is located within the park and is believed to be 1.5 times the size of Uluru . The highest peak in the park is Mount Burrowa at an elevation of 1,300 metres (4,300 ft) above sea level .
