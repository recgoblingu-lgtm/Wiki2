Background

The minnow-nase , ( Chondrostoma phoxinus ) is a species of freshwater ray-finned fish in the family Leuciscidae . It is found in Bosnia and Herzegovina and Croatia . Its natural habitats are rivers , intermittent rivers, and inland karsts . It is threatened by habitat loss and considered Endangered .
