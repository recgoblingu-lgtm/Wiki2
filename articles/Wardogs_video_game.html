Background

Wardogs is an upcoming multiplayer first-person shooter video game developed by British video game developer Bulkhead and published by Team17 . It was released into close beta on 22 August 2026 for people who pre-ordered it and is releasing into early access for Microsoft Windows on 10 September 2026.

Gameplay

Wardogs is a multiplayer first-person shooter MilSim simulation video game inspired by King of the Hill that features a player versus player and can support up to 100-player servers. The 100 players are split into teams that fight for a 2x2 kilometre territory across a 256-square-kilometre sandbox map, and the team with the most amount of teammates in the control zone wins a point, although you can make your presence in the zone count as two people by entering the "hot zone". Every account starts with $10,000, in which you can purchase a custom loadout that ranges from weapons to gear, utility, and vehicles. According to reports, the format of Wardogs is based on a mod for the video game Arma 3 . Wardogs also features a wager-like system where players can bet on the possible outcomes; throughout matches you can find money lying around, which you can either save, buy loadouts or trade the money for gold bars. Notably, the game does not feature a battle pass or microtransactions. According to testers, you will have a bank balance at the top right corner of the screen with red and green numbers below showing your current profit or loss for that life.

Development and release

Built in Unreal Engine 5 , the start date of development for Wardogs is unknown, although it was said it took years. It was announced on 5 February 2026 by Team17 as a “Tactical All Out Warfare”. Bulkhead pitched the game to Tencent with a budget of around £10 million and £12 million, although they rejected it and said they wanted to "go bigger", in which Bulkhead made the decision to break off from Tencent and make a deal with Team17, which started the development of Wardogs . On 6 June 2026, a gameplay trailer was released to Bulkhead's YouTube channel; it was also revealed at Future Games Show Summer Showcase. During the development of Wardogs , Bulkhead laid off several workers, which directly affected QA testing. A showcase of the full game will be livestreamed to Twitch on 3 September 2026. According to the developers, they are working on Proton and Linux support for the game. The game launched into a closed beta on 22 August 2026 for people who pre-ordered it, with it releasing into early access next month on 10 September 2026.

Reception

According to Steam statistics, Wardogs reached the top of its charts at number one within hours of the early access launch, directly competing with Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 , which entered early access on the same day. It also had over 1 million Steam wishlists. According to reports, Wardogs has thousands of testers that gave very positive feedback, with some also saying performance and optimisation have exceeded expectations. Jokely, the developers posted a video to YouTube titled "Ten Reasons Not to Buy Wardogs" in which they explain what Wardogs is and to "look into it more" when purchasing it. Nearly 500 thousand players played the closed beta, and it had over 100,000 concurrent players on Steam at one point.
