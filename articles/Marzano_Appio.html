Background

Marzano Appio is a comune (municipality) in the Province of Caserta in the Italian region Campania , located about 50 kilometres (31 mi) northwest of Naples and about 35 kilometres (22 mi) northwest of Caserta . Marzano Appio borders the following municipalities: Caianello , Conca della Campania , Presenzano , Roccamonfina , Tora e Piccilli , Vairano Patenora .
