Act2.games

Act2.games is an encyclopedia entry in the Rec Room revivals collection.

Act2.games is listed by a public Rec Room build directory as a web-based project for playing older Rec Room maps, games, CV2 content, and avatars. It is presented as a community preservation utility and is not affiliated with Rec Room.
