Background

Fitzhugh is an English Anglo-Norman surname originating in Northamptonshire and Bedfordshire . It is patronymic as the prefix Fitz - derives from the Latin filius , meaning "son of". Its variants include FitzHugh , Fitz-Hugh , Fitz Hugh , fitz Hugh , and its associated given name turned surname Hugh . Fitzhugh is rare as a given name. A family with the surname of Fitzhugh were proven descendants of Acaris, son of Bardolf, a son of Odo, Count of Penthièvre who was a close relative and important ally of William the Conqueror .
