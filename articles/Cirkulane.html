Background

Cirkulane ( pronounced [tsiɾkuˈlaːnɛ] , German : Zirkulane ) is a settlement in the Haloze area of Slovenia . It is the seat of the Municipality of Cirkulane . It lies between the right bank of the Drava River and the border with Croatia . The area traditionally belonged to the region of Styria . It is now included in the Drava Statistical Region . The parish church in the village is dedicated to Saint Barbara and belongs to the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Maribor . It was built in 1684 on the site of an earlier building. A second church in the southern part of the settlement is dedicated to Saint Catherine . It was built in 1926 to replace a 13th-century building.
