Background

The 14 governorates of Syria , or muhafazat (sing. muhafazah ), are divided into 65 districts, or manatiq (sing. mintaqah ), including the city of Damascus . The districts are further divided into 281 subdistricts, or nawahi (sing. nahiya ). Each district bears the same name as its district capital. Districts and subdistricts are administered by officials appointed by the governor, subject to the approval of the minister of the interior. These officials work with elected district councils to attend to assorted local needs, and serve as intermediaries between central government authority and traditional local leaders, such as village chiefs, clan leaders, and councils of elders.

List of historical districts
