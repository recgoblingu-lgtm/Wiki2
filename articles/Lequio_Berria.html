Background

Lequio Berria is a comune (municipality) in the Province of Cuneo in the Italian region Piedmont , located about 60 kilometres (37 mi) southeast of Turin and about 50 kilometres (31 mi) northeast of Cuneo . As of 31 December 2004, it had a population of 542 and an area of 11.9 square kilometres (4.6 mi). Lequio Berria borders the following municipalities: Albaretto della Torre , Arguello , Benevello , Borgomale , Bosia , Cravanzana , and Rodello .
