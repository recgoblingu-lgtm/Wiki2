Background

In recreational mathematics , a repunit is a number like 11, 111, or 1111 that contains only the digit 1 — a more specific type of repdigit . The term stands for "repeated unit" and was coined in 1966 by Albert H. Beiler in his book Recreations in the Theory of Numbers . A repunit prime is a repunit that is also a prime number . Primes that are repunits in base-2 are Mersenne primes . As of May 2025, the largest known prime number 2 − 1 , the largest probable prime R 8177207 and the largest elliptic curve primality -proven prime R 109297 are all repunits in various bases.
