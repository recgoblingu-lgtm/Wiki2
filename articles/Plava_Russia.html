Background

The Plava ( Russian : Плава ) is a river in Tula Oblast , Russia , a right tributary of the Upa . It is 89 kilometres (55 mi) long, and has a drainage basin of 1,880 square kilometres (730 sq mi). The town of Plavsk is situated on the Plava.
