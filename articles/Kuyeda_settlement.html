Background

Kuyeda ( Russian : Куеда ) is a rural locality (a settlement) and the administrative center of Kuyedinsky District of Perm Krai , Russia. Population: 9,551 ( 2010 census ) ; 9,809 ( 2002 census ) ; 9,613 ( 1989 Soviet census ) .

History

The settlement started in 1915 as a railway station during the construction of the Kazan – Yekaterinburg line. It was named after a nearby village which, in turn, got its name from the Kuyeda River .
