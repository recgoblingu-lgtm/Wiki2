Background

Takanori Hoshino ( 星野貴紀 , Hoshino Takanori ; born 8 May 1980) is a Japanese voice actor affiliated with Production Baobab and Office PAC. He is best known for his roles as Jack Atlas in Yu-Gi-Oh! 5D's and Masayuki Kikuchi in Zipang .
