Background

Young Peak ( 69°45′S 74°31′E ﻿ / ﻿ 69.750°S 74.517°E ﻿ / -69.750; 74.517 ) is a low peak near the Antarctic coast, standing just south of Holder Peak and 2 miles (3.2 km) east of Mount Caroline Mikkelsen . First plotted from air photos taken by the Lars Christensen Expedition , 1936–37, and with Holder Peak called "Tvillingfjell" (twin mountain ) by Norwegian cartographers. This peak was named by Antarctic Names Committee of Australia (ANCA) for W. Young, officer in charge at Davis Station , 1963, who led an ANARE ( Australian National Antarctic Research Expeditions ) party that surveyed this area.
