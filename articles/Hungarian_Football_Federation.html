Hungarian Football Federation

Hungarian Football Federation is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

The Hungarian Football Federation (HFF) is the governing body of football and futsal in Hungary. It organizes the Hungarian league and the Hungary national team. The MLSZ is responsible for the Hungarian football league system, the men's and women's national teams. The headquarters are in Budapest. The MLSZ is a member of the Hungarian Olympic Committee.

Background

The Hungarian Football Federation ( HFF ) ( Hungarian : Magyar Labdarúgó Szövetség; MLSZ , pronounced [ˈmɒɟɒr ˈlɒbdɒruːɡoː ˈsøvɛt͡ʃːeːɡ] ) is the governing body of football and futsal in Hungary . It organizes the Hungarian league and the Hungary national team . The MLSZ is responsible for the Hungarian football league system , the men's and women's national teams. The headquarters are in Budapest . The MLSZ is a member of the Hungarian Olympic Committee .
