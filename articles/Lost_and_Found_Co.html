Lost and Found Co.

Lost and Found Co. is an encyclopedia entry in the Indie games collection.

Lost and Found Co. is a 2026 hidden object adventure game developed by Thai studio Bit Egg Inc. and published by Bit Egg and Gamirror Games. It was released for Windows and macOS on 6 March 2026.

Background

Lost and Found Co. is a 2026 hidden object adventure game developed by Thai studio Bit Egg Inc. and published by Bit Egg and Gamirror Games. It was released for Windows and macOS on 6 March 2026. The game follows Ducky, a duck transformed into a human by the war goddess Mei. The transformation leaves Mei weakened, and after drinking a potion she is transformed into a small dragon. Ducky then works for a lost-property company established by Mei in an attempt to regain worshippers and restore her power. Players search animated scenes for specified objects and characters, complete optional objectives, and decorate Ducky's room. The game received "universal acclaim" according to Metacritic , won the Grand Jury Award at the 2024 SEA Game Awards and Thai Game of the Year at Thai Game Fest 2026, and had sold more than 100,000 copies by August 2026.

Gameplay

Gameplay is divided into illustrated isometric stages containing large numbers of animated characters and objects. Each stage gives the player a list of objects or people to locate by examining the scene and clicking on them. Some targets only become accessible after the player interacts with objects in the environment. Stages also contain optional objectives, some of which are revealed through interactions with particular characters or objects. The main story consists of eleven chapters, each centred on a client seeking help from the Lost and Found Co. Finding the requested objects advances the client's story, and characters introduced in earlier chapters may reappear later. The game contains more than fifteen stages, which can be revisited to complete unfinished objectives or restarted. Short animated scenes between chapters advance the story. The environments also contain interactive details, with objects responding when clicked and some interactions triggering additional events. Completing objectives awards "likes", which are required to progress through the story. A hint system can direct the player towards difficult objects. Creatures called juju possess objects throughout the stages; finding them provides additional hints. In addition to the main story stages, the game includes separate requests and challenge stages. Premise

Lost and Found Co. is set in the town of Plumville and follows Ducky, a duck who is transformed into a human by Mei, an ancient war goddess whose worship has dwindled. Using her power to transform Ducky leaves Mei weakened. After drinking a potion obtained with the help of a fox named Kit, Mei is transformed into a small dragon. Because Mei's power depends on people's faith in her, she and Ducky operate a lost-property business from her shrine in an attempt to gain new worshippers and restore her power. The shrine is threatened with demolition by the corporation OKCO, led by its masked chief executive Zorro, as part of a planned property development.

Development and release

Lost and Found Co. was developed by Bit Egg Inc., an indie game studio based in Thailand, with Natachanan Lumpikanon serving as game director. According to Bit Egg, the studio selected the hidden object format in part because it could make use of its experience with Spine animation. Lumpikanon wanted the game to be accessible to people who did not regularly play video games, drawing in part on his mother's interest in hidden object games. The team sought to distinguish the game from more static examples of the genre by filling its scenes with animated characters and environmental interactions. A demo was released on Steam on 17 July 2023. The game appeared during the 2024 Wholesome Direct, where its planned platforms included computers and mobile devices. In May 2024, Gamera Games was announced as the game's publisher in mainland China, Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macao. The publisher changed its name to Gamirror Games in April 2025. The game was initially scheduled for release on 11 February 2026, but was subsequently delayed by several weeks. It was released through Steam for Windows and macOS on 6 March 2026. In 2026, Bit Egg received Thailand's Prime Minister's Export Award for Best Service Enterprise in Digital Content & Software.
