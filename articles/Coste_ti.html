Background

Costești ( Romanian pronunciation: [kosˈteʃtʲ] ) is a town in Argeș County , Muntenia , Romania . The population as of 2021 was 9,460. The town administers six villages: Broșteni, Lăceni, Pârvu Roșu, Podu Broșteni, Smei, and Stârci. The 1930 Costești wooden church fire took place here.
