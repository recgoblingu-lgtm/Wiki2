Finnie Bay

Finnie Bay is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Finnie Bay is an arm of the Foxe Basin in the Qikiqtaaluk Region of Nunavut, Canada. It is located on the northeastern Foxe Peninsula, in western Baffin Island. The area between Finnie Bay and the closest community, Kinngait, situated 118 km (73 mi) to the south, is hilly. Nuwata, a former settlement, is situated 11 km (6.8 mi) to the west.
