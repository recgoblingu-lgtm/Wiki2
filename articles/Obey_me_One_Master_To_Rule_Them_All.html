Obey me! One Master To Rule Them All

Obey me! One Master To Rule Them All is an encyclopedia entry in the Mobile games collection.

"Obey Me! " refers to a series of video games distributed by NTT Solmare. The first and most popular of the games is a smartphone application game, available for both Android and Apple. It was released globally on the 12th of December 2019. The genre is romance simulation, commonly referred to as Otome. As of January 2022, the number of downloads worldwide has exceeded 6 million.

Background

"Obey Me! " refers to a series of video games distributed by NTT Solmare. The first and most popular of the games is a smartphone application game, available for both Android and Apple. It was released globally on the 12th of December 2019. The genre is romance simulation, commonly referred to as Otome . As of January 2022, the number of downloads worldwide has exceeded 6 million .

Obey Me! Shall We Date?

The main character, which is portrayed by a Gender Neutral Sheep, is transported to Devildom by a summoning ritual to attend Devildom Academy as a exchange student for 1 year. The historic exchange program is designed to foster relations between the human world, the celestial realm, and the Devildom. The main character will be hosted by the 7 Avatars of Deadly Sin, who so happen to also be 7 gorgeous brothers. The player is challenged to navigate the story, survive the quirks and shenenigans of the brothers while simulatenously convince all 7 to make a pact with them. The characters will text the player every day and sometimes call through the app. The story is broken up though cut scenes and dance battles. Depending on choices you can raise the affection of the devil of your choosing to unlock new content. Battles are done by way of cards, which is similar to Gacha game. Each card has their own stats and stat trees. The developers are very invested in the game, and highly active on social media.
