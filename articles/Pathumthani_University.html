Pathumthani University

Pathumthani University is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Pathumthani University is a university in Pathum Thani, Thailand. Founded as Pathumthani College in 1999, it was upgraded to university status in 2005. The university is organized into seven faculties and a graduate school.

Background

Pathumthani University (Thai: มหาวิทยาลัยปทุมธานี ) is a university in Pathum Thani , Thailand. Founded as Pathumthani College in 1999, it was upgraded to university status in 2005. The university is organized into seven faculties and a graduate school.
