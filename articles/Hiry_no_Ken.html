Hiryū no Ken

Hiryū no Ken is an encyclopedia entry in the Game franchises collection.

Hiryū no Ken is a series of fighting video games dating back to 1985. They have been developed by Culture Brain and released for the arcades, NES, Super NES, Game Boy, Game Boy Color, PlayStation and Nintendo 64 platforms.

Background

Hiryū no Ken ( 飛龍の拳 , lit. "Fist of the Flying Dragon" ) is a series of fighting video games dating back to 1985. They have been developed by Culture Brain and released for the arcades , NES , Super NES , Game Boy , Game Boy Color , PlayStation and Nintendo 64 platforms.
