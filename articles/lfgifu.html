Background

Ælfgifu (also spelled Ælfgyfu ; Elfgifa, Elfgiva, Elgiva ) is an Anglo-Saxon feminine personal name , from ælf " elf " and gifu "gift". When Emma of Normandy , the mother of Edward the Confessor , became queen of England in 1002, she was given the native Anglo-Saxon name of Ælfgifu to be used in formal and official contexts.
