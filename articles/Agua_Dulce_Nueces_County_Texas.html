Background

Agua Dulce ( / ɑː w ə ˈ d u l s ɪ / ah-wə- DOOL -sih ) is a city in Nueces County , in the U.S. state of Texas . It is located on the Texas Mexican Railway at the intersection of State Highway 44 and Farm to Market Road 70 in west-central Nueces County. The name, Spanish for "sweet water", refers to a nearby creek and The Battle of Agua Dulce Creek was a major battle in the Mexican-American War. A town in Texas, Sweetwater in Nolan County in West Texas , uses the English name. The town was established by the 1900s. The population of Agua Dulce was 685 in the 2020 census .
