Background

Monte Cerignone is a comune (municipality) in the Province of Pesaro e Urbino in the Italian region Marche , located about 90 kilometres (56 mi) northwest of Ancona and about 40 kilometres (25 mi) west of Pesaro . Monte Cerignone borders the following municipalities: Macerata Feltria , Mercatino Conca , Montecopiolo , Monte Grimano , Sassocorvaro Auditore , Tavoleto . Sights include the Malatesta fortress, which was partly designed by Francesco di Giorgio Martini . The town has the head office of the coffeeshop chain Caffè Pascucci .
