Background

The Augusta, Gibson and Sandersville Railroad was incorporated in 1884 and began operating from Augusta to Gibson and Sandersville , Georgia , in 1885 and 1886, respectively. In 1893, it went bankrupt and was reorganized as the Augusta Southern Railroad . The line ran as 3 ft ( 914 mm ) narrow gauge until 1895.
