Background

Oisín ( pronounced [əˈʃiːnʲ, ˈɔʃiːnʲ] , approximately USH -een ) is an Irish male given name; meaning "fawn" or "little deer", derived from the Irish word os ("deer") + -ín (diminutive suffix). It is sometimes anglicized as Osheen ( / ɒ ˈ ʃ iː n / OSH -een ) or spelt without the acute accent ( fada ), as Oisin . Variants in other languages include Scottish Gaelic : Oisean ( pronounced [ˈɔʃan] ), Welsh : Osian and English: Ossian .
