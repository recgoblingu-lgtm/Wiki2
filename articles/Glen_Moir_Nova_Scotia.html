Background

Glen Moir is a subdivision in Bedford , Nova Scotia , Canada within the Halifax Regional Municipality , located between the Highway 102 in the west, Bedford Highway ( Trunk 2 ) in the east, Meadowbook Dr in the North and the Hammonds Plains Road in the South. It occupies about 312 ha (770 acres) of land.
