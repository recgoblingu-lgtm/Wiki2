Rudakov (volcano)

Rudakov (volcano) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Rudakov is a stratovolcano located in the central part of Urup Island, Kuril Islands, Russia. The volcano has a 700 metres (2,300 ft) wide crater which contains a 300 m (1,000 ft) wide crater lake.

Background

Rudakov ( Russian : Рудаков ) is a stratovolcano located in the central part of Urup Island, Kuril Islands , Russia . The volcano has a 700 metres (2,300 ft) wide crater which contains a 300 m (1,000 ft) wide crater lake .
