Background

Mahīdhara ("earth-bearing") was a 16th-century commentator on the Vedas . His treatises include the Mantramahodadhi ("great ocean of mantras"), written around 1588, and the Vedadipa ( veda-dīpa , "light of the Vedas"). The latter focuses on the Vajasaneyi-samhita of the White Yajurveda . Mahīdhara's name is associated with a legendary mountain described in the Mahabharata , which is also an epithet of Vishnu . Mahīdhara's commentaries belong to a period later than that of Yaska .
