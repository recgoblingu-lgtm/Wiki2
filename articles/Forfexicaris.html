Background

Forfexicaris valida is a species of Lower Cambrian arthropod , the only species in the family Forfexicarididae . It is known from only two specimens from the Maotianshan shale Lagerstätte .

Description

F. valida had a bivalved carapace , like that seen in ostracod crustaceans , which was 15 millimetres (0.59 in) long and 13 mm (0.51 in) high. It had a pair of stalked eyes, and a pair of great appendages , which closely resemble those of Occacaris .
