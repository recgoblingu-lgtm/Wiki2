Background

The Ministry of Interior ( Hebrew : משרד הפנים , romanized : Misrad HaPnim ; Arabic : وزارة الداخلية ) in the State of Israel is one of the government offices that is responsible for local government , citizenship and residency , identity cards , and student and entry visas .
