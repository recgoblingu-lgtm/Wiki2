Background

Massignano is a comune (municipality) in the Province of Ascoli Piceno in the Italian region Marche , located about 70 kilometres (43 mi) southeast of Ancona and about 30 kilometres (19 mi) northeast of Ascoli Piceno . As of 31 December 2004, it had a population of 1,621 and an area of 16.3 square kilometres (6.3 mi). Massignano borders the following municipalities: Campofilone , Cupra Marittima , Montefiore dell'Aso , Ripatransone .
