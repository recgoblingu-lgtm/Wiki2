USS Chester

USS Chester is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Two ships of the United States Navy have been named Chester, after the city of Chester, Pennsylvania.USS Chester (CL-1), was a scout cruiser in service from 1908 to 1921. USS Chester (CA-27), was a heavy cruiser commissioned in 1930, in use throughout World War II, and decommissioned in 1946.

Background

Two ships of the United States Navy have been named Chester , after the city of Chester, Pennsylvania .
