Background

Nasu ( 那須郡 , Nasu-gun ) is a district located in Tochigi Prefecture , Japan. The district consists of the two discontiguous towns of Nasu and Nakagawa , separated by the city of Ōtawara in the middle. The total area of the district was 1,209.59 km before the former constituent towns of Nishinasuno , and Shiobara were consolidated with Kuroiso to form the new city of Nasushiobara on January 1, 2005. In 2003, the district had an estimated population of 154,881 and a density of 128.04 persons per km.
