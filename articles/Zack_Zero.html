Background

Zack Zero is a 2.5D platform-adventure video game developed and published by Crocodile Entertainment via PlayStation Network on January 17, 2012 and Microsoft Windows , through Steam, on April 29, 2013. The player helps Zack Zero save his partner Marlene from Evil Zulrog who wishes to exchange her for a unique material involved in time travel called Kelestinia.
