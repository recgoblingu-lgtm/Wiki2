Background

Tooradin was a railway station on the South Gippsland railway line in South Gippsland , Victoria , Australia , the station operated until the closure of the line between Cranbourne Station and Leongatha Station in July 1993 (passenger traffic) although the railway continued to carry freight traffic to Koala Siding near Lang Lang until January 1998. The line was constructed to Tooradin in 1888.
