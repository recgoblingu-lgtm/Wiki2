Background

In mathematics , specifically in algebraic topology , the cup product is a method of adjoining two cocycles of degree and to form a composite cocycle of degree . This defines an associative (and distributive) graded commutative product operation in cohomology, turning the cohomology of a space into a graded ring , , called the cohomology ring . The cup product was introduced in work of J. W. Alexander , Eduard Čech and Hassler Whitney from 1935–1938, and, in full generality, by Samuel Eilenberg in 1944.
