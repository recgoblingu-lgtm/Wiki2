Background

The 17th Dogra Regiment was an infantry regiment of the British Indian Army . It was formed in 1922, after the Indian government decided to reform the army moving away from single battalion regiments to multi-battalion regiments. During the Second World War , soldiers from the 17th Dogras fought in Malaya (1941-1942) and Burma (1943-1945). After the partition of India in 1947, it was allocated to the Indian Army and renamed the Dogra Regiment .
