Background

Luisago ( Brianzöö : Luisagh [lyi̯ˈzaːk] ) is a comune (municipality) in the Province of Como in the Italian region Lombardy , located about 35 kilometres (22 mi) northwest of Milan and about 6 kilometres (4 mi) southwest of Como . As of 31 December 2004, it had a population of 2,532 and an area of 2.1 km (1 sq mi). Luisago borders the following municipalities: Casnate con Bernate , Cassina Rizzardi , Fino Mornasco , Grandate , Villa Guardia .
