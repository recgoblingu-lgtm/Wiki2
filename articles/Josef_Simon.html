Background

Josef Simon (1 August 1930 – 28 March 2016) was a contemporary German philosopher and professor of the University of Bonn, born in Hupperath . He wrote extensively on metaphysics , epistemology , the philosophy of German idealism and various philosophers, mainly Kant , Hamann and Nietzsche . Perhaps Simon's most influential work has been in the philosophy of language . His main work, Philosophie des Zeichens , has been influenced by, among others, Kant, Hegel , Peirce and Wittgenstein , Hamann, Humboldt or Nietzsche. He died in Bonn .
