Background

Olga Ivanovna Kotlyarova (born 12 April 1976) is a retired Russian runner. She used to compete mainly in 400 metres , and has an Olympic bronze medal from 2000 in relay . She is also a world champion (indoor and outdoor) in this event. In 2005, she concentrated more on the 800 metres distance, managing to set a new personal best and to finish fourth at the World Athletics Final . In August 2006, she became the European Champion in the 800 metres .
