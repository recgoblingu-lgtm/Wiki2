Background

Dooms is a census-designated place (CDP) in Augusta County , Virginia , United States. As of the 2020 census , Dooms had a population of 1,279. It is part of the Staunton – Waynesboro Micropolitan Statistical Area .

Geography

Dooms is located at 38°6′8″N 78°51′13″W ﻿ / ﻿ 38.10222°N 78.85361°W ﻿ / 38.10222; -78.85361 (38.102255, −78.853735). According to the United States Census Bureau , the CDP has a total area of 4.0 square miles (10 km), all land.
