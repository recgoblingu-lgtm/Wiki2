Background

Dalit Mazdoor Kisan Party ( Dalit Worker Peasant Party) was an Indian political party founded in 1984 by the remains of the Lok Dal . The DMKP was mainly based in Uttar Pradesh . It was led by former Prime Minister Charan Singh . It was founded on 21 October 1984. It was merger of Charan Singh 's Lok Dal , Democratic Socialist Party of Hemwati Nandan Bahuguna , Rashtriya Congress of Ratubhai Adani and some members of Janata Party like Devi Lal . Later it changed its name to the Lok Dal. In the 1984 Lok Sabha election , DMKP contested on 168 seats and won 3 seats.
