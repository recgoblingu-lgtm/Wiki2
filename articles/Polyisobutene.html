Background

Polyisobutene ( polyisobutylene ) is a class of organic polymers prepared by polymerization of isobutene . The polymers often have the formula Me 3 C[CH 2 CMe 2 ] n H (Me = CH 3 ). They are typically colorless gummy solids. Cationic polymerization , initiated with a strong Brønsted or Lewis acid , is the typical method for its production. The molecular weight (MW) of the resulting polymer determines the applications. Low MW polyisobutene, a mixture of oligomers with M n s of about 500, is used as plasticizers . Medium and high MW polyisobutenes, with M n ≥ 20,000, are components of commercial adhesives .
