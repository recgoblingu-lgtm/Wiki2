Background

Aa Eikō no Kōshien ( 嗚呼栄光の甲子園 ) is an arcade baseball game released by Taito in 1991. The game is played under normal baseball rules with the exception being that players can charge themselves up to attempt a better hit or pitch. The game was re-released as part of Taito Memories in 2005.
