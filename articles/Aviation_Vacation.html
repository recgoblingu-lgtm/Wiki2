Background

Aviation Vacation is a 1941 Warner Bros. Merrie Melodies theatrical short directed by Tex Avery , with story by Dave Monahan and musical direction by Carl W. Stalling . The cartoon was released on August 2, 1941. This is one of the cartoons that Warner would occasionally produce that featured none of its stable of characters, just a series of gags, usually based on outrageous stereotypes and plays on words, as a narrator (voiced by Robert C. Bruce ) describes the action.
