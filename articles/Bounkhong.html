Background

Prince Bounkhong was the last uparaja of Luang Phrabang . He was granted the title of Chao Ratsaphakhinay by King Chulalongkorn of Siam in 1884. From 1911 to 1920, he was a member of the Government Council of French Indochina . Chao Maha Oupahat Bounkhong was the father of Phetsarath , Souvanna Phouma , Souphanouvong , Souvannarath and Kindavong He died at Luang Phrabang on 26 July 1920, having had 11 sons and 13 daughters by 11 wives.
