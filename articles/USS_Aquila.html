USS Aquila

USS Aquila is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Two ships in the United States Navy have been named Aquila after the constellation Aquila.USS Aquila (AK-47) was a cargo transport in service from 1941 to 1945. USS Aquila (PHM-4) was a Pegasus-class hydrofoil launched in 1981.

Background

Two ships in the United States Navy have been named Aquila after the constellation Aquila .
