Background

Milevsko ( Czech pronunciation: [ˈmɪlɛfsko] ; German : Mühlhausen ) is a town in Písek District in the South Bohemian Region of the Czech Republic . It has about 8,000 inhabitants. It is located mostly in the Tábor Uplands . The most important monument in the town is the Milevsko Monastery.

Administrative division

Milevsko consists of six municipal parts (in brackets population according to the 2021 census): Velká forms an exclave of the municipal territory.

Etymology

The initial name of the settlement and the local monastery was Milevice. This name was a shortened form of Milejovice, derived from the personal name Milej. The adjective from Milevice was milevský , which gave rise to the name change to Milevsko.

Geography

Milevsko is located about 21 kilometres (13 mi) northeast of Písek and 62 km (39 mi) south of Prague . Most of the municipal territory lies in the Tábor Uplands , but the northern part extends into the Vlašim Uplands and includes the highest point of Milevsko, the hill Zběžnice at 608 m (1,995 ft) above sea level. The town is surrounded by several fishponds.
