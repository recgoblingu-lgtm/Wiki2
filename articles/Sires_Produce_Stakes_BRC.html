Background

The Sires' Produce Stakes is a Brisbane Racing Club Group 2 Thoroughbred horse race for two-year-olds run at set weights over 1400 metres, at Eagle Farm Racecourse , Brisbane , Australia during the Queensland Winter Racing Carnival.

History

The race was always held at Eagle Farm Racecourse until 2013 when the BRC scheduled it at Doomben Racecourse for the first time with a slightly shorter distance of 1350 metres. The following horses attained the Sires' Produce Stakes (ATC) – Sires' Produce Stakes (BRC) double: Tulloch (1957), Man of Iron (1958), Fine and Dandy (1958), Luskin Star (1977), Karaman (1978) and Zephyr Zip (1979).
