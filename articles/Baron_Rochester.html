Background

Baron Rochester , of Rochester in the County of Kent , is a title in the Peerage of the United Kingdom . It was created in 1931 for the Liberal and National Labour politician, Ernest Lamb . He served as Paymaster General from 1931 to 1935. As of 2017 the title is held by his grandson, the third Baron, who succeeded in 2017. The Hon. Tim Lamb , Chief Executive of the England and Wales Cricket Board from 1997 to 2004, is the younger son of the second Baron Rochester.
