Background

Liège Island (in English also Liege Island ) is an island, 17 kilometres (9 nmi) long and 5.6 kilometres (3 nmi) wide, lying immediately northeast of Brabant Island in the Palmer Archipelago , and separated from Hoseason Island and Christiania Islands to the northeast by Croker Passage . Its interior is occupied by Brugmann Mountains . Liège Island is located at 64°01′10″S 61°56′00″W ﻿ / ﻿ 64.01944°S 61.93333°W ﻿ / -64.01944; -61.93333 . British mapping in 1978 and 1980. The island was charted by the Belgian Antarctic Expedition (1897–1899) under Adrien de Gerlache , who named it for the province of Liège , Belgium .
