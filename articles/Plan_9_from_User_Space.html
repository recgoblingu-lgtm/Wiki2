Background

Plan 9 from User Space (also plan9port or p9p ) is a port of many Plan 9 from Bell Labs libraries and applications to Unix-like operating systems . Currently it has been tested on a variety of operating systems , including Linux , macOS , FreeBSD , NetBSD , OpenBSD , Solaris and SunOS . The project's name is a reference to the 1950s Ed Wood film Plan 9 from Outer Space . A number of key applications have been ported, as have programs used by the system itself, along with the requisite libraries from Plan 9.
