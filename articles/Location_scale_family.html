Background

In probability theory , especially in mathematical statistics , a location–scale family is a family of probability distributions parametrized by a location parameter and a non-negative scale parameter . For any random variable whose probability distribution function belongs to such a family, the distribution function of also belongs to the family (where means " equal in distribution "—that is, "has the same distribution as"). In decision theory , if all alternative distributions available to a decision-maker are in the same location–scale family, and the first two moments are finite, then a two-moment decision model can apply, and decision-making can be framed in terms of the means and the variances of the distributions.
