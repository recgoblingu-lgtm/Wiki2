Background

Roger Clavet (born February 8, 1953) is a Canadian politician from Quebec . A journalist, he was first elected to the House of Commons of Canada in the 2004 Canadian federal election . He was a member of the Bloc Québécois for the riding of Louis-Hébert . He was the Bloc's critic of Asia-Pacific. He was defeated in the 2006 federal election . Clavet was born in Quebec City , Quebec .
