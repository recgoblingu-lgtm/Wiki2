Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines

Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines is a city in southwestern Quebec, Canada, 40 kilometres (25 mi) northwest of the city of Montreal in the Thérèse-De Blainville Regional County Municipality, in the region of Laurentides. Its population was 14,990 during the census of 2014.

Background

Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines ( French pronunciation: [sɛ̃t an de plɛn] ) is a city in southwestern Quebec , Canada, 40 kilometres (25 mi) northwest of the city of Montreal in the Thérèse-De Blainville Regional County Municipality , in the region of Laurentides . Its population was 14,990 during the census of 2014. The city has the Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines Complex of the Correctional Service of Canada , which includes the Regional Reception Centre (multi-level security), housing Special Handling Unit (SHU), Canada's highest-security unit; Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines Institution (maximum security), and the Regional Mental Health Centre (multi). It also has the Archambault Institution . Sainte-Anne-Des-Plaines got city status in 1987

Climate

Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines has a warm summer humid continental climate ( Köppen climate classification Dfb ) with warm or hot summers and cold winters the record low is -41 °C (-41 °F) on January 15, 1957 The highest temperature was 36 °C (97 °F) on August 1, 1975

Demographics

In the 2021 Census of Population conducted by Statistics Canada , Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines had a population of 15,221 living in 5,882 of its 6,005 total private dwellings, a change of 5.5% from its 2016 population of 14,421 . With a land area of 93.44 km (36.08 sq mi), it had a population density of 162.9/km (421.9/sq mi) in 2021.

Education

The Commission scolaire de la Seigneurie-des-Mille-Îles (CSSMI) operates French-language schools. Sir Wilfrid Laurier School Board operates the English-language public schools.
