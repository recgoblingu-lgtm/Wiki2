Background

Yabuki ( 矢吹町 , Yabuki-machi ) is a town located in Fukushima Prefecture , Japan. As of 1 February 2020, the town had an estimated population of 16,955 in 6051 households, and a population density of 280 persons per km. The total area of the town was 60.4 square kilometres (23.3 sq mi).

Geography

Yabuki is located in the flatlands of south-central Fukushima prefecture, approximately 212 kilometers north of Tokyo.

Demographics

Per Japanese census data, the population of Yabuki has remained relatively stable over the past 40 years.

Climate

Yabuki has a humid climate ( Köppen climate classification Cfa ). The average annual temperature in Yabuki is 11.7 °C (53.1 °F). The average annual rainfall is 1,312 mm (51.7 in) with September as the wettest month. The temperatures are highest on average in August, at around 24.2 °C (75.6 °F), and lowest in January, at around 0.32 °C (32.58 °F).
