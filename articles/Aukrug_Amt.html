Background

Aukrug is a former Amt ("collective municipality") in the district of Rendsburg-Eckernförde , in Schleswig-Holstein , Germany . The seat of the Amt is in Aukrug. It was disbanded on 1 January 2012 along with the Hanerau-Hademarschen , the Amt Hohenwestedt-Land and Hohenwestedt to become Mittelholstein .
