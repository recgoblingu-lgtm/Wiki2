Przemyśl Voivodeship was a unit of administrative division and local government in Poland that existed from 1975 to 1998, superseded by the Podkarpackie Voivodeship. Its capital city was Przemyśl.

Background

Przemyśl Voivodeship ( Polish : województwo przemyskie ) was a unit of administrative division and local government in Poland that existed from 1975 to 1998, superseded by the Podkarpackie Voivodeship . Its capital city was Przemyśl .
