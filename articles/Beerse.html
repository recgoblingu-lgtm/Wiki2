Background

Beerse ( Dutch pronunciation: [ˈbeːrsə] ) is a municipality located in the Belgian province of Antwerp . The municipality comprises the towns of Beerse proper and Vlimmeren . In 2024, Beerse had a total population of 18,601. [1] The total area is 37.48 km (14.47 sq mi).
