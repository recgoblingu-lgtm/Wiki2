The arrondissement of Villeneuve-sur-Lot is an arrondissement of France in the Lot-et-Garonne department in the Nouvelle-Aquitaine région. It has 92 communes. Its population is 89,282 (2021), and its area is 1,559.7 km2 (602.2 sq mi).
