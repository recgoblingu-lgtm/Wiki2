Background

Maa Kheru ( Ancient Egyptian : mꜣꜥ ḫrw ) is a phrase meaning "true of voice" or "justified" or "the acclaim given to him is right". The term is involved in ancient Egyptian afterlife beliefs , according to which deceased souls had to be judged morally righteous. Once the soul had passed the test, the Weighing of the Heart , they were judged to be mꜣꜥ ḫrw and allowed to enter the afterlife . The phrase was often used to denote someone who had died and become a god, placed after the name of the individual in question. As such, it is frequently found in inscriptions in Egyptian tombs and royal mortuary temples , especially as part of an introductory clause for autobiographical inscriptions celebrating the tomb or temple owner's achievements in life.
