Ambartsumian

Ambartsumian is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Ambartsumian, Ambartsumyan, Hambardzumyan, Hambardzumian, Hambartsumyan or Hambartsumian is an Armenian surname. In Armenian onomastics, it is a religious surname derived from 'Hambardzum' meaning Ascension of Jesus. The suffix '-yan' denotes surname. The variants beginning without H originated in the USSR as Russian lacks the sound H.
