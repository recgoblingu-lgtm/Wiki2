Background

Hands-On Mobile , formerly known as Mforma , is a wireless entertainment company established in 2000. The company develops, publishes and distributes mobile content via network operators and portals. It offers its content in or via Java ME , BREW , SMS , MMS , and WAP in English, French, Italian, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Czech, Chinese and Korean. Hands-On Mobile is a private company headquartered in San Francisco, California. It has offices in San Diego , London and Manchester (UK), Madrid (Spain), Paris (France), Munich (Germany), Beijing and Shanghai (China), and São Paulo (Brazil). Investors include eFund International, Draper Fisher Jurvetson , General Catalyst Partners, Institutional Venture Partners and Bessemer Venture Partners .

History

Mforma was founded in 2000. The company changed its name to Hands-On Mobile in April 2006.
