Background

Thunder Lake Provincial Park is a provincial park located in Alberta , Canada , and is located 140 kilometres (87 mi) west of Edmonton , on the shore of the park features setting for boating , water-skiing , swimming , running , fishing and camping . Thunder lake is approximately 21 kilometres (13 mi) West of the nearest town, Barrhead . The park is located on the north-western shore of Thunder Lake . The lake itself has a total water surface of 7.03 km (2.71 sq mi) and reaches a maximum depth of 6.1 m (20 ft). The main fish found are Northern Pike .
