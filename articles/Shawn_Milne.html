Background

Shawn Milne (born November 9, 1981) is a professional road bicycle racer from Gloucester, Massachusetts , United States . In 2009, he rode in events on the USA Cycling National Racing Calendar and UCI Continental Circuits for Team Type 1 . On May 31, 2009, he won the Air Force Cycling Classic. He was selected to represent the United States at the 2002 and 2003 Under 23 World Road Race Championships. Milne was a member of the 2000 Gloucester High School (Massachusetts) national champion and national record-setting indoor track distance medley relay team along with Josh Palazola, Tristan Colangelo and Ngai Otieno.
