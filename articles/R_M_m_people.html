Rơ Măm people

Rơ Măm people is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

The Rmam people are a small ethnic group in Vietnam. They speak a language in the Central Bahnaric branch of the Mon–Khmer family. They mostly reside in Le Village, Mo Rai Commune, Kon Tum. Hunting, gathering, agriculture, and weaving are the main sources of wealth in current Ro Mam society.

Background

The Rmam people are a small ethnic group in Vietnam (639 in 2019). They speak a language in the Central Bahnaric branch of the Mon–Khmer family. They mostly reside in Le Village, Mo Rai Commune, Kon Tum . Hunting, gathering, agriculture, and weaving are the main sources of wealth in current Ro Mam society.
