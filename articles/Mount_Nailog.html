Mount Nailog

Mount Nailog is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Mount Nailog is one of the two prominent peaks of Sibuyan Island, Philippines with a height of 789 metres (2,589 ft) and a topographic prominence of 464 metres (1,522 ft). The highest peak on the island is Mount Guiting-Guiting, with an elevation of 2,037 metres (6,683 ft).
