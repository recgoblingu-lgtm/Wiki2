Réda Abdenouz is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Réda Abdenouz is a retired Algerian middle-distance runner who specialized in the 800 metres. He was a finalist at the 1992 Summer Olympics, twice a medallist at the Mediterranean Games and represented his country at two editions of the World Athletics Championships.

Background

Réda Abdenouz ( Arabic : رضا عبد النوز ; born 25 September 1968) is a retired Algerian middle-distance runner who specialized in the 800 metres . He was a finalist at the 1992 Summer Olympics , twice a medallist at the Mediterranean Games and represented his country at two editions of the World Athletics Championships .
