Background

435 Ella is a typical Main belt asteroid . It was discovered by Max Wolf and A. Schwassmann on 11 September 1898 in Heidelberg . This is the eponymous member of a proposed asteroid family with at least 15 members. Photometric observations during 1995 show a rotation period of 4.264 hours. 435 Ella is classified as a DCX-type asteroid.
