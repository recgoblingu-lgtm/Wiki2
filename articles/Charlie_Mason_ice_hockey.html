Charlie Mason (ice hockey)

Charlie Mason (ice hockey) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Charles Charters Mason was a Canadian professional ice hockey right winger who played 93 games with the New York Rangers, New York Americans, Detroit Red Wings, and Chicago Black Hawks in the National Hockey League between 1934 and 1939.

Background

Charles Charters Mason (February 1, 1912 – May 18, 1971) was a Canadian professional ice hockey right winger who played 93 games with the New York Rangers , New York Americans , Detroit Red Wings , and Chicago Black Hawks in the National Hockey League between 1934 and 1939. He was born in Seaforth , Ontario .
