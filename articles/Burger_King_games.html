Background

The Burger King games , sometimes known collectively as King Games , are a series of three advertisement-based video games ( advergames ) sold at Burger King . The games were sold as part of a promotion during the holiday season from late November until December 24, 2006. All three games were developed by the Blitz Arcade Division of Blitz Games , and were published by King Games . They advertise Burger King in-game. The games were compatible with both the original Xbox and the Xbox 360 , and feature online play through Xbox Live . The games were originally created as downloadable games for Xbox Live Arcade but were considered to be of such high quality they were instead moved to 'box product' and distributed directly at Burger King stores for $3.99 apiece.
