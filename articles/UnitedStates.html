United States

The United States of America is a country located primarily in North America. It consists of 50 states, a federal district, and several territories.

Overview

The United States is one of the largest countries in the world by both area and population. It is known for its cultural influence, technological development, and economic power.

The capital city is Washington, D.C., and the largest city is New York City.

Government

The United States is a federal republic with a constitution that divides power between the national government and individual states.

It has three branches of government:

  • Executive (President)
  • Legislative (Congress)
  • Judicial (Supreme Court)

Culture

The United States has a diverse culture influenced by immigration from many countries. It is known for music, film, sports, and technology industries.

Geography

The country includes a wide variety of landscapes, including mountains, forests, deserts, and coastlines. It spans multiple climate zones.
