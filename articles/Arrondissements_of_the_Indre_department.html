Arrondissements of the Indre department

Arrondissements of the Indre department is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

The 4 arrondissements of the Indre department are:Arrondissement of Le Blanc, with 57 communes. The population of the arrondissement was 30,501 in 2021. Arrondissement of Châteauroux, with 84 communes. The population of the arrondissement was 126,377 in 2021. Arrondissement of La Châtre, with 51 communes. The population of the arrondissement was 27,656 in 2021. Arrondissement of Issoudun, with 49 communes. The population of the arrondissement was 32,694 in 2021.

History
