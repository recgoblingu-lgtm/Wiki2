Background

Magistrats Européens pour la Démocratie et les Libertés ( MEDEL ; English: European Judges for Democracy and Liberty ) is a European association of judges and public prosecutors , set up in 1985 to promote a progressive vision of justice in Europe. MEDEL has 22 member organizations from 13 European countries. MEDEL holds observer status with the Council of Europe , and participates in activities of the Consultative Council of European Judges (CCJE).
