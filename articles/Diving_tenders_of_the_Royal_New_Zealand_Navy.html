Background

Diving tenders of the Royal New Zealand Navy from its formation on 1 October 1941 to the present. There have been four diving tenders, all with the same name. The latest tender was commissioned in 2019 and it sunk in 2024. Manawanui is a Māori word meaning "to be brave or steadfast".
