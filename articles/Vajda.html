Vajda

Vajda is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Vajda is a Hungarian language surname. It is derived from Proto-Slavic *vojevoda. In medieval times, vajda was the equivalent of voivode, meaning a "war-leader" or "war-lord".
