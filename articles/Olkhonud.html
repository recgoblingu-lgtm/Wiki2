Olkhonud

Olkhonud is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Olkhonud, also rendered as Olqunuut, was the clan of Hoelun, the mother of Genghis Khan. They helped Genghis to defeat the Naimans. The Olkhunut people were very closely related to the Hongirad tribe. Their descendants still live in Khovd Province of Mongolia and in Inner Mongolia of China.

Background

Olkhonud ( Mongolian : Олхуноуд, Олхонууд, Олгонууд ; Chinese : 斡勒忽讷 ; pinyin : wòlēihūnè ), also rendered as Olqunuut , was the clan of Hoelun , the mother of Genghis Khan . They helped Genghis to defeat the Naimans . The Olkhunut people were very closely related to the Hongirad tribe . Their descendants still live in Khovd Province of Mongolia and in Inner Mongolia of China .
