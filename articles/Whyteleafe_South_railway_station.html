Background

Whyteleafe South railway station serves part of the suburban village of Whyteleafe in the district of Tandridge , Surrey , England . The station and all trains serving it are operated by Southern , and it is on the Caterham Line 18 miles 18 chains (29.3 km) from Charing Cross . There are three railway stations in the village, the others being Whyteleafe , and Upper Warlingham on the Oxted Line . This station was named Warlingham until 11 June 1956; its single-storey main buildings are on the Down side. The narrow gabled station house still exists.
