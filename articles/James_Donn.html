Background

James Donn (1758–1813) was an English botanist and gardener. He was trained by William Aiton , a protege of Sir Joseph Banks and was Curator of the Cambridge University Botanic Garden , Cambridge , from 1790 until his death. His most important work was Hortus Cantabrigiensis , first published in 1796 but with several later, much expanded, editions. It carried on past his death until 1845. A copy was given to the Library of the Gray Herbarium of Harvard University in 1895. He became a Fellow of the Linnean Society in 1812. A memorial to James Donn, exists on St Edward the Martyr's church in Cambridge. A grandson was the English composer William Sterndale Bennett .
