Background

Town to City is a 2026 city-building video game developed by Dutch studio Galaxy Grove and published by British publisher Kwalee. Set in a stylised, Mediterranean -inspired version of the 19th century, the game emphasizes a gridless building system and a relaxed aesthetic. It was released in early access for Microsoft Windows on September 16, 2025 via Steam . The full release arrived on May 26, 2026.

Gameplay

Town to City is a city-building game with both a guided story/campaign mode and a sandbox mode. The campaign introduces systems through progressively unlocked objectives, while sandbox play can reduce or disable some constraints (such as happiness or money) to focus on creativity and town beautification. A central feature is its gridless placement system, allowing roads, buildings, and decorations to be placed freely to create organic layouts with winding streets and plazas. Players expand housing and services to attract new residents and meet individual and class-based needs through amenities, employment, and infrastructure. Decorative tools (such as lighting, flowers, trees, and parks) and an in-game photo mode support the game's presentation-focused playstyle. As population grows, players can develop multiple towns and connect them through regional growth and trade systems, with additional economic elements such as farming and tourism.

Development and release

Town to City was announced on 30 January 2025 by Kwalee and Galaxy Grove alongside an announcement trailer; Galaxy Grove founder Joost van Dongen described the project as an attempt to reimagine aspects of the city-building genre. A playable demo was made available during Steam Next Fest in June 2025. In late August 2025, the publisher confirmed the early access release date as 16 September 2025. The game subsequently launched in early access on Steam for Windows on that date.

Reception

DLCompare described the game as emphasizing creative freedom and a low-pressure management loop, with campaign guidance and a more open-ended sandbox mode.
