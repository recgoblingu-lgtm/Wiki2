Background

Flinders Group National Park (Cape York Peninsula Aboriginal Land) is a national park in Queensland (Australia), 1,745 kilometres (1,084 mi) northwest of Brisbane . The national park was previously named Flinders Group National Park until it was renamed on 28 November 2013. This national park has a tropical climate, with a humid season from December to April, when temperatures exceed 30 °C (86 °F). Many species of land and sea birds are represented here.
