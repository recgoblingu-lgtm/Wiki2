Vindhyachal division

Vindhyachal division is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Vindhyachal division, also sometimes known as Mirzapur division, is one of the 18 administrative geographical units of the northern Indian state of Uttar Pradesh. Mirzapur city is the administrative headquarters. As of 2018, the division consists of 3 districts:-Bhadohi Mirzapur Sonbhadra
