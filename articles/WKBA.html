Background

WKBA (1550 AM , "Classic Country 93.1") is a daytime only broadcast radio station licensed to Vinton, Virginia , serving Roanoke and the Roanoke Valley in Virginia . WKBA is owned and operated by Brian Sizer, through licensee Backroads Radio, LLC. It mostly simulcasts WBRF in Galax, Virginia . Previously, WKBA was a religious station known as "The Ministry Station".
