Background

Songavazzo ( Bergamasque : Songaàss ) is a comune (municipality) in the Province of Bergamo in the Italian region of Lombardy , located about 80 kilometres (50 mi) northeast of Milan and about 30 kilometres (19 mi) northeast of Bergamo . As of 31 December 2004, it had a population of 662 and an area of 12.7 square kilometres (4.9 mi). Songavazzo borders the following municipalities: Bossico , Castione della Presolana , Cerete , Costa Volpino , Fino del Monte , Onore , Rogno , Rovetta .
