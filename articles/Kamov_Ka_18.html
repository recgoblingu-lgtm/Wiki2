Kamov Ka-18

Kamov Ka-18 is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

The Kamov Ka-18 was a Soviet four-seat utility helicopter that first flew in 1955. It was a development of the Kamov Ka-15, with a lengthened fuselage and a more powerful engine - the 190 kW (255 hp) Ivchenko AI-14V radial. Total production from Kamov was about 120.

Background

The Kamov Ka-18 ( NATO reporting name Hog ) was a Soviet four-seat utility helicopter that first flew in 1955. It was a development of the Kamov Ka-15 , with a lengthened fuselage and a more powerful engine - the 190 kW (255 hp) Ivchenko AI-14V radial. Total production from Kamov was about 120.
