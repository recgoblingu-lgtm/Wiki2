Background

Nikolay Nikolayevich Gusakov ( Russian : Никола́й Гусако́в ; May 14, 1934 – December 14, 1991) was a former Soviet Nordic combined skier who won a bronze in the individual event at the 1960 Winter Olympics in Squaw Valley . He also won the Nordic combined event at the Holmenkollen ski festival in 1961. Gusakov trained at the Armed Forces sports society in Moscow and later in Leningrad . He was the first Soviet athlete to win an Olympic medal in the Nordic combined and the first one to win the Nordic combined event at the Holmenkollen ski festival .
