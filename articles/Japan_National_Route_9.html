Background

National Route 9 ( 国道9号 , Kokudō kyū-gō ) is an important highway in the Kansai and Chūgoku regions. It connects the prefectural capitals of Kyoto , Tottori , Matsue ( Shimane Prefecture ), and Yamaguchi . Other significant cities along the route include Yonago, Tottori and Shimonoseki . National Route 9 also passes through parts of Hyōgo Prefecture . With a total length of 638.4 kilometers (396.7 mi), National Route 9 is the second longest national highway in Japan, being shorter than only National Route 4 .
