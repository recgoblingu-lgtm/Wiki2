World Collection

World Collection is an encyclopedia entry in the Game franchises collection.

World Collection is a series of Japanese releases for original Xbox games published by Microsoft, featuring select overseas titles, released at a reduced price compared to normal releases. Titles in this series retain their original overseas cover artwork with a "World Collection" border, and are left entirely in English, with a Japanese-language manual. The series was launched in October 2003, with regular releases every 2-3 months until August 2005.

Background

World Collection is a series of Japanese releases for original Xbox games published by Microsoft , featuring select overseas ( North American and European ) titles, released at a reduced price compared to normal releases. Titles in this series retain their original overseas cover artwork with a "World Collection" border, and are left entirely in English , with a Japanese-language manual. The series was launched in October 2003, with regular releases every 2-3 months until August 2005.
