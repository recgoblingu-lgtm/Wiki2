Background

Bassington is a former civil parish , now in the parish of Eglingham , in the county of Northumberland , England. It is located about 3 miles (5 km) northwest of Alnwick . Bassington is traversed by the River Aln . In 1951 the parish had a population of 2.

Governance

Bassington is in the parliamentary constituency of Berwick-upon-Tweed . Bassington was formerly a township in Eglingham parish, from 1866 Bassington was a civil parish in its own right until it was abolished on 1 April 1955 and merged with Eglingham.
