Background

Bornem ( Dutch pronunciation: [ˈbɔrnəm] ; old spelling: Bornhem ) is a municipality located in the Belgian province of Antwerp . The municipality comprises the village of Bornem proper, Hingene , Mariekerke and Weert , and Wintam [ nl ] . There are also the hamlets of Branst , Buitenland , Eikevliet and Wintam .
