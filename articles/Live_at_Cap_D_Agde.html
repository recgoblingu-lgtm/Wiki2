Live at Cap D'Agde

Live at Cap D'Agde is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Live at Cap D'Agde is a live album by the band King Crimson, released through the King Crimson Collector's Club in April 1999.Tracks 1–6 were recorded at the Arena, Cap d'Agde, France on August 26, 1982. Tracks 7–9 were recorded at the Arena, Fréjus, France on August 27, 1982. The video of the complete performance was issued on VHS as The Noise and on DVD as part of Neal and Jack and Me.

Background

Live at Cap D'Agde is a live album by the band King Crimson , released through the King Crimson Collector's Club in April 1999. The album includes liner notes by Robert Fripp , one of the band's two guitarists.

Track listing

All songs written by Adrian Belew , Robert Fripp , Tony Levin and Bill Bruford , except track 9 which is written by Robert Fripp.
