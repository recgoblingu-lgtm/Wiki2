Background

John Anthony Michael Parrish (known as Michael Parrish ) was the Chairman of the Brentwood and Ongar Conservative Association during the split in the local party over the influence of the Peniel Pentecostal Church . Parrish is married with two children. Parrish stood as a Conservative candidate in the 2002 Brentwood Borough Council elections . Parrish owns the 'Secret Nuclear Bunker', a local joke, at Kelvedon Hatch , and owns farmland in the Navestock area.
