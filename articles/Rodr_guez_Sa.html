Rodríguez Saá

Rodríguez Saá is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Rodríguez Saá is the surname of a powerful family of the San Luis Province in Argentina, whose members include:Adolfo Rodríguez Saá (elder), governor of the province (1909–12) Adolfo Rodríguez Saá, former senator and governor of the province and interim president of Argentina for a few days. Alberto Rodríguez Saá, former senator and current governor of the province. Nicolás Rodríguez Saá, national deputy (2019–21). Ricardo Rodríguez Saá, governor of the province (1934–38).
