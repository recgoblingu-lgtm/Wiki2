Computer

A computer is an electronic device that processes data and performs tasks according to a set of instructions called programs.

Overview

Computers can take many forms, from large servers used in data centers to small portable devices like laptops and smartphones. They are used for communication, education, entertainment, and work.

Modern computers are capable of performing billions of calculations per second.

Types of Computers

  • Desktop computers – Stationary computers typically used at a desk.
  • Laptops – Portable computers with built-in screen and keyboard.
  • Servers – Powerful computers that provide services over a network.
  • Mobile devices – Smartphones and tablets used for portable computing.

History

Early computers were large mechanical machines used for calculations. Over time, they evolved into electronic systems using transistors and microchips.

The development of the microprocessor in the 1970s made personal computers widely available.

Role in Society

Computers play a major role in modern life, powering communication systems, businesses, scientific research, and entertainment platforms.
