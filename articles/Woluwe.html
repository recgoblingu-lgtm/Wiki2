Background

The Woluwe ( French: [wolywe] ; Dutch: [ˈʋoːlyu.ə] ) is a stream that flows through several municipalities in the south-east and east of Brussels , Belgium, and is a right tributary of the Senne in Vilvoorde . The Kleine (little) Maalbeek is a tributary of the Woluwe in Kraainem . Many ponds formed along the stream over time, among which the Mellaerts Ponds still exist. The valley of the Woluwe crosses the municipalities of Auderghem , Watermael-Boitsfort , Woluwe-Saint-Pierre , Woluwe-Saint-Lambert , Kraainem , Zaventem , Machelen and Vilvoorde . The towns of Woluwe-Saint-Pierre, Woluwe-Saint-Lambert and Sint-Stevens-Woluwe derive their name from it.
