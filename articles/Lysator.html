Background

Lysator is an academic computer club at Linköping University , Sweden with almost 600 members. It is an independent non-profit society, separate from the students' union and the faculties of the university .

History

Lysator was founded on 29 March 1973. The first computer used at Lysator was a Datasaab D21, delivered to Lysator on 25 May 1973. Later in the decade, members of Lysator developed and initially built a microcomputer , the LYS-16 , which was advanced for its time due to its 16- bit word size. In February 1993, Lysator put up the first web server in Sweden, among the first 10–15 in the world. On 30 July 2010, Lysator began migrating to a new 3 U home rack , increasing their available storage space from 700 GB to 13 TB .
