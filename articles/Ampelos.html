Background

Ampelos ( Ancient Greek : Ἂμπελος , lit. " Vine ") or Ampelus ( Latin ) was a personification of the grapevine and lover of Dionysus in Greek and Bacchus in Roman mythology . He was a satyr that Dionysus either turned into a constellation or the grape vine.

Nonnus

In Nonnus 's etiology , Ampelos is a beautiful satyr youth, who was loved by Dionysus , and whose death was foreseen by the god. There are two versions of his death and Dionysus's reaction to it.
