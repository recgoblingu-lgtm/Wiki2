Background

The Arena Marcel Bedard is a multi-purpose arena in Beauport , Quebec . It has a capacity of 2,000 people. It hosted the Beauport Harfangs ice hockey team. Its surname is 'La petite cabane' (the little shack) in honour of Beauport's colourful coach, Jos Canale .
