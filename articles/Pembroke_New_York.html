Background

Pembroke ( Seneca : Oà:’geh ) is a town in Genesee County , Western New York , United States. The population was 4,292 at the 2010 census. The town is named after a town in west Wales . Pembroke lies on the west border of Genesee County, west of Batavia .

History

Pembroke was first settled in 1804 by David Goss. Pembroke was established from part of the town of Batavia in 1812.

Geography

According to the United States Census Bureau , the town has a total area of 41.7 square miles (108 km), of which 41.7 square miles (108 km) is land and 0.02% is water. The New York State Thruway ( Interstate 90 ) passes through Pembroke, and New York State Route 5 intersects New York State Route 77 in the town. Tonawanda Creek and its tributary Murder Creek flow through the town. The west town line is the border of Erie County .

Notable places

The Western New York National Cemetery is located in Pembroke. As of 2016, the first burial is expected to take place by the end of the decade.
