Goddamn (EP)

Goddamn (EP) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Goddamn is the second EP released by You Am I in May 1992. The EP was You Am I's first release with bassist Andy Kent who was previously a mixer for the band. It was You Am I's last release on Timberyard Records as they were then signed to Ra Records, a sub-branch of rooArt Records.

Background

Goddamn is the second EP released by You Am I in May 1992. The EP was You Am I's first release with bassist Andy Kent who was previously a mixer for the band. It was You Am I's last release on Timberyard Records as they were then signed to Ra Records, a sub-branch of rooArt Records .

Track listing

All songs: Rogers/You Am I Tracks 6–9 were previously available on You Am I's Snake Tide EP which was first released on vinyl in 1991. On these tracks, Nik Tischler plays bass guitar.
