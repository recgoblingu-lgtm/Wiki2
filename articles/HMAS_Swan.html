HMAS Swan

HMAS Swan is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Three ships of the Royal Australian Navy (RAN) have been named HMAS Swan, for the Swan River in Western Australia.HMAS Swan (D61), a River-class destroyer launched in 1915, decommissioned in 1928, and broken up for scrap HMAS Swan (U74), a Grimsby-class sloop launched in 1936, decommissioned in 1962, and broken up for scrap HMAS Swan , a River-class destroyer escort launched in 1967, decommissioned in 1996, and scuttled as a dive wreck in 1997
