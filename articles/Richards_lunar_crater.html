Background

Richards is a small lunar impact crater that is located in the northern interior of the walled plain named Mendeleev , on the far side of the Moon . It lies about half-way between the craters Bergman to the west-southwest and Fischer to the east, both also within Mendeleev's interior. This is a circular, cup-shaped crater with a small interior floor at the midpoint of the sloping inner walls. The interior sides have a higher albedo than the surroundings. The infrared spectrum of pure crystalline plagioclase has been identified inside the crater walls and on the rim. There is a small crater situated along the north-northeastern edge of the rim. The crater chain named Catena Mendeleev passes just to the west of Richards, continuing in a line of small craters from the southwestern edge of Mendeleev.
