M898 motorway

M898 motorway is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

The M898 motorway is a 1-mile-long (1.6 km) motorway in Renfrewshire, Scotland. It is a spur route from the M8 motorway towards the Erskine Bridge. It lacks hard shoulders along its length. Its northern terminus comes at the junction with the A726 road, the former location of the toll booths for the Erskine Bridge, which were removed on 1 April 2006.
