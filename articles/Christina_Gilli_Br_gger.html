Background

Christina Gilli-Brügger (born 3 July 1956) is a Swiss cross-country skier who competed from 1984 to 1988. She finished fourth both in the 4 × 5 km relay and in the 20 km events at the 1988 Winter Olympics in Calgary . At the 1984 Winter Olympics in Sarajevo , she finished sixth in the 4 × 5 km relay. Gilli-Brügger's best finish at the FIS Nordic World Ski Championships was fifth in the 20 km event at Oberstdorf in 1987 . Her best World Cup finish was ninth twice (1986, 1988).
