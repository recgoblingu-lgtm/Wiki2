Background

Malagnino ( Cremunés : Malagnéen ) is a comune (municipality) in the Province of Cremona in the Italian region Lombardy , located about 80 kilometres (50 mi) southeast of Milan and about 7 kilometres (4 mi) east of Cremona . Malagnino borders the following municipalities: Bonemerse , Cremona , Gadesco-Pieve Delmona , Pieve d'Olmi , Sospiro , Vescovato .
