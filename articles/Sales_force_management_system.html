Sales force management system

Sales force management system is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Sales force management systems are information systems used in customer relationship management (CRM) marketing and management that help automate some sales and sales force management functions. They are often combined with a marketing information system, in which case they are often called CRM systems.

Background

Sales force management systems (also sales force automation ( SFA ) systems ) are information systems used in customer relationship management (CRM) marketing and management that help automate some sales and sales force management functions. They are often combined with a marketing information system , in which case they are often called CRM systems .
