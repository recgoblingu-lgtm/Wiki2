Kard Combat

Kard Combat is an encyclopedia entry in the Indie games collection.

Kard Combat is a 2011 card game developed and published by the Canadian indie studio Hothead Games. Released for iOS on July 13, 2011, it was co-designed by Richard Garfield, the game designer of the card game Magic: The Gathering.

Background

Kard Combat is a 2011 card game developed and published by the Canadian indie studio Hothead Games . Released for iOS on July 13, 2011, it was co-designed by Richard Garfield , the game designer of the card game Magic: The Gathering .

Reception

Kard Combat received "generally favorable" reviews according to the review aggregation website Metacritic . The game was positively received.
