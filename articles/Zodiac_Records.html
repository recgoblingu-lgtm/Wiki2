Zodiac Records

Zodiac Records is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Zodiac Records has been the name of at least six different record labels:Zodiac Records, an Auckland, New Zealand label of Stebbing Recording and Sound Zodiac Records, a New York City, United States company in the 1950s Zodiac Records, a Chicago-based company in the late 1960s, best known for their key artist Ruby Andrews and her trademark hit "Casanova ". Zodiac Records, a New York City, United States company founded in 1987 and revived in 2006 Zodiac Records, a Netherlands label in the 1990s Zodiac Records, an Arogno, Switzerland company in the early 2000s
