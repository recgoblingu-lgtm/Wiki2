Pedubastis is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Pedubastis or Petubastis, are Hellenized forms of the ancient Egyptian personal theophoric name Padibastet (P3-dj-Bstt), meaning 'Given by Bastet'. Notable bearers were:Pedubast I, a pharaoh of the 23rd Dynasty Pedubast II, a ruler of Tanis Pedubast III, a rebel pharaoh during the 27th Dynasty Pedubast, a high steward during the 26th Dynasty
