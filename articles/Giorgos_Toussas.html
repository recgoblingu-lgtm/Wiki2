Background

Giorgos Toussas ( Greek : Γιώργος Τούσσας ) (born 8 September 1954) is a Greek politician and Member of the European Parliament (MEP) for the Communist Party of Greece ; part of the European United Left–Nordic Green Left . He was born in Koilada .
