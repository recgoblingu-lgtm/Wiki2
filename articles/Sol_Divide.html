Sol Divide

Sol Divide is an encyclopedia entry in the Classic games collection.

Sol Divide is a scrolling shooter developed by Psikyo and released as an arcade video game in 1997. Ports were published for the PlayStation, Sega Saturn, and later PlayStation 2, Microsoft Windows, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 4, and Xbox One.

Background

Sol Divide is a scrolling shooter developed by Psikyo and released as an arcade video game in 1997. Ports were published for the PlayStation , Sega Saturn , and later PlayStation 2 , Microsoft Windows , Nintendo Switch , PlayStation 4 , and Xbox One .

Release

Sol Divide was released on February 24, 2005 for the PlayStation 2. It was released for Nintendo Switch on March 22, 2018.
