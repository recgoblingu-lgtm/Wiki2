Background

The Canon AT-1 is a 35mm FD-mount single-lens reflex camera manufactured by Canon of Japan from December 1976. It was produced purely for export and was never sold in the home Japanese market. It is a version of the popular AE-1 but without the shutter-speed priority auto-exposure mode of that camera. The AT-1 features manual exposure only. This made the camera cheaper. A light meter is included, featuring TTL center-weighted average metering with a CdS photocell , and a match needle in the viewfinder . The accessories produced for the AE-1 also work with the AT-1, including motor drives. Similar to the AE-1 and AE-1 program camera, it has a built-in light meter. The aperture settings are in the lens.
