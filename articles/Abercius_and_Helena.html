Background

Abercius and Helena are saints of the Catholic church . They are said to have been the children of Alphaeus the Apostle, although this has been challenged by some parties. Both of them are known to have been martyrs : Abercius by being exposed naked to bees , and Helena by stoning . They are commemorated with a feast day on May 20. They are commemorated in the Orthodox Church on May 26.
