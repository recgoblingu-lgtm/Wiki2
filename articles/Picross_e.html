Background

Picross e is a series of nonogram puzzle video games developed and published by Jupiter for the Nintendo 3DS handheld game console . It is the successor to Jupiter's Nintendo-published Picross games including the Mario's Picross series and Picross DS . It was later succeeded by the Picross S series. On 29 February 2024, a year after the discontinuation of the Nintendo 3DS's Nintendo eShop , Jupiter released Picross S+ , a compilation of every puzzle previously included in the Picross e series, with the first game's puzzles being included in the base game, and the rest being sold as separate downloadable content .

Games

The puzzles of the Picross e series were later re-released as part of Picross S+ for Nintendo Switch .
