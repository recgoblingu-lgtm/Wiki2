Background

Marataízes is a municipality located by the Atlantic Ocean in southern Espírito Santo , Brazil. It has a population of 38,883 and holds as such the highest density in the state. Marataízes is a tourist city . The municipality contains the 42.20 hectares (104.3 acres) Falésias de Marataízes Natural Monument , created in 2008 to protect the cliffs to the south of the town.
