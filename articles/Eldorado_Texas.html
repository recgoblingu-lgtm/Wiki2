Eldorado, Texas

Eldorado, Texas is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Eldorado is the only city in and the county seat of Schleicher County, Texas, United States. The population was 1,574 at the 2020 census. Eldorado is located on U.S. Highway 277, some 21 miles (34 km) north of Sonora and 43 miles (69 km) south of San Angelo, Texas.

Background

Eldorado ( / ˌ ɛ l d ə ˈ r ɑː d oʊ / EL -də- RAH -doh , /- ˈ r eɪ d oʊ / - RAY -doh ) is the only city in and the county seat of Schleicher County , Texas , United States. The population was 1,574 at the 2020 census . Eldorado is located on U.S. Highway 277 , some 21 miles (34 km) north of Sonora and 43 miles (69 km) south of San Angelo , Texas.

History

The location of present-day Eldorado was part of a land grant made by the state of Texas in 1878 to the Houston East and West Texas Railroad Company for the construction of a railroad. The land ended up being unused, and in 1895, W. B. Silliman acquired a stake in the area and established a townsite that he named Eldorado. He attracted residents from the nearby town of Verand by promising them free lots. A post office was opened in the same year and in 1896, the town had a population of 100. Eldorado was chosen as the county seat of Schleicher County in 1901. In 1914, the town had a population of 500. The city voted to incorporate in 1925, but reversed their decision in 1927, only to vote again to incorporate in 1929. In 1930, the Kansas City, Mexico and Orient Railway was built through the town.

Geography

Eldorado is located at 30°51′39″N 100°35′54″W ﻿ / ﻿ 30.86083°N 100.59833°W ﻿ / 30.86083; -100.59833 (30.860746, −100.598329). According to the United States Census Bureau , the city has a total area of 1.4 square miles (3.6 km), all land.

2020 census

As of the 2020 census , Eldorado had a population of 1,574 people living in 631 households. The median age was 39.5 years; 26.4% of the residents under 18 and 19.0% were 65 or older. For every 100 females, there were 98.2 males, and for every 100 females 18 and over, there were 93.6 males. Of the occupied households, 35.2% had children under 18 living in them, 45.3% were married-couple households, 19.5% had a male householder with no spouse or partner present, 27.4% had a female householder with no spouse or partner present, and about 26.0% were made up of individuals; 14.0% had someone living alone who was 65 or older. The city had 765 housing units, of which 17.5% were vacant. The homeowner vacancy rate was 2.3% and the rental vacancy rate was 6.3%. None of the residents lived in urban areas, while 100.0% lived in rural areas.
