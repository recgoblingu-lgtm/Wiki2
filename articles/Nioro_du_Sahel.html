Background

Nioro du Sahel (often referred to as simply Nioro ) is a town and urban commune in the Nioro Region of western Mali , 241 km from the city of Kayes. It is located 443 kilometres or 275 miles (by road) north-west of the Malian capital Bamako . As of 2023, the commune had a population of 109,565. The town has an airstrip at Nioro Airport .

History

Founded in circa 1240 by a Diawambé slave named Beydari Tamboura, Nioro was made the capital of the Bambara kingdom of Kaarta by king Bodian Moriba (r. 1818-1832), replacing Yélimané . The town became an important trading center between Upper Senegal and the Sudan . On April 11th 1855 the Toucouleur conqueror El Hadj Umar Tall invaded Kaarta and seized Nioro, which became his capital. He had a great mosque built in Nioro in 1856, and a large Muslim community subsequently settled in the town. After Umar Tall's death, his son Ahmadu succeeded him as head of the Toucouleur Empire . Ahmadu made his brother Muntaga Tall the Faama of Nioro in 1874. In 1885 Muntaga, encouraged by overtures from the French, rebelled. Ahmadu laid a long and grueling siege of Nioro, but rather than surrender Muntaga committed suicide by blowing up his powder magazine. After this, Ahmadu based himself in Nioro, attempting to build a fully Islamic society. A long series of conflicts and negotiations between the Toucouleur and the French culminated in a campaign against Nioro in late 1890. Ahmadu's army, led by the Wolof king-in-exile Alboury Ndiaye , failed to defend the city against the advancing colonial army under Lieutenant Colonel Louis Archinard in January 1891.
