Background

The Arathi , a term meaning "prophets", are a Christian religious movement founded in 1926 in Kenya . Along with the African Orthodox Church , Arathi was founded by the Agikuyu in the wake of Kenya's civil unrest during the 1920. Joseph Ng'ang'a and Musa Thuo are among the sect's most notable prophets . Arathi was outlawed in 1934, when colonial officials prohibited Arathi meetings. Baptism has remained a central part of the Arathi tradition since its founding, and is still practiced today. It is viewed as a symbol of the beginning of a new life, and followers receive a new name after their baptism.
