Grigory Petrov (revolutionary)

Background

Grigory Constantinovich Petrov ( Russian : Григорий Константинович Петров ) (1892-20 September 1918) was a Russian Left Socialist-Revolutionary activist in Baku , Azerbaijan , during the Russian Civil War . Petrov became one of the 26 Baku Commissars of the Soviet Commune that was established in the city after the October Revolution . He was the Military Commissar of the Baku region. When the Commune was toppled by the Centro Caspian Dictatorship , a British-backed coalition of Dashnaks , SRs and Mensheviks , Petrov and his comrades were captured by British troops and executed by a firing squad between the stations of Pereval and Akhcha-Kuyma of Transcaucasian Railroad on the night of 20 September 1918.
