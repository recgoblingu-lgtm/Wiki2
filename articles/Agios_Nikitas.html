Background

Agios Nikitas ( Greek : Άγιος Νικήτας ) is a village on the island of Lefkada , Greece , part of the municipal unit of Lefkada . It is a small beach resort, situated on the northwestern coast, 4 km northwest of Karya and 10 km southwest of Lefkada city. The village has a church.
