Słupsk Voivodeship

Słupsk Voivodeship is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Słupsk Voivodeship was a unit of administrative division and local government in Poland from 1975 to 1998, previously part of Szczecin Voivodeship (1945–50) and Koszalin Voivodeship (1950–75), superseded by Pomeranian Voivodeship and West Pomeranian Voivodeship. Its capital city was Słupsk.

Background

Słupsk Voivodeship ( Polish : województwo słupskie ) was a unit of administrative division and local government in Poland from 1975 to 1998, previously part of Szczecin Voivodeship (1945–50) and Koszalin Voivodeship (1950–75), superseded (since 1999) by Pomeranian Voivodeship and West Pomeranian Voivodeship ( Sławno County ). Its capital city was Słupsk .
