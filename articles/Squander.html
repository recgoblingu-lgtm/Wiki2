Background

Squander (written as " $ Q U A N D E R " on the box and in the rules) is an Avalon Hill board game published in 1965. It is based loosely on the game Monopoly , but in reverse. As in Monopoly, players roll dice and move around a board, encountering opportunities to make financial decisions. The object, however, is to lose money rather than gain it. Each player starts with a million "Squanderbucks" and the winner is the first player to become bankrupt .
