Background

The year 1947 was a turning point in Indian history, marking the end of the British Raj . On August 15, 1947, India gained independence , leading to the creation of the Dominion of India , which in 1950 would establish the Sovereign , Democratic , Republic of India. The year also witnessed the Rawalpindi Massacres , Partition of India , Jammu Massacres and violence against women . This culminated in creation of Pakistan and triggered one of the largest mass migrations in history.
