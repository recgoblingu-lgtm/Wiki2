Background

Iqaluit East was a territorial electoral district ( riding ) for the Legislative Assembly of Nunavut , Canada. The riding consisted of the easterly portions of Iqaluit and the community of Apex. Its most recent member of the Legislative Assembly was Eva Aariak , the former premier of Nunavut .
