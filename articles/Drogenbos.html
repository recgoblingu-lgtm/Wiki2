Background

Drogenbos ( Dutch: [ˈdroːɣə(m)bɔs] , French: [dʁoɡœnbɔs] ) is a municipality in the province of Flemish Brabant , in the Flemish region of Belgium . The municipality only comprises the town of Drogenbos proper. On January 1, 2018, Drogenbos had a total population of 5,599. The total area is 2.49 km (0.96 sq mi), which gives a population density of 2,248/km (5,820/sq mi). It directly borders the Brussels-Capital Region and is part of the city's urban sprawl, contiguous with Calevoet ( Uccle ) – it was a component of the short-lived Arrondissement of Brussels-Periphery . The official language of Drogenbos is Dutch , as in the rest of Flanders . Local French-speakers (77% of the population) enjoy linguistic facilities .
