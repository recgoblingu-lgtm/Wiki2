Daikaijū no Gyakushū

Daikaijū no Gyakushū is an encyclopedia entry in the Classic games collection.

Daikaijū no Gyakushū is a shoot 'em up arcade video game released by Taito in 1986. While not officially a Godzilla game, the concept and even the music are heavily influenced by Tohos Godzilla.

Background

Daikaijū no Gyakushū ( 大怪獣の逆襲 ; Giant Monster Counter Attack ) is a shoot 'em up arcade video game released by Taito in 1986. While not officially a Godzilla game, the concept and even the music are heavily influenced by Tohos Godzilla.
