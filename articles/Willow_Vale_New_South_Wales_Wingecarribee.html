Background

Willow Vale is a Northern Village of the Southern Highlands of New South Wales , Australia , in Wingecarribee Shire . It is located 2 km north of Mittagong and is often considered part of Braemar along with its neighbour Balaclava . According to the 2016 census , Willow Vale had a population of 717. At the 2021 census , 826 people were living at Willow Vale.
