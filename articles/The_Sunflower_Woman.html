The Sunflower Woman

The Sunflower Woman is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

The Sunflower Woman is a 1918 Hungarian film directed by Michael Curtiz. Based on the play by Yugoslav and Croatian playwright from Dubrovnik, Ivo Vojnović, the film was shot on location in Dubrovnik.

Background

The Sunflower Woman ( Hungarian : A Napraforgós hölgy ) is a 1918 Hungarian film directed by Michael Curtiz . Based on the play by Yugoslav and Croatian playwright from Dubrovnik , Ivo Vojnović , the film was shot on location in Dubrovnik.
