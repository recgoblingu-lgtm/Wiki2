Background

Cannon Ball is a census-designated place (CDP) on the Standing Rock Sioux Reservation and in Sioux County, North Dakota , United States. It is located in the northeastern part of Sioux County, having developed at the confluence of the Cannonball River and Lake Oahe of the Missouri River . The population was 864 as of the 2020 census , and was estimated at 857 in 2025.

Geography

According to the United States Census Bureau , the CDP has an area of 96.669 square miles (250.37 km), of which 88.266 square miles (228.61 km) is land and 8.403 square miles (21.76 km) (8.69%) is water.

Demographics

As of the 2024 American Community Survey , there were 207 estimated households in Cannon Ball with an average of 3.61 persons per household. The CDP has a median household income of $42,031. Approximately 50.7% of the CDP's population lives at or below the poverty line . Cannon Ball has an estimated 39.1% employment rate, with 3.8% of the population holding a bachelor's degree or higher and 86.6% holding a high school diploma. There were 220 housing units at an average density of 2.49 per square mile (1.0/km). The top five reported languages (people were allowed to report up to two languages, thus the figures will generally add to more than 100%) were English (94.2%), Spanish (0.0%), Indo-European (0.3%), Asian and Pacific Islander (0.0%), and Other (5.4%). The median age in the CDP was 29.2 years.

2020 census

As of the 2020 census , there were 864 people, 209 households, and 155 families residing in the CDP. The population density was 9.79 inhabitants per square mile (3.8/km). There were 226 housing units at an average density of 2.56 per square mile (1.0/km). The racial makeup of the CDP was 3.82% White , 0.12% African American , 91.55% Native American , 0.00% Asian , 0.00% Pacific Islander , 0.12% from some other races and 4.40% from two or more races. Hispanic or Latino people of any race were 1.85% of the population.
