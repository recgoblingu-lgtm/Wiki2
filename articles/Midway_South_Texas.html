Background

Midway South is a census-designated place (CDP) in Hidalgo County , Texas , United States. The population was 2,307 at the 2020 United States Census . It is part of the McAllen – Edinburg – Mission Metropolitan Statistical Area .

Geography

Midway South is located at 26°9′36″N 98°1′6″W ﻿ / ﻿ 26.16000°N 98.01833°W ﻿ / 26.16000; -98.01833 (26.159984, -98.018322). According to the United States Census Bureau , the CDP has a total area of 1.2 square miles (3.2 km), all land.

Demographics

Midway South first appeared as a census designated place in the 2000 U.S. census .

2020 census

As of the census of 2000, there were 1,711 people, 419 households, and 372 families residing in the CDP. The population density was 1,376.3 inhabitants per square mile (531.4/km). There were 461 housing units at an average density of 370.8 per square mile (143.2/km).
