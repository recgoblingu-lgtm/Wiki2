Background

Unley Park railway station is located on the Belair line in suburban Adelaide , Australia. It is on the boundary between Hawthorn and Westbourne Park , on the south side of Cross Road, seven kilometres from Adelaide station .

History

The station was opened around the 1910s. It was temporarily closed in 1995 when one set of tracks through the station was converted to standard gauge as part of the One Nation Adelaide-Melbourne line gauge conversion project. The western platform was demolished as part of the project.
