Background

Lyn Erhard , better known under the pen name of Charlotte Chandler , is an American biographer and playwright. Chandler authored biographies of Groucho Marx , Federico Fellini , Billy Wilder , Bette Davis , Marlene Dietrich , Joan Crawford , Ingrid Bergman , Mae West and Alfred Hitchcock . A resident of New York City, Chandler is a member of the board of Film at Lincoln Center .
