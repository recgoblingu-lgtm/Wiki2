Background

Norsjö ( Swedish pronunciation: [ˈnǔːrɧœ] ) is a locality and the seat of Norsjö Municipality in Västerbotten County , Sweden , with 2,051 inhabitants in 2010. It is the birthplace of writer Torgny Lindgren , singer-actor-musician Tommy Körberg , and American author Charlotte Agell , who currently resides in Maine , United States. Norsjö also plays a part in the Stieg Larsson novel The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo .
