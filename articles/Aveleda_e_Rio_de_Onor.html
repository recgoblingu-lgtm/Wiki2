Background

Aveleda e Rio de Onor is a civil parish in the municipality of Bragança , Portugal. It was formed in 2013 by the merging of former parishes Aveleda and Rio de Onor ( Spanish : Rihonor de Castilla ; Leonese : Ruidenor/Rueidenor [rwei.ðeˈno.ɾi] ). The population in 2011 was 272, in an area of 106.35 km².

Language

Portuguese is spoken by everyone. Historically the main language was Rionorese , an Astur-Leonese language, which is almost extinct today.
