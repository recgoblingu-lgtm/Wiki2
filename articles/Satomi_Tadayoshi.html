Background

Satomi Tadayoshi ( 里見 忠義 ; 1594 – July 27, 1622) was a retainer of the Japanese clan of Ōkubo following the Azuchi-Momoyama period of the 17th century. Following the conspiracy of the Ōkubo clan against the authority of the Tokugawa Shogunate , Tadayoshi along with many others under the Ōkubo were dispossessed of their personal holdings.
