Background

Furuset is residential and suburban area, situated in the northeast of Oslo, Norway . It was a separate borough of Oslo up to January 1, 2004, when it became part of the new borough of Alna . Furuset is a suburb in eastern Groruddalen , consisting of both residential blocks and houses. Furuset Church ( Furuset Kirke ) was built during 1980. The church belongs to Ellingsrud Furuset parish and Østre Aker deanery. The church is located at Ulsholtveien 37, just south of Furuset Cultural Park ( Furuset kulturpark ). Furuset Aktivitetspark opened in the neighborhood in 2008.
