Background

The 1964–65 season was Blackpool F.C. ' s 57th season (54th consecutive) in the Football League . They competed in the 22-team Division One , then the top tier of English football , finishing seventeenth. Ray Charnley was the club's top scorer for the seventh consecutive season, with 22 goals (21 in the league and one in the League Cup ).
