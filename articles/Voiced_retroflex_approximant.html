Background

A voiced retroflex approximant is a type of consonant used in some languages . The symbol in the International Phonetic Alphabet that represents this sound is ⟨ ɻ ⟩, a turned lowercase letter r with a rightward hook protruding from the lower right of the letter. The velar bunched approximant found in some varieties of Dutch and American English is nearly indistinguishable from a retroflex approximant in sound, but has a different articulation.
