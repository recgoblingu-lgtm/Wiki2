Chhattisgarhi language

Chhattisgarhi language is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Chhattisgarhi is an Indo-Aryan language spoken by approximately 16.25 million or 1.62 crore people in Chhattisgarh, western Odisha, Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra in India. It is the co-official language of Chhattisgarh alongside Hindi. It is grouped within the Eastern Hindi languages and is counted by the Indian national census as a dialect of Hindi.

Background

Chhattisgarhi ( छत्तीसगढ़ी ) is an Indo-Aryan language spoken by approximately 16.25 million or 1.62 crore people in Chhattisgarh , western Odisha , Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra in India . It is the co-official language of Chhattisgarh alongside Hindi . It is grouped within the Eastern Hindi languages and is counted by the Indian national census as a dialect of Hindi.
