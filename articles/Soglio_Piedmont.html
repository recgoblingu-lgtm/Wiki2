Background

Soglio is a comune (municipality) in the Province of Asti in the Italian region Piedmont , located about 30 kilometres (19 mi) east of Turin and about 14 kilometres (9 mi) northwest of Asti . As of 31 December 2004, it had a population of 129 and an area of 3.5 square kilometres (1.4 mi). Soglio borders the following municipalities: Camerano Casasco , Cortanze , Cortazzone , Montechiaro d'Asti , Piea , and Viale .
