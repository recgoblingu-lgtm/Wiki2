USS Chevalier

USS Chevalier is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Two ships of the United States Navy have borne the name Chevalier, in honor of the Lieutenant Commander Godfrey Chevalier, a pioneer of naval aviation.USS Chevalier (DD-451), was a Fletcher-class destroyer, launched in 1942 and sunk in 1943. USS Chevalier (DD-805), was a Gearing-class destroyer, launched in 1944 and struck in 1975. Chevalier was transferred to South Korea in 1972, and renamed Chung Buk; she was scrapped in 2000.
