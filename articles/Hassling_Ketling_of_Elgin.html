Background

Ketling (Hassling-Ketling of Elgin ) is a fictional character in Henryk Sienkiewicz 's novel Fire in the Steppe , the third volume of his award-winning The Trilogy . A Scotsman, Ketling moved to Poland where he became a Colonel of Artillery in service of the king of Poland John Casimir . Ketling married Krystyna Drohojowska , a former fiancée of his friend, Michał Wołodyjowski . Their marriage resulted in two pregnancies, a son having been born from the first one. Ketling was killed in the Siege of Kamieniec Podolski , when he (together with Wołodyjowski) blew himself up in a gunpowder depot. The historical personality on which the character was loosely based was certain Major Heyking, a mercenary from Courland and the commander of the Kamieniec Fortress during the said war. In 1969 Jerzy Hoffman 's film Ketling is portrayed by Jan Nowicki .
