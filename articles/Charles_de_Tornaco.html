Background

Baron Charles Victor Raymond André Evance de Tornaco ( pronounced [ʃaʁl də toʁnako] ; 7 June 1927 – 18 September 1953) was a racing driver from Belgium . He participated in 4 Formula One World Championship Grands Prix, debuting on 22 June 1952. He scored no championship points. De Tornaco was the co-founder of Ecurie Belgique , which later became Ecurie Francorchamps , and most of his racing career was with this team, driving Ferraris . His father, Baron Raymond de Tornaco (1886-1960), was also a racing driver, competing in endurance events like the inaugural 1923 running of the 24 Hours of Le Mans . In practice for the 1953 Modena Grand Prix , de Tornaco rolled his car and suffered serious head and neck injuries. There were no adequate medical facilities present, and he died on his way to hospital in a private saloon car.
