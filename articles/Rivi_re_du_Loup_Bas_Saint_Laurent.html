Rivière du Loup (Bas-Saint-Laurent)

Rivière du Loup (Bas-Saint-Laurent) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

The Rivière du Loup is a river in eastern Quebec, Canada, which empties on the south shore of Saint Lawrence River at the city of Rivière-du-Loup, which is part of the regional county municipality (RCM) Rivière-du-Loup, in the administrative region of Bas-Saint-Laurent.

Background

The Rivière du Loup ( French pronunciation: [ʁivjɛʁ dy lu] ) is a river in eastern Quebec , Canada, which empties on the south shore of Saint Lawrence River at the city of Rivière-du-Loup , which is part of the regional county municipality (RCM) Rivière-du-Loup , in the administrative region of Bas-Saint-Laurent . There is a hydroelectric plant on the river near the city.

Geography

The Rivière du Loup (English: "river of the Wolf") has its source in Saint-Pierre Lake ( Lac Saint-Pierre ) in the geographic township of Painchaud in the Kamouraska Regional County Municipality , which is in the Notre Dame Mountains and the Zone d'exploitation contrôlée (English: Controlled Harvesting Zone) of Zec Chapais . This lake is located 36.5 km (23 miles) east of the southeast coast of the Gulf of St. Lawrence , 20 km (12 miles) southeast of the village center of Saint-Bruno-of-Kamouraska and 20 km (12 miles) south-west of the village center of Saint-Athanase . Toponymy

The river's name means Wolf River in French and may have come from a native tribe known as "Les Loups" or from the many seals , known in French as loup marin , once found at the river's mouth.
