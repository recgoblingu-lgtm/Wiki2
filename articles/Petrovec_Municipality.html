Background

Petrovec Municipality ( Macedonian : Петровец ) is a municipality in northern North Macedonia , near the capital Skopje . Petrovec is also the name of the village where the municipal seat is found. It is located in the Skopje Statistical Region . Skopje International Airport is located in the Petrovec municipality.

Geography

The municipality borders with Studeničani Municipality and Zelenikovo Municipality to the west, Ilinden Municipality and Kumanovo Municipality to the north, Sveti Nikole Municipality to the east, and Veles Municipality to the south.
