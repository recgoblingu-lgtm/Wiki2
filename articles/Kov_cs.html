Background

Kovács or Kovacs , meaning blacksmith , is one of the most common Hungarian family name . The name is found in Hungary and Hungarian expatriate communities. There are similar names with the Kováts or Kovats , or Kovách spellings. The name means " blacksmith " in Hungarian , and it is a loanword from Slavic languages . The top three most frequent surnames in Hungary are Nagy , Kovács , and Tóth .
