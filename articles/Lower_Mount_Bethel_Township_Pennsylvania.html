Background

Lower Mount Bethel Township is a township in Northampton County, Pennsylvania , United States. The population of Lower Mount Bethel Township was 2,921 at the 2020 census. Lower Mount Bethel Township is part of the Lehigh Valley metropolitan area, which had a population of 861,899 and was the 68th-most populous metropolitan area in the U.S. as of the 2020 census.

Geography

According to the U.S. Census Bureau , the township has a total area of 24.6 square miles (63.6 km), of which 23.8 square miles (61.7 km) is land and 0.8 square miles (2.0 km) (3.10%) is water. It is drained by the Delaware River , which forms its eastern and southern boundary and separates it from New Jersey . Its villages include Gruvertown, Martins Creek , Mount Pleasant, and Riverton.

There were 1,347 housing units at an average density of 55.7 per square mile (21.5/km).
