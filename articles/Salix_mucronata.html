Background

Salix mucronata (commonly called the Cape silver willow or Safsaf willow ) is a tall, graceful, Semi- Deciduous willow tree . It grows along riverbanks in South Africa , and is used for a wide range of traditional medicines. The Cape willow is dioecious (separate male and female trees).
