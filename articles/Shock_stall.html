Background

A shock stall is a stall created when the airflow over an aircraft 's wings is disturbed by shock waves formed when flying at or above the aircraft's drag divergence Mach number . Shock stall may cause control problem during speed transition ( transonic to supersonic ). Thin supercritical wing section and swept-back wing can postpone shock stall to higher speed. A stall is the decrease in lift to a value below the weight, and the associated increase in drag upon the separation of the boundary layer (in this case behind the shock wave ). A shock stall occurs, when the lift coefficient as function of the Mach Number reaches its maximum value.
