Background

Red Rooster is an upcoming 3D role-playing video game , developed and published by Summer Eternal . It is known for being a spiritual alternative to Disco Elysium , with Summer Eternal having former ZA/UM employees who left the company and founded their studio in 2024. The game was first announced in September 2026, with official reveal on October 14 of the same year, with no exact release date stated. Developers have plans launching the game for PC , PlayStation 5 , Xbox Series and Switch 2 .

Overview

Summer Eternal describe Red Rooster as "incendiary, physics-driven RPG combining the best of cerebral, worldbuilding-rich RPGs." The reveal of the game on October 14 was accompanied by a 500-page physical book titled “Anthology Volume One,” showcasing the game’s concept art , design, and world building. The studio stated that the game is "nearing the end of pre-production." Red Rooster contains several gameplay similarities to Disco Elysium, such as "The Coop" similar to the "Thought Cabinet."

Plot

The game takes place in the city of Arta-Tantor , located on the "border archipelago" in a political alternate-history setting. The player controls Redrick Roost - an inmate who was offered the role of an arson investigator and partnered up with an drug-dependent mathematician Dr Astrid Albers to investigate a mysterious arson that seems to connect to his brother, the city’s fire-chief. In exchange for Redrick's help, he is promised a blanket pardon for his crimes and a guaranteed return to the society he has not contacted for over a decade.
