Background

Huoqiu County ( simplified Chinese : 霍邱县 ; traditional Chinese : 霍邱縣 ; pinyin : Huòqiū Xiàn ) is a county in the west of Anhui Province, People's Republic of China , under the jurisdiction of Lu'an City and bordering Henan province to the west. It has a population of 1,640,000 and an area of 3,483 square kilometres (1,345 sq mi). The government of Huoqiu County is located in Chengguan Town. Huoqiu County has jurisdiction over 19 towns and 10 townships.

Transportation

Huoqiu County has one passenger railway station , Huoqiu , on the Fuyang–Lu'an railway .

Economy

Around a third of foie gras produced worldwide, or 5,000 tonnes, originates from the five million Landes geese raised in Huoqiu.
