Pristimantis fetosus

Pristimantis fetosus is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Pristimantis fetosus is a species of frog from the family Strabomantidae. It is endemic to Colombia. Its natural habitats are tropical moist montane forests and rivers. It is threatened by habitat loss.
