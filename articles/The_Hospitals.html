The Hospitals

The Hospitals is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

The Hospitals were an American noise rock band from San Francisco, California, United States, active from 2002 to 2009. They were formed by Adam Stonehouse and Rod Meyer (Guitar) in 2002 in Portland, Oregon, US. The Hospitals have released recordings through Load Records, In the Red, Yakisakana Records, Future Primitive, and Not Not Fun.

Background

The Hospitals were an American noise rock band from San Francisco , California , United States, active from 2002 to 2009. They were formed by Adam Stonehouse (Drums and vocals) and Rod Meyer (Guitar) in 2002 in Portland, Oregon , US. The Hospitals have released recordings through Load Records , In the Red , Yakisakana Records , Future Primitive , and Not Not Fun .
