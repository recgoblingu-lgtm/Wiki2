Background

Así es el tango (English: Therefore it is The Tango ) is a 1937 Argentine romantic drama film musical directed and written by Eduardo Morera , based on a play by Florencio Chiarello . Starring Tita Merello and Tito Lusiardo . Based on tango culture, it is a typical product of the Golden Age of Argentine cinema .

Synopsis

Two deceived women plot revenge against their husbands. A composer is deceived by an adventurer whose girlfriend he falls in love with.
