Background

Loyasse Cemetery ( French : Cimetière de Loyasse ) is a cemetery in the city of Lyon , France . The cemetery is located on the Fourvière hill in the western part of the city, not far away from the Metallic tower of Fourvière and Notre-Dame de Fourvière . It is the 'richer' cemetery of Lyon, when compared with the Cimetière de La Guillotière , with elaborate graves in various architectural styles.
