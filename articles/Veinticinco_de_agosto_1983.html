Background

Veinticinco de agosto, 1983 ( August the 25th, 1983 ) is a chamber opera by Juan María Solare , to a Spanish libretto by Javier Adúriz based on the life of Jorge Luis Borges . Solare composed the opera from 1992 to 1993. The opera is approximately 37.5 minutes in length and consists of one act with fifteen scenes.
