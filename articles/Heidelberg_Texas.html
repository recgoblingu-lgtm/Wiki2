Background

Heidelberg is a census-designated place (CDP) in Hidalgo County , Texas , United States. The population was 1,507 at the 2020 United States Census . It is part of the McAllen – Edinburg – Mission Metropolitan Statistical Area .

Geography

Heidelberg is located at 26°11′15″N 97°52′59″W ﻿ / ﻿ 26.18750°N 97.88306°W ﻿ / 26.18750; -97.88306 (26.187387, -97.883154) about 21 km north of the US/ Mexico border at Progreso . It is a small residential community, grouped around Heidelberg Park. According to the United States Census Bureau , the CDP has a total area of 2.8 square miles (7.3 km), all land.

Demographics

Heidelberg first appeared as a census designated place in the 2000 U.S. census .
