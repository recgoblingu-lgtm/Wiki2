Ministry of National Defence (Portugal)

Ministry of National Defence (Portugal) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

The Ministry of National Defence is a Portuguese government ministry, which is responsible for preparing and executing the national defence policy, within the scope of its powers, as well as ensuring and supervising the administration of the Portuguese Armed Forces.

Background

The Ministry of National Defence ( Portuguese : Ministério da Defesa Nacional or MDN ) is a Portuguese government ministry, which is responsible for preparing and executing the national defence policy, within the scope of its powers, as well as ensuring and supervising the administration of the Portuguese Armed Forces .
