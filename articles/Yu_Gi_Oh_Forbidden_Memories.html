Yu-Gi-Oh! Forbidden Memories

Yu-Gi-Oh! Forbidden Memories is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Yu-Gi-Oh! Forbidden Memories is a 1999 digital collectible card game developed and published by Konami for the PlayStation. It is based on the Kazuki Takahashi manga Yu-Gi-Oh! and operates on a modified version of the Yu-Gi-Oh! Trading Card Game. The single-player campaign follows an ancient Egyptian prince, an ancestor of series protagonist Yugi Mutou, who must recover the Millennium Items and thwart the scheme of the evil high priest Heishin. The game was originally released in Japan in 1999, followed by releases in North America in 2002 and in Europe in 2003. It received mixed reviews upon its Western release; while critics acknowledged its appeal to fans of the franchise, they faulted its high difficulty, steep learning curve, and lack of tutorials. The game was commercially successful, selling over two million units in North America and Europe by 2004.

Gameplay

Yu-Gi-Oh! Forbidden Memories is a digital collectible card game based on the Yu-Gi-Oh! Trading Card Game , with the rules modified and streamlined for increased flexibility. Each player's deck consists of 40 cards, drawing from a total of 722 monster and magic cards. The players field cards on a board divided into two ranks of five slots: the front rank holds monsters, while the rear rank holds spells and traps. A duel ends when one player's life points reach zero or when a player can no longer draw from their deck. Monsters are classified into 20 categories (such as warrior, fairy, aqua, spellcaster, etc.). Each carries values of attack and defense points and belongs to one of several terrain groups (such as forest, mountain, dark, etc.). When a monster occupies an advantageous terrain, it receives a boost in its attack and defense points. In addition, every monster played is assigned a cosmic alignment (Sun, Moon, Jupiter, Neptune, etc.) which remains fixed for that card; a monster with a superior alignment attacking an inferior monster gains 500 attack points, while an inferior monster attacking a superior monster adds 500 defense points to the superior monster. Players begin each turn by drawing into a hand of five and may play one or more cards. Plot

In ancient Egypt, the high priest Heishin discovers a forbidden treasure of ancient sorcerers, which grants a dark power. Meanwhile, the young prince of the Amenhotep Dynasty frequently sneaks from the palace to duel commoners in card games, frustrating his advisor Simon Muran. At a town festival, the prince and his friends Teana and Jono encounter the arrogant priest Seto, who easily defeats Jono before the prince bests Seto in a duel. That night, Heishin and his forces, including Seto, invade the palace using dark sorcery, capturing the pharaoh and queen. Seto confronts the fleeing prince, demanding the location of a "Millennium Item" to spare the royals. The battered Simon arrives, handing the Millennium Puzzle to the prince and urging him to protect it, as it is the key to unlocking an immense power. Cornered by Heishin, the prince challenges him to a duel but loses. As Heishin moves to seize the Puzzle, Simon holds him off, and the prince shatters it. The prince and Simon's souls are sealed inside the Puzzle's labyrinthine realm. Simon explains this was the only way to keep the Puzzle from Heishin; the prince will sleep until someone reassembles it in the future, allowing his return. Development and release

Yu-Gi-Oh! Forbidden Memories was developed and published by Konami , and is based on the Kazuki Takahashi manga Yu-Gi-Oh! . Development was directed by Morikuni Kubo, with Satoshi Shimomura acting as producer. The story's scenario was written by Masaaki Karube. Masafumi Kamio served as the game's art director. The music was composed by Naoko Ishii, Hiroshi Tanabe, and Waichiro Ozaki. The game was released in Japan on December 9, 1999. In North America, it was released simultaneously with the Game Boy Color title Yu-Gi-Oh! Dark Duel Stories on March 19, 2002, becoming the first two video games in the franchise to be released in the territory. The game was released in Europe on March 7, 2003.
