Little Earthquakes (video)

Little Earthquakes (video) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Little Earthquakes is a VHS video released by American singer-songwriter and pianist Tori Amos in 1992, which serves as Amos' first video release. The release contains all four music videos released in conjunction with Amos' debut solo album of the same name interspersed with live performances and interview footage.
