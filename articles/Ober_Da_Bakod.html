Background

Ober Da Bakod ( transl. over the fence ) is a Philippine television sitcom series broadcast by GMA Network . Directed by Ariel Ureta , it stars Janno Gibbs , Leo Martinez and Anjo Yllana . It premiered on September 14, 1992. The series concluded on May 27, 1997.

Adaptations

The series had two film adaptations: Ober da bakod: The Movie (1994) and Ober da Bakod 2 (Da Treasure Adbentyur) (1996).
