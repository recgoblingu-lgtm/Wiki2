Idol Janshi Suchie-Pai

Idol Janshi Suchie-Pai is an encyclopedia entry in the Game franchises collection.

Idol Janshi Suchie-Pai is a series of mahjong video games that have been developed and released by Jaleco on a variety of platforms including arcade, PC, and video game consoles. The first game in the series was on the Super Famicom in 1993 as Bishōjo Janshi Suchie-Pai and was renamed Idol Janshi Suchie-Pai for its subsequent releases. Kenichi Sonoda did the character designs. There is also an anime OVA that is based on the game.

Background

Idol Janshi Suchie-Pai ( アイドル雀士スーチーパイ , Aidoru Janshi Sūchī-Pai ; lit. ' Idol Mahjong Player Suchie-Pai ' ) is a series of mahjong video games that have been developed and released by Jaleco on a variety of platforms including arcade , PC , and video game consoles . The first game in the series was on the Super Famicom in 1993 as Bishōjo Janshi Suchie-Pai and was renamed Idol Janshi Suchie-Pai for its subsequent releases. Kenichi Sonoda did the character designs. There is also an anime OVA that is based on the game.
