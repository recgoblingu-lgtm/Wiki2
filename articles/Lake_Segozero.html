Background

Lake Segozero ( Russian : Сегозеро , Finnish : Seesjärvi ) is a large freshwater lake in the Republic of Karelia , northwestern part of Russia . It is located at 63°18′N 33°45′E ﻿ / ﻿ 63.3°N 33.75°E ﻿ / 63.3; 33.75 . The Segezha is a major outflow from Segozero and it empties to Vygozero . After the hydroelectric power plant was built on Segezha River the surface area of Segozero Lake have risen from 815 km² to 906 km². The lake was dammed in the 1970s.
