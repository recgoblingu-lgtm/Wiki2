Background

San Nicolas , officially the Municipality of San Nicolas ( Tagalog : Bayan ng San Nicolas ), is a municipality in the province of Batangas , Philippines . According to the 2024 census, it has a population of 24,392 people.

Etymology

San Nicolas is derived from Spanish for Saint Nicholas of Tolentino , the town's patron saint .

History

San Nicolas traces its roots to barrio Wawa, which was once part of the municipality of Taal . The barrio also covered areas of what is now Alitagtag , Agoncillo , Lemery , and San Luis , according to old locals. It was first inhabited by Malays from Borneo . It was the site of Taal's second municipal center from its establishment in 1572 to 1754, during the peak of Moro piracy. Taal was designated as the provincial capital of Batangas from 1732 to 1754. The 1754 Taal Volcano eruption, which lasted for months, greatly destroyed the area, making Taal move its municipal center to its present location near Balayan Bay . After which, Wawa was renamed to San Nicolas. Geography

According to the Philippine Statistics Authority , the municipality has a land area of 14.37 square kilometers (5.55 sq mi) constituting 0.46% of the 3,119.75-square-kilometer (1,204.54 sq mi) total area of Batangas. The municipality also occupies the southern half of Taal Volcano , shared by the municipality of Talisay in the northern half.
