Background

Bit-synchronous operation is a type of digital communication in which the data circuit -terminating equipment ( DCE ), data terminal equipment ( DTE ), and transmitting circuits are all operated in bit synchronism with a clock signal . In bit- synchronous operation, clock timing is usually delivered at twice the modulation rate , and one bit is transmitted or received during each clock cycle. Bit-synchronous operation is sometimes erroneously referred to as digital synchronization.
