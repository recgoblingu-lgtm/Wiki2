Background

In Galois theory , a branch of mathematics , the embedding problem is a generalization of the inverse Galois problem . Roughly speaking, it asks whether a given Galois extension can be embedded into a Galois extension in such a way that the restriction map between the corresponding Galois groups is given.

Definition

Given a field K and a finite group H , one may pose the following question (the so called inverse Galois problem ). Is there a Galois extension F/K with Galois group isomorphic to H . The embedding problem is a generalization of this problem: Let L/K be a Galois extension with Galois group G and let f : H → G be an epimorphism. Is there a Galois extension F/K with Galois group H and an embedding α : L → F fixing K under which the restriction map from the Galois group of F/K to the Galois group of L/K coincides with f ? Analogously, an embedding problem for a profinite group F consists of the following data: Two profinite groups H and G and two continuous epimorphisms φ : F → G and f : H → G . The embedding problem is said to be finite if the group H is. A solution (sometimes also called weak solution) of such an embedding problem is a continuous homomorphism γ : F → H such that φ = f γ . If the solution is surjective, it is called a proper solution .
