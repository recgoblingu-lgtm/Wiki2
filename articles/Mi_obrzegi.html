Background

Miłobrzegi [miwɔˈbʐɛɡi] (formerly German Friedensburg ) is a settlement in the administrative district of Gmina Świdwin , within Świdwin County , West Pomeranian Voivodeship , in north-western Poland. It lies approximately 2 km (1 mi) north-west of Świdwin and 88 km (55 mi) north-east of the regional capital Szczecin . For the history of the region, see History of Pomerania .
