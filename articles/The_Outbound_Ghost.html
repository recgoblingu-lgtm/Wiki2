Background

The Outbound Ghost is a 2022 fantasy role-playing video game developed by Conradical Games and published by Digerati. The game was released on Windows and Nintendo Switch , PlayStation 4 , PlayStation 5 , Xbox One , and Xbox Series X and Series S , on November 30, 2022, and received mixed reviews, with Conradical Games openly feuding with Digerati over the quality of the release.

Plot

The game focuses on a ghost who tries to uncover of how the residents of the town of Outbound suddenly died. He also guides other souls into the afterlife.

Gameplay

The game is an RPG. The combat use a point-based, turn-based system, where the player and the enemies will use points to select actions to defeat each other.

Development

The Outbound Ghost was funded through Kickstarter . The game was influenced by the Paper Mario series. It was originally planned as an adventure game, but the additional funds raised through Kickstarter and the partnership with publisher Digerati allowed it to develop into a more ambitious RPG. The developer released video development diaries on YouTube throughout the game's development.
