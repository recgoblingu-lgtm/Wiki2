Aikatsu Stars! The Movie

Aikatsu Stars! The Movie is an encyclopedia entry in the Classic games collection.

Aikatsu Stars! The Movie is a 2016 Japanese animated musical film directed by Shin'ya Watada and written by Yūko Kakihara; the film is based on the anime television series by BN Pictures and Bandai. Produced by BN Pictures and distribuited by Toei Company. its focuses on Yume and the others travel from Four Star Academy to a remote island for looking at the Legendary Dress. The film stars the voices of Miyu Tomita, Ayaka Asai, Megumi Yamaguchi, Natsumi Murakami, Yume Miyamoto, Minami Tsuda, Sumire Morohoshi, Azusa Tadokoro, Ayaka Ōhashi. It was released in Japan on August,13 2016.
