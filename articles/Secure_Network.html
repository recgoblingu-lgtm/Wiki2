Background

Secure Network is a small offensive security and security research company focusing on Information Security based in Milan , Italy . Besides having notability in Italy , it received international exposure with a research project on Bluetooth security (co-sponsored by F-Secure ) codenamed BlueBag, which has been also selected for the Black Hat Briefings conference 2006 in Las Vegas . In 2009, it also organized SEaCURE.IT, the first international technical security conference ever held in Italy. Secure Network also offers internet security compliance consulting to private companies.
