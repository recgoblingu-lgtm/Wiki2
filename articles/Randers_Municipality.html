The municipality covers an area of 748.21 km2 (288.89 sq mi), and has a population of 100,564. Its mayor, since 1 January 2018 is Torben Hansen, a member of the Social Democrats. Background

Randers Municipality ( Danish : Randers Kommune , pronounced [ ˈʁɑnɐs kʰoˈmuːnə ] ) is a municipality ( Danish , kommune ) in Region Midtjylland on the Jutland peninsula in central Denmark . The municipality covers an area of 748.21 km (288.89 sq mi) , and has a population of 100,564 (as of 1 January 2026). Its mayor, since 1 January 2018 is Torben Hansen, a member of the Social Democrats . The main town and the site of its municipal council is the city of Randers . The municipality is part of Business Region Aarhus and of the East Jutland metropolitan area , which had a total population of 1.378 million in 2016.

Overview

The Guden River ( Gudenå ), Denmark's longest river, runs from Randers Fjord in Nørhald municipality to the east, through the city of Randers, and splits in two about 5 km west of the central city. The northern tributary is called Nørreå ("Northern River"), and it continues westward, defining part of the boundary between Århus and Viborg Counties, and terminates at Vedsø ("Lake Ved") near the town of Rindsholm in Viborg municipality. The 160-kilometre long Guden River continues to Tinnet Krat in Vejle County. The city's nearly 3-kilometre long Guden River Park ( Gudenåparken ) stretches along the river's banks in the centre of the town.

History

On 1 January 2007, Randers municipality was, as the result of Kommunalreformen ("The Municipal Reform" of 2007) , merged with the existing Nørhald and Purhus , together with a portion of each of the following municipalities— Langå , Sønderhald , and Mariager — to form an enlarged Randers municipality.
