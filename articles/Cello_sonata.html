Cello sonata

Cello sonata is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

A cello sonata is a piece written in sonata form, often with the instrumentation of a cello taking solo role with piano accompaniment. Some of the earliest cello sonatas were composed in the 18th century by Francesco Geminiani and Antonio Vivaldi; subsequent famous cello sonatas include those by Johannes Brahms, Ludwig van Beethoven, Felix Mendelssohn, Fryderyk Chopin, and Richard Strauss.

Background

A cello sonata is a piece written in sonata form, often with the instrumentation of a cello taking solo role with piano accompaniment. Some of the earliest cello sonatas were composed in the 18th century by Francesco Geminiani and Antonio Vivaldi ; subsequent famous cello sonatas include those by Johannes Brahms , Ludwig van Beethoven , Felix Mendelssohn , Fryderyk Chopin , and Richard Strauss .
