Blažov (Javorina)

Blažov (Javorina) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Background

Blažov ( Hungarian : Balázsvágás ; Rusyn : Блажів ) was a municipality (village) in Slovakia . It was one of the villages that ceased to exist in the course of the creation of the municipality-military area Javorina on their territory in 1953. Former names: Blaziv, Blasenau, Balázsvágás A native of this village was the Greek-Catholic bishop in Mukacheve , Havryil Blazhovskyi (c.1705–1742).
