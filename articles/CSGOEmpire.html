CSGOEmpire is an encyclopedia entry in the Esports titles collection.

Ossi Eemeli Ketola is a Finnish entrepreneur and high-stakes poker player known online as "Monarch." He is the founder of the Counter-Strike 2 skin-gambling platform CSGOEmpire and the casino Duel.com. Ketola gained international prominence in 2024–2025 for participating in some of the highest-stakes heads-up poker matches in the history of the game.
