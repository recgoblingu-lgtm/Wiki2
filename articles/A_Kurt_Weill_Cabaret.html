Background

A Kurt Weill Cabaret was a Broadway and off-Broadway production featuring the music of Kurt Weill . A precursor, The World of Kurt Weill in Song , opened off-Broadway at One Sheridan Square in the West Village on June 6, 1963, starring Will Holt and Martha Schlamme . In 1979, it was revised as A Kurt Weill Cabaret and opened at the Bijou Theater on Broadway, with Alvin Epstein and Martha Schlamme and ran for 72 performances. The Harold Clurman Theatre (now Theatre Five in the Theatre Row Building ) showed it in 1984. A recording of the original production was released by MGM Records (E/SE 4180 OC) in 1963.
