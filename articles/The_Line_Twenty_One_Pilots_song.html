The Line (Twenty One Pilots song)

The Line (Twenty One Pilots song) is an encyclopedia entry in the Esports titles collection.

American musical duo Twenty One Pilots have released eight studio albums, six live albums, one compilation album, 10 extended plays, 35 singles, seven promotional singles, and 57 music videos. The band was formed in 2009 and currently consists of Tyler Joseph and Josh Dun. After two self-released albums, Twenty One Pilots in 2009 and Regional at Best in 2011, they were signed by Fueled by Ramen in 2012, which released their following studio albums, as well as Blurryface Live, a three-LP, tri-gatefold picture disc vinyl, Scaled and Icy and MTV Unplugged.
