Pentyl propanoate

Pentyl propanoate is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Pentyl propanoate is an organic ester formed by the condensation of pentan-1-ol and propanoic acid. It is a colorless liquid with an apple-like odour or as sweet, fruity, apricot, pineapple, tropical, banana. Having a low density, it floats on water.

Background

Pentyl propanoate (also known as amyl propionate ) is an organic ester formed by the condensation of pentan-1-ol and propanoic acid . It is a colorless liquid with an apple-like odour or as sweet, fruity, apricot, pineapple, tropical, banana. Having a low density, it floats on water.
