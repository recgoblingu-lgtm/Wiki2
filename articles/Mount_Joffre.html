Background

Mount Joffre is a mountain located on the Continental Divide , in Peter Lougheed Provincial Park , Alberta , and Elk Lakes and Height of the Rockies Provincial Parks in British Columbia . The mountain was named in 1918 by the Interprovincial Boundary Survey after Marshal Joseph Joffre , commander-in-chief of the French Army during World War I . The normal climbing route ( UIAA class II) is via the north face, which is covered by the Mangin Glacier .
