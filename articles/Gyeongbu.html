Background

The name Gyeongbu refers to the Seoul – Busan corridor in South Korea . It is used as the name of the Gyeongbu railway line and Gyeongbu Expressway , both of which connect Seoul—the South Korean capital and largest city—to Busan—the largest port and second-largest city. The name "Gyeongbu" is formed from the first characters in the names " Gyeongseong " 경성 ( 京 城 ) and "Busan" 부산 ( 釜 山 ).
