HDMS Triton

HDMS Triton is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

At least five ships of the Royal Danish Navy have borne the name HDMS Triton:HDMS Triton (1790) a 30 gun frigate designed by E.W.Stibolt HDMS Triton (1913) HDMS Triton (1915) an Æger-class submarine launched in 1915 and sold for scrapping in 1946. HDMS Triton (F347) a Triton-class corvette launched in 1954 and decommissioned in 1981. HDMS Triton (F358) a Thetis-class frigate launched in 1990.
