Background

The House of Shuvalov ( Russian : Шува́лов ) is the name of a Russian noble family, which was documented since the 16th century. The Shuvalov family rose to distinction during the reign of Empress Elizabeth and was elevated to the rank of count on 5 September 1746.

Residences

The Shuvalov family's residences included three palaces in Saint Petersburg and a manor nearby: Through marriage, the Shuvalovs also acquired property in Courland , including Rundale Palace , which was originally built for Ernst Johann von Biron .
