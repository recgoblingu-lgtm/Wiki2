Wygoda, Gmina Jędrzejów

Wygoda, Gmina Jędrzejów is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Wygoda is a village in the administrative district of Gmina Jędrzejów, within Jędrzejów County, Świętokrzyskie Voivodeship, in south-central Poland. It lies approximately 9 km (6 mi) north-east of Jędrzejów and 28 km (17 mi) south-west of the regional capital Kielce.

Background

Wygoda [vɨˈɡɔda] is a village in the administrative district of Gmina Jędrzejów , within Jędrzejów County , Świętokrzyskie Voivodeship , in south-central Poland. It lies approximately 9 km (6 mi) north-east of Jędrzejów and 28 km (17 mi) south-west of the regional capital Kielce .
