Background

In computing , the KOMPILER was one of the first language compilation and runtime systems for International Business Machines ' IBM 701 , the fastest commercial U.S. computer available in 1955. Information on KOMPILER is listed on page 16 of Volume 2, Number 5 (May 1959) of the Communications of the ACM . Known versions are KOMPILER 2 for IBM 701 and KOMPILER 3 for the IBM 704 . KOMPILER was eventually replaced by a Fortran compiler on the IBM 704.
