Background

Merseburg ( German pronunciation: [ˈmɛʁzəbʊʁk] ) is a town in central Germany in southern Saxony-Anhalt , situated on the river Saale , and approximately 14 km south of Halle (Saale) and 30 km west of Leipzig . It is the capital of the Saalekreis district. It had a diocese founded by Archbishop Adalbert of Magdeburg . The University of Merseburg is located within the town. Merseburg has around 35,000 inhabitants.

Administrative reforms

Venenien was incorporated into Merseburg on 1 January 1949. The parish Kötzschen followed on 1 July 1950. Since 30 May 1994, Meuschau is part of Merseburg. Trebnitz, previously part of Kreypau , followed in 2003. Beuna was annexed on 1 January 2009. Geusa is a part of Merseburg since 1 January 2010.
