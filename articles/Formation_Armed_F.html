Background

Formation Armed F is a 1988 vertically scrolling shooter video game developed and published by Nichibutsu for arcades . It was initially only released in Japan, with a PC Engine port released in 1990 by Pack-In-Video . Hamster Corporation acquired the rights to the game alongside Nichibutsu's intellectual property, releasing the game as part of their Arcade Archives series for the PlayStation 4 in 2016 and Nintendo Switch in 2019 as simply Armed F .

Gameplay

The player controls a spacecraft called the Vowger and shoots enemies, collects power-ups and attempts to defeat bosses to advance levels. The Vowger can be changed to shoot in a multitude of formations and directions.
