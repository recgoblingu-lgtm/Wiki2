Background

Shaw is a village in Berkshire , England. It is located to the north of Newbury , on the River Lambourn near the village of Donnington . It is in the parish of Shaw-cum-Donnington . The parish church of St Mary is early Victorian, dating to 1842, and a Grade II listed building . Shaw mill was a corn mill, now converted to apartments. The miller's house is also Grade II listed.

Shaw House

Shaw House , an Elizabethan country house , was one of the Royalist headquarters during the Second Battle of Newbury and, later, the childhood home of the historian, James Pettit Andrews . Having been a school for many years, it is now a conference centre owned by West Berkshire Council . The building is a Grade I listed building . The park and gardens are Grade II listed.

Transport

From 18 February 2013, Shaw is served by Newbury & District bus services 6 and 6A from Newbury.
