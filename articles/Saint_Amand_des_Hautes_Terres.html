Saint-Amand-des-Hautes-Terres is a former commune in the Eure department in Normandy in northern France. On 1 January 2016, it was merged into the new commune of Amfreville-Saint-Amand.

Background

Saint-Amand-des-Hautes-Terres ( French pronunciation: [sɛ̃.t‿amɑ̃ de ot tɛʁ] ) is a former commune in the Eure department in Normandy in northern France . On 1 January 2016, it was merged into the new commune of Amfreville-Saint-Amand .
