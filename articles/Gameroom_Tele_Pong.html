Background

The Gameroom Tele-Pong (sometimes also called Entex Gameroom Tele-Pong or ENTEX Gameroom Tele-Pong ) is a dedicated first-generation home video game console developed, published and marketed by Entex Industries starting in 1976 . It had a price of US$60. The Gameroom Tele-Pong displays the games in black and white. The score is built in the console. It does have sound. The Gameroom Tele-Pong is similar to the first Japanese video game console, Epoch 's TV Tennis Electrotennis , released a year prior. The console does not contain a central CPU but uses 8 discrete SN74LS00 chips. It is only battery-operated (1.5V "C" cell x 4). There was also a version released in the United Kingdom marketed by Binatone called the TV Game Unit . It had a price of £23.95.
