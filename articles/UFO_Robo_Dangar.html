UFO Robo Dangar

UFO Robo Dangar is an encyclopedia entry in the Classic games collection.

UFO Robo Dangar is a vertically scrolling shooter arcade video game released by Nichibutsu in 1986. It is a spinoff of the Cresta Series, which includes Moon Cresta, Terra Cresta and Sol Cresta. The game is inspired by the anime series UFO Robo Grendizer and Danguard Ace.

Background

UFO Robo Dangar is a vertically scrolling shooter arcade video game released by Nichibutsu in 1986. It is a spinoff of the Cresta Series, which includes Moon Cresta , Terra Cresta and Sol Cresta . The game is inspired by the anime series UFO Robo Grendizer and Danguard Ace .

Reception

Malc of Shmups rated the game ten out of ten, calling it his favourite shoot 'em up . He praised the gameplay for "sheer playability" while stating the graphics are "quite average and functional" and the music has an "arcadey melody over a heavy techno drum beat" that sounds strange yet pleasing at the same time.
