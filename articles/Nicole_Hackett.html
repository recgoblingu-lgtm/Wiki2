Background

Nicole Hackett (born 10 December 1978 in Sydney ) is an athlete from Australia , who competes in triathlon . She won the bronze medal at the 2002 Commonwealth Games however her greatest achievement is her son James born 4 June 2006 Hackett completed at the first Olympic triathlon at the 2000 Summer Olympics . She took ninth place with a total time of 2:03:10.81. Her split times were 19:41.08 for the swim, 1:05:37.10 for the cycling, and 0:37:52.63 for the run.
