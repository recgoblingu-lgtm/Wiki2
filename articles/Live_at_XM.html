Background

Live at XM is an EP by Wheatus released on 15 December 2004 and exclusively available at Apple 's iTunes Store . It features five songs from a live performance that was part of an hour-long Wheatus special, broadcast by XM Satellite Radio . The song 'Lemonade' on the EP was dedicated to Brendan B. Brown's neighbors. The EP was removed from iTunes at an unknown date, and is not otherwise available.
