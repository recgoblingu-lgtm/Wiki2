Background

The bearded eelgoby ( Taenioides jacksoni ), also known as the bearded goby , is a species of goby endemic to South Africa where it inhabits muddy areas of mangrove swamps , estuaries and rivers . This species can reach a length of 12 centimetres (4.7 in) TL .
