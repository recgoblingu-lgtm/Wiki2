Background

Sandals Cay (formerly called Kokomo Island ) is part of the privately owned Sandals Royal Caribbean all-inclusive resort in Montego Bay , Jamaica . It is apparent that Sandals resorts has an almost identical island in their Nassau, Bahamas location called the Royal Bahamian. The original name for the island came from the song " Kokomo ”. It is a very small island (2.5 acres or 1.012 hectares), and according to the Sandals Royal Caribbean website, the only amenities on the island are a Thai restaurant, a swimming pool , a jacuzzi , a bar , and a secluded beach . The island caters to couples on tropical getaways.
