Background

"Dota O Ako" is a song by Filipino rapper Aikee, from his collaborative album with Vanessa , Tayo Ang Bagay (2010). Originally entitled "Dota Vs Gf" , it was written by Aikee and produced by Francis Guevarra. It was released in 2011 through MCA Music alongside an accompanying music video made by RHTV . Lyrically, the song talks about a girlfriend making her boyfriend choose between her or the video game mod Defense of the Ancients (2003). A re-recording of the song featuring Sabrina was released in 2013 by UMG Philippines . The re-recording was nominated for Best R&B Recording at the 26th Awit Awards . In 2021, an answer song entitled "Dota at Ako" , accompanied by a music video, was recorded by the original singers and was produced by advertising agency Gigil and Netflix Philippines to promote the series Dota: Dragon's Blood .

Background

According to online sports magazine Spin.ph , multiplayer online battle arena video games have held a long presence within the Philippines. Since 2003, the amount of computer shops within the Philippines had initially risen due to the popularity of Defense of the Ancients (DotA, 2003), a multiplayer online battle arena mod for the video game Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos (2002) and its expansion, The Frozen Throne (2003). Its popularity was in both formal and informal contexts, ranging from organized tournaments and games among friends. Rapper Aikee released a collaborative album with then- Sugarpop member Vanessa entitled Tayo Ang Bagay (2010). One of the tracks included in the album was entitled "Dota Vs Gf", which was met with opposition but Aikee insisted for it to be included as the songwriter and self-proclaimed gamer.

Release, composition, and music video

"Dota Vs Gf", later renamed as "Dota O Ako", was released in 2011 through MCA Music (now UMG Philippines) as a single. As both Aikee and Vanessa were managed by RHTV , they appealed to the management for them to help produce a music video for the song. Aikee later stated in a 2021 interview with IGN Southeast Asia that they had no budget at the time and just used a green screen for the video's visuals in order to make it relatable among students as they were also students at the time. In 2013, Aikee re-recorded the song with singer Sabrina as Vanessa had a busy schedule at the time and was under contract with another company. "Dota O Ako" is three minutes and 54 seconds long. It was written by Aikee and produced by Francis Guevarra. Lyrically, the song is about an argument between a girlfriend and a boyfriend, wherein the girlfriend makes her boyfriend choose between her or his gaming addiction to DotA.

Reception and commercial performance

Ash Mahinay of IGN Southeast Asia hailed "Dota O Ako" as the "Best Dota [ sic ] Song Ever". In another commendation of the song, Mary Graces Tomines of Pop! named it a "playful anthem" and stated that the lyrics and its beat made it "a staple at school events and gatherings." Both Mahinay and Tomines named the song as part of jejemon subculture. In 2013, the re-recording with Sabrina was nominated for Best R&B Recording at the 26th Awit Awards . In 2015, "Dota O Ako" was certified gold in the Philippines by PARI .
