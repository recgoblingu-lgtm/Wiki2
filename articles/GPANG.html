GPANG

GPANG is an encyclopedia entry in the Platforms collection.

GPANG was a 3D mobile game service introduced in 2004 by the Korean provider KTF. This service allowed subscribers to access a downloadable game portal and play advanced 3D mobile games, including MMOs. KTF's GPANG competed with SK Telecom's own 3D mobile game service called GXG. On June 1 2009, GPANG ceased to exist.

Background

GPANG ( Korean : 지팡) was a 3D mobile game service introduced in 2004 by the Korean provider KTF . This service allowed subscribers to access a downloadable game portal and play advanced 3D mobile games, including MMOs. KTF's GPANG competed with SK Telecom 's own 3D mobile game service called GXG . On June 1 2009, GPANG ceased to exist.
