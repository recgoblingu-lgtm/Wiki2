Background

The Erramala ( Telugu : ఎఱ్ఱమల ) are a range of low hills in southern India , in Kurnool district and Nandyal District of Andhra Pradesh state. The Erramala hills lie on the Deccan plateau , running east–west and dividing the basin of the Penna River to the south from the basin of the Krishna River to the north. The hill range may be extended along the Penna River basin in Kadapa district .
