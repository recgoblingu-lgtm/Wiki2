Background

Allianshallen is an indoor arena in Uppsala , Sweden , used mostly for floorball . It is the home arena of FBC Uppsala and of the women's team of IK Sirius IBK , while the men's team use Fyrishov as their home arena. Allianshallen has been used as an indoor arena since January 1999, in a building which was formerly owned by publisher Almqvist & Wiksell .
