Background

Set on a tropical island, the game features mechanics similar to other farming simulators, such as Stardew Valley . It was a major success on Kickstarter , reaching its initial funding goal within 36 hours. Gameplay

The player controls a customizable protagonist who’s been disenchanted with city life and moves to a small farming community on a tropical island. Upon arrival, the player is given an overrun farm and is tasked with restoring it and planting crops. Harvested crops can be sold for profit, with opportunities to increase income through various methods. In Coral Island , players can engage in a wide range of farming activities, including animal husbandry, adopting pets, fishing, crafting, cooking, combat with monsters, exploring caves and mines for rare resources. Players can also interact and form relationships with the town’s non-player characters (NPCs) . As players' relationships with characters develop, they can unlock additional dialogue and storylines. Marriage is available, including options for same-sex partnerships, and players have the opportunity to conceive a child with their chosen partner. The game includes an offering system tied to four altars located throughout the island. Each altar requiring six offerings based on crops, animal products, foraged items, mineable resources, and insects and fish. The offerings can be submitted in any order, and players can receive a reward upon completing the entire bundle. Plot

The player returns to Starlet Town, their hometown on Coral Island, to work on a farm there and meets Conner, the town's mayor. The town is in disarray due to giant dark roots appearing on the island and an oil spill, and may be forced to cooperate with an oil company called Pufferfish Corporation, which secretly plans to convert the town into an oil facility. The player sets out to revive the island and prevent the corporation from taking control of the island, all while befriending the townsfolk, providing new products for the town's supermarket, and increasing the museum's popularity. During their mission, the player meets the Chieftain, the leader of a group of monkey-like creatures called giants, who grants them the power of nature and has them help revive his four fellow giants, who have been turned to stone in the mines. To do so, they must enter the mines and activate the runes at the bottom of each shaft. The player also helps the island's harvest goddess and meets the merfolk who live near the island. Initially wary of humans, the merfolk come to trust the player after their coral tree is revived, which in turn fully restores the island and eliminates the roots and oil spill. Development

Stairway Games, an Indonesian developer, chose to set the game in a similar setting and that influenced its cast of characters, crops, architecture, and biomes. The character art was heavily influenced by Disney animation . Coral Island was partially funded through a successful Kickstarter campaign that ran from February 1 to March 3, 2021, raising $1,639,368 from 36,374 backers, making it one of the most successful campaigns of 2021. The campaign surpassed its initial funding goal of $70,000 within 36 hours and unlocked additional content through stretch goals. After entering early access in October 2022, Humble Games released Coral Island for Windows, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S on November 14, 2023. Shortly after its release, an imposter account on social media targeted fans with NFT scams. In response, the developers issued a warning and clarified that the game does not incorporate NFTs or blockchain technology. A planned Nintendo Switch port has yet to materialize, and the effective closure of Humble Games in July 2024 interfered with updates for existing platforms and cast doubt on the Switch release.
