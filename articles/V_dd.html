Background

Väddö is an island in the Baltic Sea in Roslagen district, Sweden . It is situated in Norrtälje Municipality . Together with the adjoined Björkö it is considered the seventh largest island of Sweden. Väddö is separated from the mainland by the artificial Väddö Canal , first dug in the 16th century .
