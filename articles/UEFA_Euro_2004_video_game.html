UEFA Euro 2004 (video game)

UEFA Euro 2004 (video game) is an encyclopedia entry in the Esports titles collection.

UEFA Euro 2004 is the official licensed video game of UEFA Euro 2004 football tournament hosted in Portugal. The game was developed by EA Canada and published by EA Sports. It was released for Microsoft Windows, Xbox and PlayStation 2.

Background

UEFA Euro 2004 is the official licensed video game of UEFA Euro 2004 football tournament hosted in Portugal . The game was developed by EA Canada and published by EA Sports . It was released for Microsoft Windows , Xbox and PlayStation 2 . It is the second game from EA Sports based on the quadrennial UEFA European Championship and features all 51 national teams from the respective qualifying round . The commentary is provided by John Motson and Ally McCoist .

Gameplay

UEFA Euro 2004 uses a tweaked and improved version of the graphics engine from FIFA 2004 and introduces many new gameplay features and game modes to both the UEFA Euro and FIFA series .

Development

Electronic Arts made the game's development public on February 6, 2004, when it announced that it had signed an agreement with UEFA to develop, publish and distribute the official game based on UEFA Euro 2004 championship that would begin on June 12 of that same year in Portugal. The game was developed by EA Canada, the studio behind the development of the FIFA series of video games.

Soundtrack

For the soundtrack, the developers specifically chose rock -based tracks due to the genre's big resurgence in Europe at the time.
