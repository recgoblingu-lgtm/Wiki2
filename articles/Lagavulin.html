Background

Lagavulin ( Scottish Gaelic : Lag a' Mhuilinn , "hollow of the mill") is a small village approximately 3 miles (5 kilometres) outside Port Ellen on the Isle of Islay , Scotland . The village is within the parish of Kildalton, and is situated on the A846 road . It is best known for being the home of Lagavulin single malt whisky .
