Background

Höllviken is a locality situated in Vellinge Municipality , Skåne County , Sweden with 15,678 inhabitants in 2019. It is located close to both Malmö and the Øresund Bridge which connects Sweden and Denmark . The town is a popular summer vacation spot for northern Europeans, who are attracted to its sandy beaches and the historical sites which date back to the time of the Vikings . The peninsula on which the town is situated is unique because it is surrounded by two different seas: Öresund and the Baltic Sea . Prior to the 1960s, the peninsula remained mainly populated by wealthy businessmen from the Malmö region during the summers. Recently, however, it has begun to grow as a commuter town .
