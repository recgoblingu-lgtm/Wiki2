Background

Guðrún is a feminine given name. The English and German version of the name is Gudrun . It is one of the most frequently given female names in Iceland . In 2004, it was ranked first before Anna and Sigríður . According to Icelandic custom , people are generally referred to by first and middle names and patronyms are used if disambiguation is required. The name is earliest attested in a runestone as kuþrun . In Old Norse , Goðrún was an alternative version. The Faroese equivalent is Guðrun and the mainland Scandinavian version is Gudrun . The Old Norse name is composed of the elements guð or goð , meaning "god"; and rūn , meaning " rune ", "secret lore". The Scandinavian Gudrun was revived in the last half of the 19th century.
