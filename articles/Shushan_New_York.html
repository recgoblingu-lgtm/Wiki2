Background

Shushan is a hamlet in the town of Salem in Washington County , New York , United States. It is in the eastern region of the state, located four miles west of the Vermont border. Its ZIP code is 12873. Among Shushan's attractions is the Shushan Bridge , constructed in 1858. The hamlet is named after Susa or Shush which is an ancient city in Iran.
