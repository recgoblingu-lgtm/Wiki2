Background

Prodromos Kathiniotis ( Greek : Πρόδρομος Καθηνιώτης ; born 30 July 1969) is a Greek singer and reality-TV celebrity distinguished as a spontaneous, straightforward, manly and folksy personality. His fame is largely attributed to the Greek version of Big Brother . His first album, "The first game" has elements of traditional Greek music. He appeared as a panel member in a daily TV show called H Ellada Paizei ("Greece Plays" - Alter Channel ) hosted by Marietta Chrousala .
