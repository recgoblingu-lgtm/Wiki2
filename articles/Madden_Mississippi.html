Madden, Mississippi

Madden, Mississippi is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Madden is an unincorporated community in Leake County, Mississippi. It had a population of 74 in 2006. Madden is the home of Leake Academy, a private nonprofit Christian school grades K4 through 12. It was established in January 1970.

Background

Madden is an unincorporated community in Leake County , Mississippi . It had a population of 74 in 2006. Madden is the home of Leake Academy, a private nonprofit Christian school grades K4 through 12. It was established in January 1970.

Madden NFL 07

Video game manufacturer EA Sports released Madden NFL 07 in Madden, because it is the only community in the United States to have the same name as football personality John Madden , for whom the game was named. Each resident received a free Xbox 360 gaming console and a free copy of Madden NFL 07 . NFL stars Jerry Rice , Warren Moon , and Marshall Faulk attended the release ceremony.
