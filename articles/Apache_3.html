Apache 3

Apache 3 is an encyclopedia entry in the Classic games collection.

Apache 3 is a 3D scrolling shoot 'em up arcade video game released by Tatsumi in 1988. Players control a yellow AH-64 Apache helicopter with weapons and shoot everything in the air and on the ground.

Background

Apache 3 is a 3D scrolling shoot 'em up arcade video game released by Tatsumi (and Data East in North America) in 1988. Players control a yellow AH-64 Apache helicopter with weapons and shoot everything in the air and on the ground.
