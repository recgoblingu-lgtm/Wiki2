Günter Rexrodt

Günter Rexrodt is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Günter Rexrodt was a German politician of the Free Democratic Party (FDP) who served as Federal Minister for Economic Affairs in the fourth and fifth governments of Chancellor Helmut Kohl from 1993 to 1998. He lived in Berlin.

Background

Günter Rexrodt ( German pronunciation: [ˈɡʏntɐ ˈʁɛksʁoːt] ; 12 September 1941 – 19 August 2004) was a German politician of the Free Democratic Party (FDP) who served as Federal Minister for Economic Affairs in the fourth and fifth governments of Chancellor Helmut Kohl from 1993 to 1998. He lived in Berlin .

Early life and education

After the Abitur in 1960 in Arnstadt , Thuringia and an extra year in West Berlin , Rexrodt graduated with a Diplom in business studies from the Free University Berlin where he also received his doctorate ("Dr. rer. pol") in 1971.

Career in the public sector

From 1968 to 1979 Rexroth worked for the chamber of commerce in Berlin. From 1979 to 1982 he worked as a ressort manager at "Office for Economy of the Federal State of Berlin". In 1980, Rexrodt became a member of the FDP. From 1983 to 1987 – under the leadership of Walter Rasch – as well as from 1989 to 1994 – under Carola von Braun – he served as the alternate chair of the party in Berlin. From 1982 to 1985 Rexrodt served as the State Secretary in the State Administration for Economic Affairs under Elmar Pieroth . From 1985 to 1989 he was the State Minister ( Senator ) for Economic Affairs of the federal state of Berlin, which was led by Governing Mayor Eberhard Diepgen .

Career in the private sector

From April 1989 Rexrodt worked for Citibank in New York City and from January 1990 to August 1991 he was a member of the board of directors at Citibank's Germany business, based in Frankfurt. Already in September 1991, Rexrodt moved to the Treuhand in Germany, where he was a member of the board of directors until January 1993. Reportedly, Chancellor Kohl personally phoned John S. Reed , chairman of Citibank in New York, to ask that Rexrodt be let out of his contract to assume a seat on the board of the Treuhandanstalt. During his time at the Treuhandanstalt, he was in charge of privatizing Eastern Germany's textiles and agricultural sectors. He also disposed of the property of the former Socialist Unity Party of Germany , including publishers and real estate interests.
