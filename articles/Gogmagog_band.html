Background

Gogmagog were an English hard rock and heavy metal supergroup based in Chipping Barnet assembled in 1985 by record producer Jonathan King . The band's lineup featured former Iron Maiden drummer Clive Burr and vocalist Paul Di'Anno , former Gillan (and future Iron Maiden) guitarist Janick Gers , former Whitesnake bassist Neil Murray , and former Def Leppard guitarist Pete Willis . Together for only a short time, Gogmagog originally released a three-song E. P. on the small independent Food For Thought label in 1985 entitled I Will Be There . The title track was composed by longtime Kiss collaborator Russ Ballard and originally released on his 1981 solo album Into the Fire , with the other two songs being written by producer King ("It's Illegal, It's Immoral, It's Unhealthy, but It's Fun" and "Living in a Fucking Time Warp"). Ballard had wrote several huge hit songs including Frida 's " I Know There's Something Going On ", Three Dog Night 's " Liar ", Ace Frehley 's " New York Groove ", Rainbow 's " Since You Been Gone " and Santana 's " Winning ". The band members did not provide any original material. Originally, King attempted to put together a supergroup revolving around Whitesnake vocalist David Coverdale , bassist John Entwistle of The Who , and drummer Cozy Powell , all of whom were keen on the project.
