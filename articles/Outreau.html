Outreau

Outreau is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Outreau is a commune in the Pas-de-Calais department in the Hauts-de-France region of France west of Boulogne. The river Liane forms the eastern border of the commune with Boulogne.

Background

Outreau ( French pronunciation: [utʁo] ; Picard : Outriauwe; West Flemish : Wabingen ) is a commune in the Pas-de-Calais department in the Hauts-de-France region of France west of Boulogne . The river Liane forms the eastern border of the commune with Boulogne.
