Background

Nicolaas Peter Vanos (April 13, 1963 – August 16, 1987) was an American professional basketball player for the Phoenix Suns of the National Basketball Association (NBA). The San Mateo, California native was selected 32nd by the Suns in the 1985 NBA draft after playing at Hillsdale High School and Santa Clara University . He played only two seasons in the NBA, both of them for the Suns at center , ending with his death in a plane crash in 1987 at age 24. Vanos and his girlfriend Carolyn Cohen both died as passengers on Northwest Airlines Flight 255 , which crashed just after takeoff from the Detroit Metropolitan Airport on August 16, 1987. The two were on their way back to Phoenix after a visit to Cohen's parents in Michigan . The Sunday evening crash killed all but one of the 155 persons aboard, and two motorists on the ground.
