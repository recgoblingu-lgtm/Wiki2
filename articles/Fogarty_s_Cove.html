Background

Fogarty's Cove is a 1977 folk music album by Stan Rogers . It was his first album, released by Barnswallow Records, which was then purchased by Stan Rogers and made into Fogarty's Cove Records. The CD was one of several Stan Rogers albums reissued in 2011 by Borealis Records . The reissued CDs featured completely new cover artwork.
