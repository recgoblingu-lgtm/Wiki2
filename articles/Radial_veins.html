Radial veins

Radial veins is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

In anatomy, the radial veins are paired veins that accompany the radial artery through the back of the hand and the lateral aspect of the forearm. They join the ulnar veins to form the brachial veins.

Background

In anatomy , the radial veins are paired veins that accompany the radial artery through the back of the hand and the lateral aspect of the forearm . They join the ulnar veins to form the brachial veins . They follow the same course as the radial artery .
