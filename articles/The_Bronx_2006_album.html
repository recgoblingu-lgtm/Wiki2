Background

The Bronx is The Bronx 's second studio album and the first recording by the band on a major label , Island Def Jam . Released on July 18, 2006, it is the band's second eponymous album and has a change in musical style. It was leaked online on April 26, 2006, almost two months before it was released on vinyl LP. The album was named best album of the year by Rock Sound magazine and was in Spin magazine's list of the top 40 albums of the year at number 36.
