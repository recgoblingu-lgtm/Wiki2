Background

Finley is a city in Steele County , North Dakota, United States. It is the county seat of Steele County. The population was 401 at the 2020 census , and was estimated to be 391 in 2024. Finley was founded in 1897.

History

Finley was founded in 1897. In 1911, a train wreck in Finley caused by a broken rail killed six and injured 13. In 1919, the county seat was transferred to Finley from Sherbrooke, North Dakota .

Geography

According to the United States Census Bureau , the city has a total area of 3.47 square miles (8.99 km), all land.

2020 census

As of the 2020 census , there were 401 people, 188 households, and 108 families residing in the city.
