Background

The 1976 Australian Grand Prix was a motor race held at the Sandown International Motor Racing Circuit in Victoria , Australia on 12 September 1976. It was open to racing cars complying with either Australian Formula 1 or Australian Formula 2 . The race was the forty first Australian Grand Prix and doubled as Round 1 of the 1976 Australian Drivers' Championship . John Goss won the race driving a Matich A53 Repco-Holden , and in doing so became the only driver to win both of Australia's highest profile motor races, the Australian Grand Prix and the Bathurst 1000 . Goss won the 47 lap race by just half a second from Australian international Vern Schuppan who was driving an Elfin MR8 Chevrolet entered by Ansett Team Elfin . Finishing third, over a lap behind, was John Leffler driving a Lola T400 Chevrolet .
