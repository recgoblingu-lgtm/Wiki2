Flotsam (video game)

Flotsam (video game) is an encyclopedia entry in the Indie games collection.

Flotsam is a survival city-building video game developed and published by Pajama Llama Games, which released in early access on September 26, 2019. It was previously published by Kongregate, until the developers self-published it in 2020. The game left early access and be fully released on December 5, 2025.

Background

Flotsam is a survival city-building video game developed and published by Pajama Llama Games, which released in early access on September 26, 2019. It was previously published by Kongregate , until the developers self-published it in 2020. The game left early access and be fully released on December 5, 2025.

Gameplay

Flotsam is a townbuilding survival game. The player starts with a group of stranded survivors in a flooded world and guides these survivors through the world while recycling all the garbage that is found in the ocean.

Development

Flotsam is developed by Ghent -based indie studio Pajama Llama Games. The game released in early access on Steam, Kartridge, GoG and Humble Bundle September 26, 2019.
