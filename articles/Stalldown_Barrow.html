Stalldown Barrow

Stalldown Barrow is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Stalldown Barrow, sometimes called Staldon, is a megalithic site in Devon, about 5 kilometres (3 mi) from Harford. It consists of a long stone row. It is fairly close to the stone circle on Stall Moor.

Background
