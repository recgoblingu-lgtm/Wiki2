Pliskin

Pliskin is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Pliskin is a Jewish locational surname, which originally meant a person from the villages of Pliski, Belarus or Pliszki, Poland. Notable people with the surname include:Joseph Pliskin, Israeli health researcher Zelig Pliskin, American rabbi and writer Iroquois Pliskin, fictional character in the Metal Gear series
