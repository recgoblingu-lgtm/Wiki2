January 1

January 1 is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

January 1 is the first day of the calendar year in the Gregorian Calendar; 364 days remain until the end of the year. This day is also known as New Year's Day since the day marks the beginning of the year.

Background

January 1 is the first day of the calendar year in the Gregorian Calendar ; 364 days remain until the end of the year (365 in leap years ). This day is also known as New Year's Day since the day marks the beginning of the year.
