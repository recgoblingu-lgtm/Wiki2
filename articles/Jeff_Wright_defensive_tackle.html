Jeff Wright (defensive tackle)

Jeff Wright (defensive tackle) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Jeffrey Dee Wright is an American former professional football player who was a nose tackle for seven seasons with the Buffalo Bills of the National Football League (NFL). The Bills selected him 213th overall in the eighth round of the 1988 NFL draft. He became the Bills starting nose tackle in his third season. He played in four Super Bowls for the Bills, all losing efforts. Wright played college football first at Coffeyville Community College in Kansas, and then at Central Missouri State University, now known as the University of Central Missouri, where he played for the Mules.

Background

Jeffrey Dee Wright (born June 13, 1963) is an American former professional football player who was a nose tackle for seven seasons with the Buffalo Bills of the National Football League (NFL). The Bills selected him 213th overall in the eighth round of the 1988 NFL draft . He became the Bills starting nose tackle in his third season. He played in four Super Bowls for the Bills, all losing efforts. Wright played college football first at Coffeyville Community College in Kansas , and then at Central Missouri State University, now known as the University of Central Missouri , where he played for the Mules .
