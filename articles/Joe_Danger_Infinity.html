Background

Joe Danger Infinity is a 2014 endless runner game developed and published by the British indie studio Hello Games . The game was released for iOS on January 9, 2014, and was met with a positive reception.

Release

Infinity was intended as an upgrade to Joe Danger , but due to the excessive amount of added features , Infinity had to be made into a separate game. Hello Games announced Infinity in December 2013. The game was released for iOS on January 9, 2014.

Reception

On Metacritic , Infinity received "generally favorable" reviews. Other critics received the game positively.
