Background

Chin Chun Hock (c. 1844–1927; Chinese : 陳程學 ; Jyutping : can4 cing4 hok6 , also sometimes transcribed as Chin Ching-hock ) was the first Chinese man to settle in Seattle, Washington . He arrived in 1860 and was employed as a domestic worker . By 1868, he had founded a general merchandising store, The Wa Chong Co. ( Chinese : 華昌 ; Jyutping : waa4 coeng1 , Chinese Prosperity ), at the foot of Mill Street. He owned the Eastern Hotel which housed the first Asian workers in Seattle.
