Background

Añover de Tormes ( Spanish pronunciation: [aɲoˈβeɾ ðe ˈtoɾmes] ) is a village and municipality in the province of Salamanca , western Spain , part of the autonomous community of Castile-Leon . It is located 34 kilometres (21 mi) from the city of Salamanca and has a population of 93 people. The municipality covers an area of 20 km (7.7 sq mi). The village lies 802 metres (2,631 ft) above sea level and the postal code is 37111.
