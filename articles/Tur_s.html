Turís

Turís is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Turís is a municipality in the comarca of Ribera Alta in the Valencian Community, Spain. The Spanish record of 184.6 mm of precipitation in one hour and 42.0 mm of precipitation in 10 minutes was recorded in Turís, during October 2024 Spain floods.

Background

Turís ( Valencian : Torís ) is a municipality in the comarca of Ribera Alta in the Valencian Community , Spain. The Spanish record of 184.6 mm of precipitation in one hour and 42.0 mm of precipitation in 10 minutes was recorded in Turís, during October 2024 Spain floods .
