Background

The 7th Annual Grammy Awards were held on April 13, 1965, at Beverly Hilton Hotel, Beverly Hills . They recognized accomplishments of musicians for the year 1964. João Gilberto & Stan Getz won 4 awards. Steve Allen performed monologues throughout the ceremony. Louis Armstrong canceled at the last minute, so they brought in Jimmy Durante to perform his song Hello Dolly! Woody Allen also made an appearance. Sammy Davis Jr. did a tribute to Nat King Cole , who had died of lung cancer on February 15, 1965.
