Sea of Sorrow (Silksong expansion)

Sea of Sorrow (Silksong expansion) is an encyclopedia entry in the Indie games collection.

Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea of Sorrow is an upcoming 2026 free expansion for the 2025 Metroidvania game Hollow Knight: Silksong, developed and published by Australian independent developer Team Cherry. It was announced in December 2025 as Silksong's first expansion, for a 2026 release.

Background

Hollow Knight: Silksong - Sea of Sorrow is an upcoming 2026 free expansion for the 2025 Metroidvania game Hollow Knight: Silksong , developed and published by Australian independent developer Team Cherry. It was announced in December 2025 as Silksong ' s first expansion, for a 2026 release. Sea of Sorrow has been described by Team Cherry as a "giant expansion", and it is nautical -themed, with new areas, bosses , and tools promised. Based on aspects of the trailer and announcement, it has been speculated to feature content initially cut from Silksong by video game journalists and the Silksong community. The expansion's release has been heavily anticipated, akin to Silksong pre-release . As of 14 November 2026, it has no exact release date.

Background

Hollow Knight: Silksong was released in September 2025, over six years after its announcement. Shortly before its release, Team Cherry co-founders and co-directors Ari Gibson and William Pellen were interviewed by Bloomberg journalist Jason Schreier , revealing plans to add new content for Silksong after its release. These plans were reiterated in a follow-up interview with Schreier in November, in which Pellen also promised new information "soon". The same month, Silksong composer Christopher Larkin told Bandcamp in an interview that he was "still doing some stuff for Team Cherry".

Gameplay and plot

Team Cherry has described Sea of Sorrow as a "giant expansion", in which protagonist Hornet will "voyage across and beneath a salt-stricken ocean brimming with mystery and menace". The developers promise "new areas, bosses, tools, and more". The expansion is nautical -themed.

Development and release

On 15 December 2025, Team Cherry announced Sea of Sorrow in a blog post , alongside an animated teaser trailer . Team Cherry confirmed that Sea of Sorrow would be released sometime in 2026, with no exact date given. It was said to release at no additional cost, and serve as Silksong ' s first expansion. Team Cherry then took a few weeks off that month to prepare for 2026. In March 2026, Team Cherry released a patch for Silksong , which they described in a blog post as the last "significant" update for Silksong before Sea of Sorrow ' s release. In May, Fangamer announced a physical release for Silksong . The product's store listing mentioned that " Sea of Sorrow DLC is not included on game media but will be available to download for free". The physical edition released that November, which also included an accompanying video game manual , in which Sea of Sorrow is briefly overviewed as a "content pack" and is stated to be "coming soon".
