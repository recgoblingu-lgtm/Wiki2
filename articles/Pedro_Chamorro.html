Background

The name Chamorro may be both a patronym and a matronym . In Spanish speaking countries, the patronym is usually followed by the matronym to more precisely identify an individual (See Iberian naming customs ). In English speaking countries , the matronym is frequently omitted, resulting in ambiguity. Omission of a given second or middle name further obfuscates an individual's identity.
