Provincial Integration Party Three is a political party in Cartago, Costa Rica. The party was founded and recognized in 2003. Party president is Isabel Cristina Arias Calderón, and the party secretary José Abel Bonilla Castillo.

Background

Provincial Integration Party Three ( Spanish : Partido Integración Provincial Tres ) is a political party in Cartago, Costa Rica . The party was founded and recognized in 2003. Party president is Isabel Cristina Arias Calderón, and the party secretary José Abel Bonilla Castillo.
