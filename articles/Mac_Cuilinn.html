Background

MacCuillinn or Mac Cuillann is an Irish surname . The name is the patronymic form of a personal name derived from cuileann (translation from Irish Gaelic : holly ). The name has become Anglicised as MacCullen & McCullen , and MacQuillan & McQuillan . The name appears in Irish mythology . The family are thought to have been Celtic Kings between the 5th and 12th century. Saint Macculind (or Macallan, MacCuilinn, Macculin Dus, Maculinus; died c. 497) was an early Irish saint who was abbot or bishop of Lusk.
