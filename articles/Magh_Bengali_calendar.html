Magh (Bengali calendar)

Magh (Bengali calendar) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Magh is the tenth month in the Bengali calendar. This is the last month of the two months that make up the winter season. This month corresponds with January and February in the Gregorian calendar.

Background

Magh ( Bengali : মাঘ ) is the tenth month in the Bengali calendar . This is the last month of the two months that make up the winter season. This month corresponds with January and February in the Gregorian calendar.
