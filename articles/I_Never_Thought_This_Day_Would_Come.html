Background

I Never Thought This Day Would Come is the third album by Duke Special , which was released in Ireland by the label Universal Music Ireland on 17 October 2008, and released on soft release in the UK on 20 October 2008. The album was reviewed by The Irish Times , and a free preview of the album is available from "The Ticket" , The Irish Times' weekly entertainment supplement.
