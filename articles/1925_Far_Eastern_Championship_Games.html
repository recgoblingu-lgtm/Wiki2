Background

The 1925 Far Eastern Championship Games was the seventh edition of the regional multi-sport event , contested between China, Japan and the Philippines, and was held from 17 to 22 May in Manila , the Philippines . A total of eight sports were contested over the course of the five-day event. The Philippines are the general champions. China won the football tournament for a sixth consecutive time.
