Background

Hoback is a census-designated place (CDP) in Teton County , Wyoming , United States. The population was 1,176 at the 2010 census . It is part of the Jackson , WY– ID Micropolitan Statistical Area . The town is named for John Hoback, a mountain man who guided the Astor party through the area in 1811.

Geography

Hoback is located at 43°18′57″N 110°46′20″W ﻿ / ﻿ 43.31583°N 110.77222°W ﻿ / 43.31583; -110.77222 (43.315714, -110.772129). According to the United States Census Bureau , the CDP has a total area of 44.35 square miles (114.9 km), of which 43.46 square miles (112.5 km) is land and 0.9 square mile (2.32 km) (2.0%) is water.

Education and Public Services

Public education in the community of Hoback is provided by Teton County School District #1 . In 2023 Hoback area residents created the Hoback Junction Water and Sewer District. This was in response to dangerously high levels of nitrate in the wells in the area. In 2024 the District raised funds sufficient to build a community water system -- including the placing of hydrants for fire suppression. The system is anticipated to come on line in 2027.
