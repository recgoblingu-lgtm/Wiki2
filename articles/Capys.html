Capys

Capys is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

In Roman and Greek mythology, Capys was a name attributed to three individuals:Capys, king of Dardania. Capys, the Trojan who warned not to bring the Trojan horse into the city. Capys, mythological king of Alba Longa and descendant of Aeneas. Said to have reigned from 963 to 935 BC.
