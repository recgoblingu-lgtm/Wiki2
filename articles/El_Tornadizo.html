Background

El Tornadizo is a village and municipality in the province of Salamanca , western Spain , part of the autonomous community of Castile-Leon . It is located 61 kilometres (38 mi) from the provincial capital city of Salamanca and has a population of 88 people.

Geography

The municipality lies 887 metres (2,910 ft) above sea level and covers an area of 11 km (4.2 sq mi). The postal code is 37765.
