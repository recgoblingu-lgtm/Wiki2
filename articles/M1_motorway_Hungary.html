M1 motorway (Hungary)

M1 motorway (Hungary) is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

The M1 motorway is a toll motorway in northwestern Hungary, connecting Budapest to Győr and Vienna. The first section of the motorway opened in the 1970s, reaching the Austrian border at Hegyeshalom in 1996. It follows the route of the old Route 1 one-lane highway.

Background

The M1 motorway ( Hungarian : M1-es autópálya ) is a toll motorway in northwestern Hungary , connecting Budapest to Győr and Vienna . The first section of the motorway opened in the 1970s, reaching the Austrian border at Hegyeshalom in 1996. It follows the route of the old Route 1 one-lane highway.
