Background

Seward is a town in Schoharie County , New York , United States. The population was 1,583 at the 2020 census . The town is named after senator, governor, and secretary of state William H. Seward . The Town of Seward is in the northwestern part of the county and is northeast of Oneonta .

History

The first settlement took place circa 1754 near Seward village. During the American Revolution , much of the town was destroyed due to conflict between loyalist and revolutionary factions. The town was formed in 1840 from part of the Town of Sharon .

Geography

According to the United States Census Bureau , the town has a total area of 36.5 square miles (95 km), of which 36.4 square miles (94 km) is land and 0.1 square miles (0.26 km) (0.19%) is water. The western town line is the border of Otsego County . New York State Route 10 is a north–south highway. New York State Route 165 is an east–west highway, with its eastern terminus at NY-10 at Janesville. New York State Route 145 is a north–south highway in the northeastern part of Seward.
