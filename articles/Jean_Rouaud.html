Background

Jean Rouaud ( French pronunciation: [ʒɑ̃ ʁwo] ; born 13 December 1952) is a French author, who was born in Campbon , Loire-Atlantique . In 1990 his novel Fields of Glory (French: Les Champs d'honneur ) won the Prix Goncourt . First believed to be the first book in a trilogy, Fields of Glory turned out to be the first book in a series of five books on the family history of the author.
