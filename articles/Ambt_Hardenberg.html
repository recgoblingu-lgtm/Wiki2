Background

Ambt Hardenberg is a former municipality in the Dutch province of Overijssel . It consisted of the countryside surrounding the city of Hardenberg , which was a separate municipality. Ambt Hardenberg existed from 1818 to 1941, when it became a part of Hardenberg.
