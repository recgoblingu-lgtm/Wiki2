Monster Rancher Hop-A-Bout

Monster Rancher Hop-A-Bout is an encyclopedia entry in the Classic games collection.

Monster Rancher Hop-A-Bout is a 2000 platform video game developed and published by Tecmo for the PlayStation. It is a spin-off of the Monster Rancher series. It was released in North America on December 10, 2000 and in Japan on March 22, 2001. An arcade version was released in 2001. It will be released as part of Hamster Corporation's Console Archives series for the Nintendo Switch 2 and PlayStation 5.

Background

Monster Rancher Hop-A-Bout is a 2000 platform video game developed and published by Tecmo for the PlayStation . It is a spin-off of the Monster Rancher series. It was released in North America on December 10, 2000 and in Japan on March 22, 2001. An arcade version was released in 2001. It will be released as part of Hamster Corporation 's Console Archives series for the Nintendo Switch 2 and PlayStation 5 .

Gameplay

The player control monsters who hop along linear levels on a pogo stick, reaching the finish line while avoiding holes. Tiles affect the player's speed, hit points, direction or jumps. The game ends when the player falls into the void or loses all hit points.

Reception

David Smith of IGN gave the game a 3/10 score, criticizing its severe lack of content and simple gameplay in comparison to its price tag.
