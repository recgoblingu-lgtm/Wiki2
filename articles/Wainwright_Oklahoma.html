Background

Wainwright is a town in Muskogee County , Oklahoma , United States. It was named for a local merchant, William Henry Wainwright. The population was 93 at the 2020 census , down from 165 in 2010 .

Geography

Wainwright is in western Muskogee County, 17 miles (27 km) southwest of Muskogee , the county seat , and 12 miles (19 km) north of Checotah . According to the U.S. Census Bureau , the town has a total area of 0.3 square miles (0.78 km), all land. The town is drained by Anderson Creek, a southeast-flowing tributary of Dirty Creek, which runs east to the Arkansas River southeast of Webbers Falls .
