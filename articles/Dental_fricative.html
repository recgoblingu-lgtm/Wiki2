Dental fricative

Dental fricative is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

The dental fricative or interdental fricative is a fricative consonant pronounced with the tip of the tongue pressing under the teeth. There are several types :Voiced dental fricative - as in the English this,. Voiceless dental fricative - as in the English thin,. Dental ejective fricative
