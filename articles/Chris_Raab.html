Background

Christian Joseph Raab (born May 21, 1980), better known by his stage name Raab Himself , is an American television personality and stunt performer known as a former member of the CKY crew featured in the MTV series Viva La Bam and Jackass .

Biography

Raab was born in Willow Grove, Pennsylvania , and grew up in West Chester, Pennsylvania , where he attended West Chester East High School . The nickname "Raab Himself" was bestowed upon him by fellow CKY crew member Chris Aspite. Raab graduated from Shippensburg University of Pennsylvania with a bachelor's degree in communications and journalism. Raab has battled drug addiction and alcoholism, but said in 2019 that he was approaching a decade of sobriety. In 2018, he launched his own podcast , titled Bathroom Break Podcast . Raab also launched his own video production company named Green Gate Entertainment. Raab and his wife have a child together.
