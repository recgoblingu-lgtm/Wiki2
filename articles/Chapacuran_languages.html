Background

The Chapacuran languages are a nearly extinct Native American language family of South America . Almost all Chapacuran languages are extinct, and the four that are extant are moribund , with the exception of Wariʼ . They are spoken in Rondônia in the southern Amazon Basin of Brazil and in northern Bolivia . According to Kaufman (1990, 1994), the Chapacuran family could be related to the extinct Guamo language . His suggestions have not been accepted as valid by others.
