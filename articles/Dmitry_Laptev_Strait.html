Dmitry Laptev Strait

Dmitry Laptev Strait is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

Laptev Strait is a 60 km-wide strait in Russia. It separates Great Lyakhovsky Island of the Lyakhovsky Islands from the mainland, and connects the Laptev Sea in the west with the East Siberian Sea in the east. It is named after Russian explorer Dmitry Laptev.

Background

Laptev Strait ( Russian : Пролив Дмитрия Лаптева ; Yakut : Миитэрэй Лаптев силбэһиитэ , romanized: Mîterey Laptev silbehîte ) is a 60 km-wide strait in Russia . It separates Great Lyakhovsky Island of the Lyakhovsky Islands from the mainland, and connects the Laptev Sea in the west with the East Siberian Sea in the east. It is named after Russian explorer Dmitry Laptev .
