The Singer of My City is an encyclopedia entry in the Wikimedia discovery collection.

El Cantar de mi ciudad is a 1930 Argentine film directed and written by José A. Ferreyra. The film has historical significance as it was the first ever sound film produced in Argentina. The film was released on 3 October 1930.

Background

El Cantar de mi ciudad ( English language : The Singer of My City ) is a 1930 Argentine film directed and written by José A. Ferreyra . The film has historical significance as it was the first ever sound film produced in Argentina. The film was released on 3 October 1930.
