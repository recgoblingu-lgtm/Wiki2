Background

Nederwetten en Eckart was a municipality in the Dutch province of North Brabant . It included the villages of Nederwetten and Eckart , the latter of which no longer exists; it is now a part of Eindhoven . The municipality existed until 1821. In that year, Nederwetten became part of the municipality of Nuenen, Gerwen en Nederwetten , and Eckart merged with Woensel .
