Background

Saskatoon Silver Springs was a provincial electoral district for the Legislative Assembly of Saskatchewan , Canada. The district included the neighbourhoods of Silverspring , Forest Grove , Evergreen , Willowgrove , Erindale , and Arbor Creek . This constituency was created by the Representation Act, 2002 (Saskatchewan) out of portions of the Saskatoon Meewasin and Saskatoon Sutherland ridings. It was dissolved by the Representation Act, 2013 (Saskatchewan) into Saskatoon Silverspring-Sutherland and Saskatoon Willowgrove .
