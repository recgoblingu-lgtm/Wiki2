DoorDash

DoorDash is an American technology company that operates an online food ordering and delivery platform, connecting customers with local restaurants, grocery stores, and retailers.

It is one of the largest food delivery services in the United States and has expanded internationally.

Background

DoorDash was founded in 2013 in Palo Alto, California by Tony Xu, Andy Fang, Stanley Tang, and Evan Moore.

The company started as a small project to help local restaurants offer delivery and quickly grew into a major platform.

How It Works

Customers place orders through the DoorDash app or website. Independent contractors called Dashers pick up the food and deliver it.

The system includes live tracking, payments, and delivery coordination.

Services

  • Food delivery
  • Grocery delivery
  • Convenience store delivery
  • DashPass subscription

Impact

DoorDash has influenced the gig economy by creating flexible work opportunities and expanding access to local businesses.

Criticism

The company has faced criticism regarding driver pay, fees for restaurants, and tipping policies.
