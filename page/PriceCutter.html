Price Cutter

Price Cutter is a regional grocery store chain in the United States that offers a wide selection of food products, household goods, and everyday essentials at competitive prices.

The brand is primarily known in the Midwest, especially in Missouri, where it operates multiple locations.

Background

Price Cutter stores are operated by a regional grocery company focused on providing affordable products to local communities.

The chain has grown by serving suburban and smaller city areas with convenient store locations.

Stores and Products

Price Cutter locations typically offer:

  • Fresh produce
  • Meat and seafood
  • Dairy and frozen foods
  • Bakery items
  • Household supplies

Many stores also include deli sections and ready-to-eat food options.

Regional Presence

The chain operates mainly in the Midwestern United States, with a strong presence in Missouri and surrounding areas.

It focuses on serving local communities with accessible grocery options.

Impact

Price Cutter plays an important role in local economies by providing jobs and access to affordable groceries.

It competes with larger national chains while maintaining a regional focus.
