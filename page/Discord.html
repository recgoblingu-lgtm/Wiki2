Discord

Discord is a communication platform that allows users to chat through text, voice, and video. It is widely used by gaming communities, online groups, and organizations.

The platform is known for its server-based structure, where users can create or join communities focused on specific topics.

Background

Discord was launched in 2015 by Jason Citron and Stan Vishnevskiy. It was originally designed to improve communication for gamers but later expanded to serve many types of communities.

The platform quickly gained popularity due to its ease of use and reliable voice chat system.

Features

  • Text channels for messaging
  • Voice channels for live communication
  • Video calls and screen sharing
  • Custom servers and roles
  • Bots and automation tools

Servers and Communities

Discord is built around servers, which act as individual communities. Each server can have multiple channels, roles, and permissions.

Servers are used for gaming groups, schools, businesses, and online communities.

Impact

Discord has become one of the most popular communication platforms worldwide, especially among younger users and online communities.

It has influenced how people collaborate, socialize, and build communities online.

Monetization

Discord offers a subscription service called Nitro, which provides additional features such as enhanced customization and higher upload limits.
