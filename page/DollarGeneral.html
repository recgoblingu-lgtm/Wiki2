Dollar General

Dollar General is an American chain of discount variety stores that sells a wide range of household items, groceries, and everyday essentials at low prices.

The company is known for operating thousands of small stores, primarily in rural and suburban areas across the United States.

Background

Dollar General was founded in 1939 by J.L. Turner and Cal Turner in Kentucky, United States. The business originally started as a wholesale company before transitioning into retail stores.

The company adopted the “dollar store” concept, focusing on affordable pricing and convenient locations.

Stores and Products

Dollar General stores typically carry:

  • Food and snacks
  • Cleaning supplies
  • Health and beauty products
  • Clothing and seasonal items
  • Basic household goods

The stores are designed to be small and easy to access, often located in areas with limited retail options.

Expansion

Dollar General has expanded rapidly, operating thousands of locations across the United States. It is one of the largest discount retailers in the country.

The company continues to open new stores and expand its product offerings, including fresh food and private-label brands.

Impact

Dollar General plays a significant role in providing affordable goods to communities, especially in rural areas where larger stores may not be available.

It has become a major part of the discount retail industry.

Criticism

The company has faced criticism regarding labor practices, store conditions, and its impact on local small businesses.
