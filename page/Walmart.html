Walmart

Walmart is an American multinational retail corporation that operates a chain of hypermarkets, discount department stores, and grocery stores.

It is one of the largest companies in the world by revenue and one of the biggest employers globally.

Background

Walmart was founded in 1962 by Sam Walton in Rogers, Arkansas, United States. The company focused on offering low prices and a wide selection of goods.

It expanded rapidly across the United States and later internationally, becoming a dominant force in retail.

Stores and Products

Walmart stores typically offer:

  • Groceries and food items
  • Electronics and appliances
  • Clothing and accessories
  • Home goods and furniture
  • Pharmacy and health products

Many locations are large supercenters that combine general merchandise with full grocery sections.

Expansion

Walmart operates thousands of stores worldwide, including in North America, South America, and Asia.

The company has also invested heavily in e-commerce and online grocery services.

Impact

Walmart has had a major influence on the retail industry by emphasizing low prices, large-scale operations, and supply chain efficiency.

It plays a significant role in both local and global economies.

Criticism

Walmart has faced criticism regarding labor practices, wages, environmental impact, and its effects on small businesses.
