Wiki2 Creators

Wiki2 Creators are contributors who help expand and improve the Wiki2 encyclopedia by creating and maintaining articles across a wide range of topics.

As Wiki2 continues to grow, creators play a key role in building a large, organized archive of information. Contributions can include writing new articles, improving existing pages, and helping structure content across the site.

Becoming a Creator

Access to create and edit content on Wiki2 is managed through the community Discord server. This ensures that contributions remain organized, accurate, and consistent with the goals of the project.

Users interested in becoming a creator can request access by joining the Discord and opening a ticket. After review, approved users are granted permission to contribute.

👉 Join the Wiki2 Discord

Role of Creators

  • Write and publish new encyclopedia articles
  • Expand and refine existing content
  • Organize articles into appropriate categories
  • Maintain clarity, accuracy, and readability

Guidelines

  • Content should remain factual and informative
  • Articles should follow a consistent structure
  • Low-effort or joke pages may be removed
  • Respect other contributors and their work

Overview

Wiki2 creators are essential to the development of the platform. By contributing, users help build a continuously expanding, structured encyclopedia designed to grow over time.
